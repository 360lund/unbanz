Have a UK or EU trademark and someone else has the Instagram username?

A registered trademark is one of the strongest supports for a username claim, but only when the account is using the name in a way that infringes it.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A UKIPO or EUIPO registered trademark can support a claim to an Instagram username when the account holding it uses the name in a way that infringes your mark, such as selling similar goods or impersonating the brand. Instagram looks at the registration, the classes, and how the account uses the name.

Registered mark

UKIPO or EUIPO registration number.

Matching classes

Goods or services must overlap.

How the name is used

Infringement or impersonation.

What to prepare

  • The registration number and certificate.
  • The classes the mark covers.
  • Screenshots of the account and how it uses the name.
  • Proof you use the brand: website, products, invoices.

When it may not help

  • The account is inactive but not infringing.
  • The mark is in unrelated classes.
  • The name is a common word.

What to send

  • The exact @username you want.
  • Your current Instagram profile, so the handle can go onto it.
  • Why the name is yours: brand, business name, registered trademark, or the name you used before.
  • What you have already tried, if anything.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
