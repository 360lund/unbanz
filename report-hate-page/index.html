Do this first

  1. Screenshot everything with dates before it gets deleted.
  2. Block the account and limit comments and tags on yours.
  3. Report the account as bullying or harassment.
  4. Report posts that share your address, number or private photos as a privacy violation.
  5. If you’re in danger, contact local police first.

Special situations

  • Under 18: Meta treats bullying of minors more seriously. Say so in the report.
  • Intimate images: use Meta’s non-consensual intimate image report and StopNCII.org.
  • Threats: report to police as well as Meta.

What we won’t do

We won’t report someone just for criticising you or posting an opinion. The account has to be breaking Meta’s rules.

How Unbanz helps

We organise the evidence and file reports through Meta’s official process, then follow up. See harassment account removal for the wider service.
