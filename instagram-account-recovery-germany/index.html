Official steps that come first

  1. If a disabled or suspended notice appears, tap it and request a review. Go through every step, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password was changed, open instagram.com/hacked on a phone or computer you have used before.
  3. Search your inbox and spam for an Instagram email about the change. Its undo link only works for a limited time.
  4. Check that your German SIM works, since codes go to that number.
  5. Send one complete appeal and wait for the reply to the email on the account.

Checks that matter in Germany

  • Your +49 number: a German mobile written 0151 1234567 is entered as +49 151 1234567, without the zero. Telekom, Vodafone, O2 and other providers issue a replacement SIM after you identify yourself.
  • Police reports online: each federal state runs an Onlinewache where you can file a report about fraud, threats or identity misuse. In an emergency call 110. Tell your bank straight away if money moved.
  • Consumer advice: the Verbraucherzentrale consumer advice centres give free guidance on phishing and account takeovers.
  • Phishing bait: “your account violates copyright”, fake DHL or Amazon messages and links from hijacked friends are common. Never sign in through a link in a message.

EU appeal rights

Instagram must give users in the EU an internal complaint route under the Digital Services Act, and tell them about out-of-court dispute bodies. In Germany, the Bundesnetzagentur acts as the coordinator for the Act. Appeals Centre Europe is one certified body that accepts some content and account decisions from EU users. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on account behaviour. Use Instagram’s own review first.

Good to know.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Germany?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

Where do I report a hacked Instagram account in Germany?+

To Instagram first, through instagram.com/hacked. If there was fraud, threats or stolen money, file a report on your state’s Onlinewache or at a police station and tell your bank at once.

Can I take an Instagram or Facebook decision to an independent body?+

If you live in the EU, the Digital Services Act lets you use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe for some decisions about content or an account. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app appeal.
