Thinking of complaining to the ICO about your Meta account?

The ICO deals with how organisations handle personal data. It is not an appeal body for bans.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if Meta did not answer a subject access request properly, mishandled your personal data, or did not respond to an objection. Raise the issue with Meta first and wait for a reply. The ICO can look at data handling, but it cannot reinstate your account.

Keep records

Dates and copies.

Know the limits

It does not overturn bans.

When it fits

  • Meta did not reply to a subject access request within the deadline.
  • The reply left out data you expected.
  • You were refused access to your data without a clear reason.

How to prepare

  1. Collect your request, Meta’s reply and the dates.
  2. Write a short timeline.
  3. Submit on ico.org.uk through the data protection complaint form.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
