Lost the email or phone on your Facebook in the UK or EU?

An old work email, a closed mobile contract, a number from another country. Here is what still works when the codes cannot reach you.

Updated 31 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If you are in the UK or EU and Facebook sends codes to an email or number you no longer have, start from the login screen on a device you have used before and choose the option that says you no longer have access. Facebook may ask for ID. A GDPR data request does not reopen the account.

Steps

  1. Try logging in from a phone or laptop you have used with the account before, on your usual home connection.
  2. On the login screen, choose the option for when you no longer have access to the email or number.
  3. If the number was on a contract you ended, ask the carrier whether it can be restored to a new SIM.
  4. If it was a work email, ask the old employer whether the mailbox can be reopened briefly.
  5. Once you are back in, add a personal email you control and save your recovery codes.

Data rights and their limits

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask Meta for a copy of the personal data it holds. That can confirm which email and number are on the account. It does not give you back access. If the account was hacked, report it to the police; in England, Wales and Northern Ireland that means Action Fraud.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Can I email Facebook to change the number?+

No. There is no support address for this. Use the login screen options or the ID check Facebook offers.

Does it help to try many times?+

No. Repeated attempts can lock the flow for a while. Try once a day from the same device.

What if a hacker changed the email?+

Facebook usually emails the old address when the email changes, with a link to undo it. Look for that message, then use the Facebook hacked-account page from a device you used before.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
