Page problems agencies see

  • Page unpublished after a policy decision.
  • Page restricted from posting or running ads.
  • Admin removed or locked out, with the Page still online.
  • A Page taken over after a profile was hacked.
  • A duplicate or fan Page that confuses customers.

Routes by problem

ProblemTypical route
Unpublished for a policy reasonAppeal from Page Quality or Support Inbox
Admin lost accessUse another admin, or the Page access recovery tools
Page taken overThe hacked-account tools, then the Page recovery path
Imitation PageThe impersonation report

Before you contact the partner

  • Screenshot Page Quality and any notices.
  • List all admins and editors and what role each has.
  • Note the date of the last normal post and anything unusual just before.
  • Pause any scheduled posts or ads that rely on the Page.

After a decision

Whatever the outcome, set the Page up again with at least two full admins, and make sure the business portfolio owns the Page. See the prevention checklist.

What is the first thing to check when a Page is unpublished?+

Open Page Quality or the Support Inbox, screenshot the notice and note who the admins are.

Can Unbanz recover a Page when no admin can log in?+

It depends on the situation. Send what the client sees, and Unbanz will tell you honestly which route applies.

Will the Page return?+

No result can be promised. Meta decides every appeal.

What if the client has two admins?+

Mention it. Another admin can sometimes handle part of the problem while the appeal runs.

No. Unbanz works for the agency only.
