Details that matter in the Czech Republic

These are the points that differ for clients based in the Czech Republic.

  • Notice language: The review options are the same in Czech and English. Screenshot the notice as it appears.
  • Phone number: Czech numbers have nine digits and no leading zero, so a mobile is +420 601 234 567.
  • Business details: Have the company name, website and registration number ready if the platform asks for proof the business is real.
  • If fraud or blackmail is involved: The Czech Police (Policie ČR) take reports of fraud, threats and identity misuse. Messages posing as a bank, a parcel service or a classifieds buyer, with a link to a fake login, are a common way in. That runs alongside the platform appeal.
  • Platform rules: Under the Digital Services Act, the Czech Telecommunication Office (ČTÚ) supervises platforms in the country. It does not decide an individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe accepts some account decisions from EU users, but not hacked accounts, and Instagram’s own review comes first.
  • Client data: The Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ) is the Czech data protection authority. Send us only what the appeal needs.
  • Advertising rules: The Rada pro reklamu is the Czech advertising self-regulatory body. Meta’s own ad policies still apply on top.
