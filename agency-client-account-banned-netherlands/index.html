Agency in the Netherlands? A client account got banned.

Clients look to you first. Here is how to sort out who acts, and what to collect today.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

When a client account is banned, the person who owns it, or an admin, has to appeal from their own login. As an agency in the Netherlands, collect the notice screenshot, check which asset is restricted in Business Support Home, and keep your own admin profile clean. You can refer the client to us under your brand. Meta makes the final decision.

Which asset

Profile, Page, ad account or Portfolio.

Who appeals

The owner or an admin.

White-label

We work behind your brand.

Steps for the Netherlands agencies

  1. Ask the client for a screenshot of the notice and the date.
  2. In Business Support Home, check whether the restriction is on the client’s profile, their Page, the ad account or the Portfolio.
  3. Do not request reviews from several admin profiles at once.
  4. If your own profile got restricted while managing the client, appeal that first.
  5. Agree with the client who writes the appeal.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, with the date.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone who says they have inside access.
