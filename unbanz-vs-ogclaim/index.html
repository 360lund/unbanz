Usernames on Instagram are first-come, first-served and cannot be reserved. Nobody can reserve one for you, but Unbanz can work on a taken handle through the route that fits it.

Before you start

  • Write down the exact handle, with any dots or underscores.
  • Screenshot the account holding it, with the date.
  • Have your own profile link ready.
  • If you rely on a trademark, have the registration number and office.
  • Never share your password or login codes with anyone.

If you are in the EU

Under the EU Digital Services Act, Instagram has to explain moderation decisions and offer an internal complaint route. If Instagram removes or restricts an account in a username dispute, you can also use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe. This does not create a right to someone else’s username.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Send the handle you want and your Instagram profile link to team@unbanz.com.
  2. We tell you straight whether there is a legit route: an inactive account, a trademark or brand report, an impersonation report or Instagram’s own processes.

  4. We prepare and file everything through Instagram’s own tools and keep you posted. Instagram makes the final call, so we can’t promise timing or an outcome. If the claim fails, our refund policy gives you 100% back.

Staying safe

We never ask for passwords or codes. We don’t hack accounts, buy stolen handles or file false reports. If someone asks you to log in through their link or wants to sell you an account, walk away.
