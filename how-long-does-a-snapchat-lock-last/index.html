What each message usually means

  • Locked for a third-party app or plugin. A timed lock. The message or your email says when it ends.
  • Temporarily locked for another reason. Snapchat usually says wait around 48 hours, then log in again.
  • Permanently locked. Can’t be unlocked yourself. You’d need an appeal.
  • Device banned. The phone is blocked, not just the account.

How to unlock a temporary lock

  1. Wait until the time is up.
  2. Delete any third-party Snapchat apps first.
  3. Go to Snapchat’s accounts page and tap Unlock.
  4. Change your password and turn on two-step verification.

More: Snapchat account locked.

What resets the clock

Trying again with the same third-party app, logging in on lots of devices, or using VPN switching can extend things or turn a temporary lock permanent.
