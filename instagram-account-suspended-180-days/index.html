How to appeal properly

  1. Open the app and log in. You’ll see the suspension screen.
  2. Tap “Disagree with decision”.
  3. Confirm your phone or email and do any selfie or ID check it asks for.
  4. Keep any explanation short and factual.
  5. Screenshot the confirmation and don’t submit five times.

The full walk-through is in our suspended account appeal guide.

What happens after you appeal

Some people hear back within a day or two, others wait weeks. You may get a message that the account is restored, or that the decision stays. If it’s a no, see Instagram appeal denied.

Mistakes that make it worse

  • Making a new account while the old one is suspended. It can be linked and banned too.
  • Giving your login to someone in your DMs who promises to fix it.
  • Letting the deadline slide because you assume it’ll resolve itself.

When to get help

If the account matters (a business, creator income, years of photos) and you’re not sure what triggered it, get help before you submit. Unbanz prepares the appeal and handles it through Meta’s official process. See Instagram unbans and reviews.
