Ad account restricted because of a payment issue?

Meta restricts ad accounts when it cannot collect payment or does not trust a payment method. This page is for advertisers who see a payment-related notice and cannot run ads.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A payment restriction usually means a failed charge, an unpaid balance or a card Meta does not trust. Settle what you owe, check the payment method in Billing, then request a review in Account Quality. If the button is missing or the review is denied, Unbanz can prepare the appeal.

Settle the balance

An unpaid amount blocks most reviews. Open Billing in Ads Manager and clear the outstanding charge first.

Check the method

Expired cards, bank declines, a wrong billing address or a card used on many accounts can all trigger the notice.

Then ask for review

Once billing is clean, request the review in Account Quality with a short, honest note.

What a payment restriction can look like

  • Ads stop and Ads Manager shows a payment failed or payment method needs attention message.
  • A spending limit appears or drops, and you cannot raise it.
  • The ad account shows as restricted or disabled with a billing reason.
  • The Request review button is missing until the balance is paid.

Things to check in Billing

  1. Open Billing and payments for the ad account and read every notice.
  2. Look at the payment activity for failed or pending charges and pay the outstanding amount.
  3. Make sure the card, its expiry date and the billing address are correct and the name matches the business.
  4. Ask your bank whether it declined or flagged the Meta charges, since banks sometimes block them.
  5. Remove old cards you no longer use, and add one clean method rather than several.

What makes it harder

A card that was used on an ad account Meta restricted before, a charge that was disputed with the bank, or a card shared across many unrelated ad accounts can turn a billing problem into a wider restriction. In those situations the payment fix alone is not enough, and the appeal needs to explain the history.

How Unbanz helps

We go through the notice and your billing history, tell you what needs to be settled first, and then prepare the appeal and supporting details for Meta’s official review. We never take card numbers or logins. For the wider picture see fixing a declined Meta ad payment and ad accounts disabled for payment or unusual activity.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.
  • A screenshot of the billing page showing the amount due and the payment activity.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Why was my ad account restricted for payment reasons?+

Common reasons are a failed charge, an unpaid balance, a card Meta does not trust, or a payment method tied to an account that was restricted before.

Do I need to pay before I request a review?+

Usually yes. An outstanding balance blocks most reviews, so clear it first and keep proof of payment.

Will a new card fix the restriction?+

Not on its own. A new card helps only if the old one was the problem. If Meta linked the account to another one, the appeal still has to explain that.

Can Unbanz pay my Meta balance?+

No. You pay Meta directly. Unbanz only prepares and sends the appeal.

Do I need to share my Facebook or Meta login?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from the ad account ID, the portfolio ID and a screenshot of the notice. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
