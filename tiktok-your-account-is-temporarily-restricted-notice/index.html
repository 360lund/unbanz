What it usually means

TikTok limited features such as posting, commenting, messaging or going live for a period, usually after a guideline violation. The account is still active.

The first hour

  1. Open Settings, then Account status.
  2. Read which features are limited and until when.
  3. Appeal the specific violation once if it was a mistake.
  4. Avoid the flagged content type until it ends.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, with the date.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never give your password or login codes to anyone. Unbanz never asks for them.
