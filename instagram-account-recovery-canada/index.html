Official steps that come first

  1. If you see a disabled or suspended notice, tap it and request a review. Work through every screen, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, phone or password was changed, go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you have used for Instagram before.
  3. Look in your inbox and spam for an Instagram email about the change. Its undo link is time limited, so use it first.
  4. A video selfie request should be done in daylight, face on, with the head turns shown on screen.
  5. Send one complete appeal, then watch the email on the account for the reply.

What is different in Canada

  • Your Canadian number: Canada shares the +1 country code with the USA, so enter the full ten digits. If you lost the SIM or someone ported your number, call Rogers, Bell, Telus or your other carrier and ask them to lock the number before you start recovery.
  • Fraud reporting: the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca, 1-888-495-8501) takes fraud and cybercrime reports, and your local police can take a report as well. Tell your bank straight away if money moved, and consider a credit report alert with Equifax Canada and TransUnion if identity details leaked.
  • French and English notices: the notice follows your phone or account language. Screenshot it as it appears. We work by email in English.
  • Under 18 and sextortion: Cybertip.ca, run by the Canadian Centre for Child Protection, takes reports about online sexual exploitation, including sextortion of young people. Read what to do about sextortion on Instagram first and do not pay.
  • News content: since August 2023 Meta has blocked news links and posts for Canadian users on Instagram and Facebook after the Online News Act. That is a separate matter from a disabled account, so do not assume a missing news post means you were banned.

What not to do

  • Ignore strangers who DM you first saying they can “open” your account. Unbanz never cold-messages people and never asks for your password.
  • Don’t share your password or login codes with anyone, including us.
  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Instagram, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

Local questions, answered (2026)

Which ID does Instagram accept in Canada?

Meta’s list changes, but a Canadian passport, driver’s licence or provincial photo ID is the usual choice. Make sure the name matches the profile. Often Instagram asks for a video selfie instead of a document.

Disabled and Instagram shows no ID option?

Do the video selfie if offered, once, in good light. If nothing is offered, screenshot the screen with the date and avoid opening new accounts while you wait.

Hacked and disabled in Canada: who do I tell?

Use instagram.com/hacked first, then appeal the disable. Report the hack or scam to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501) and local police.

Where do I check status in Accounts Center?

Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status. If you cannot log in, the notice on the login screen has the review button.

Is there an outside appeal in Canada?

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner handles privacy complaints, not account appeals. There is no outside appeal body for Meta account decisions.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Canada?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

Where do I report a hacked Instagram account in Canada?+

To Instagram first, through instagram.com/hacked. If there was fraud, blackmail or stolen money, also report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and to your local police. Contact your bank immediately if money moved.

Is my account disabled because Canada blocks news on Instagram?+

No. The news block only affects whether news content shows up for Canadian users. A disabled notice is about your account and Instagram’s rules, and it has its own review option.
