1. Send the details

Message us on WhatsApp or email team@unbanz.com. Send the @username or profile link, a screenshot of the notice and when it happened. For an account removal, send the profile link and what it’s doing. For a username, send the name you want. For an ad account, add the account ID.

We tell you if it’s doable and what it costs. Scope, price, timing and any refund terms are agreed in the chat. Asking doesn’t book anything.

We get to work and keep you posted on WhatsApp or email. The usual estimate is 3–14 days from the day we start.

4. Done

Account back, account down or username yours. Guaranteed. If we take it on, we get it done. Hear from clients who’ve been through it.
