Good to know.

Can you ban any Facebook account?+

No. Only accounts that clearly break Facebook's rules, like fake profiles, impersonation, scams and harassment. We report them through Facebook's own tools and Facebook makes the call.

Can you remove a Facebook Page?+

Yes, when the Page breaks Facebook's rules, for example a scam or impersonation Page.

Will they know I reported them?+

Reports on Facebook are private, so who reported stays with you.

Will the account actually come down?+

We only take on accounts that clearly break platform policy, and we stay on it until the platform acts. If an account isn't breaking the rules, we'll tell you straight and won't take it on.

Do you handle Marketplace scammers?+

Yes. Send the listing, the profile and your messages.

Can Unbanz get a violating Facebook account or Page removed?+

Yes, if it’s breaking the rules: fake profiles, impersonators, scam Pages and harassers.

It depends on the account. You get a price before we start.
