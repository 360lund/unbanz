A simple structure

  1. One line on the business: name, country, what you sell and who to.
  2. What the notice says and when you got it.
  3. What you think triggered it, in your own words, or that you do not know.
  4. What you checked and changed: ads, landing page, admins, billing.
  5. What you attach, and a polite request for a person to look at it again.

What to leave out

  • Threats, legal language and long stories about lost revenue.
  • Claims that Meta is wrong without evidence.
  • Details that are not true or that you cannot prove.
  • Copy and pasted templates from forums.
  • Repeated requests every few hours.

A short example

I run a small online store selling leather bags from Leeds. My ad account was disabled on 12 October for unacceptable business practices. I checked my ads and found two with a countdown timer that was not real. I removed them, added our returns policy and contact page to the site, and attached our company registration and a recent supplier invoice. I would be grateful if a person could review the account again.

Use your own facts. The example only shows the tone and the order.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Ad account questions people also ask (2026)

What are the usual reasons for an ad account restriction?

The common ones are a declined or disputed payment, a billing country or currency change, a sudden spend increase, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new device or country, unverified business details, or an admin whose personal profile is itself restricted. Account Quality usually names the policy.

Business verification or ID check pending: what helps?

Use documents that match the business portfolio name and address exactly, such as an EIN letter (US), Companies House record (UK), CRA business number (Canada), KvK extract (Netherlands) or Kbis/SIREN (France). A name mismatch is one of the most common reasons verification fails.

How long does an ad account review take?

Meta does not state one. While you wait, do not open new ad accounts or move spend to a fresh profile, which can be read as circumventing systems. Check Account Quality for status rather than resubmitting.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

How long should an ad account appeal be?+

A short paragraph or two is enough. Facts and proof matter more than length.

Can I appeal more than once?+

Meta allows follow-up reviews in some situations, but repeating the same request rarely helps. Change something before you ask again.

Should I use a template from a forum?+

No. Reviewers see many copied appeals. Use your own facts.

Do I need to attach documents?+

Attach what proves the business is real and what shows the changes you made, when Meta gives you a way to upload.

Do I need to share my Facebook or Meta login?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from the ad account ID, the portfolio ID and a screenshot of the notice. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
