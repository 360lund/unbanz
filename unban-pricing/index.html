1. Send the account

Your @username and a screenshot of what the app shows you. email us or email team@unbanz.com.

2. Get your price

We look at the notice, tell you if it’s doable and give you the price and timing in the chat.

3. Say go

We get to work and keep you posted by email.

4. You’re back in

Your account, followers and posts, back where they belong.

What sets the price

The platform, the type of notice (disabled, suspended, locked or hacked) and what’s happened so far, like an earlier appeal. Every account gets its own quote.

How long it takes

The usual estimate is 7–14 days from the day we start. You get a timeline for your account in the chat.
