Handoff for the agency

  1. Pause campaigns and note what ran last.
  2. Screenshot Account Quality, the notice and the date.
  3. List the admins on the business portfolio and the ad account.
  4. Send the details to Unbanz with the client’s go-ahead.
  5. Report plain facts to the client.

What to avoid while an appeal is open

  • Do not open replacement accounts to carry on spending.
  • Do not run the same ads from a different business asset.
  • Do not edit ads without reading the notice.
  • Do not file repeated requests of your own.

Repeated or contradictory requests can slow or complicate the decision.

What the client can do meanwhile

They can keep other channels running, prepare creative that follows the ad policies and gather business documents for the day Meta asks for them. The best answer when asked about timing is that Meta decides and you will report each step.

Good to know.

Can an agency resell ad account recovery?+

Yes. The agency stays the contact for the client, and Unbanz prepares the appeal through official routes behind the scenes.

What does Unbanz need?+

A screenshot of the notice and Account Quality, the date, the business portfolio admins, and the client’s go-ahead. Never a password.

Can I keep running ads on another account?+

Do not use other accounts to avoid a restriction. It can make things worse.

Does Unbanz run ads?+

No. It handles account recovery and appeals only.

Will the ad account be restored?+

Nobody can promise that. Meta decides.
