Get your business name as your handle.

This page is for businesses, shops and startups whose brand name is taken on Instagram, Facebook or Snapchat, usually by an empty or abandoned account, and who don’t have a registered trademark yet.

Short answer

Without a trademark, the realistic routes are: the handle is inactive, the account is squatting on your name, or it’s impersonating your business. Which one applies decides how the claim goes. A handle used by an active, unrelated person usually isn’t claimable.

Routes that can work

  • Inactive account: no posts, no profile photo, no activity for a long time.
  • Squatter: an account holding your name to sell it back to you.
  • Impersonation: an account posing as your business.
  • Trademark: if you have one, see trademark username claims.

Meanwhile, pick a clean backup

Use something close and easy: yourbrandhq, yourbrand.co, getyourbrand. Avoid extra underscores and numbers. Keep the same backup across apps so customers can find you.

Don’t do this

  • Don’t buy the account from someone in DMs. That breaks the platform’s terms and you can lose it.
  • Don’t give anyone your password to “move” the handle.
