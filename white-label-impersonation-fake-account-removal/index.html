What counts as impersonation

Impersonation

An account pretending to be another person or business, usually using their name, photos and details to mislead others.

A fan account, a parody account or someone sharing the same name is not always impersonation. The platform judges each report on the facts. The direct service page is impersonation account removal.

What a good report contains

  • The link to the fake account and screenshots of it.
  • The client’s real account link.
  • What is being copied: name, photos, business details.
  • Evidence that people are being misled, such as messages from confused customers.
  • Where relevant, proof of the client’s identity or business that the platform asks for.

What this service is not

  • It is not a way to remove criticism or negative posts.
  • It is not mass reporting. Unbanz files accurate single reports and does not organise groups to report.
  • It does not use fake reports under any circumstance.

How to handle the client side

Tell the client to warn followers briefly, without engaging with the fake account. Keep screenshots of any scam messages. Do not reply to the fake account’s owner. After a report, the platform decides, and the client should not promise customers a removal date.

Short message to the client

I have started a report with the platform on the account copying you. Please do not respond to it or message the person behind it. If customers tell you they were contacted, send me screenshots. The platform decides, so I cannot say when or whether it will be removed.
