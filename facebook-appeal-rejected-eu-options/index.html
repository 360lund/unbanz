Facebook rejected your appeal and you live in the EU?

Once Facebook rejects an appeal, it says no further review is available. In the EU, the Digital Services Act leaves one more door open.

Updated 31 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If Facebook rejected your appeal and you live in the EU, you can take the decision to a certified out-of-court dispute body under Article 21 of the Digital Services Act, such as Appeals Centre Europe or User Rights. The decision notice carries the reference it needs. The body gives a written opinion; it cannot force Facebook to restore the account.

Find the notice

Email, notifications or Account Status.

Dispute body

Certified under DSA Article 21.

Your data

A GDPR access request.

Steps

  1. Find the decision in your email, your notifications or Account Status, and look for the section for people in the EU.
  2. Create the reference number Facebook offers there. Dispute bodies use it to ask Facebook about the decision.
  3. Pick a certified body and file on its website. Appeals Centre Europe, User Rights and ODS are examples.
  4. Keep the account details, the dates and the screenshots in one place. You may be asked for them more than once.

What a dispute body can and cannot do

Facebook has to engage with a certified body in good faith and consider its view, but the opinion is not binding. Filing does not stop you from going to court, and you can also complain to your national Digital Services Coordinator. Under the GDPR you can ask Meta for a copy of the personal data it holds about the account.

Related

See Facebook appeal rejected, the statement of reasons and your DSA appeal rights.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Does this work from the UK?+

The DSA routes are for people in the EU. In the UK, a UK GDPR subject access request is the main tool. See the UK guide on our site.

Is a dispute body the same as Facebook support?+

No. It is independent of Facebook and cannot log in to your account or switch it back on.

Will a dispute body restore my account?+

It cannot order that. It gives a reasoned opinion, and Facebook makes the final decision.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
