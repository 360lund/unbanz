Have a USPTO trademark and the Instagram handle is taken?

US brands with a registered mark have a solid starting point. Here is what Instagram looks at.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A USPTO registered trademark can support an Instagram username claim when the account holding the handle uses it in a way that infringes your mark or impersonates your brand. Prepare the registration number, the classes, screenshots of how the account uses the name, and proof you trade under the brand in the US.

Registration number

From the USPTO record.

Classes

Goods and services must overlap.

Evidence of use

Website, products, invoices.

What to prepare

  • The USPTO registration number and certificate.
  • Screenshots of the account and its use of the name.
  • Your website and product pages.
  • A short timeline of the brand.

Limits

  • Supplemental Register marks carry less weight.
  • Common words are harder.
  • Inactive accounts that do not infringe are handled differently.

What to send

  • The exact @username you want.
  • Your current Instagram profile, so the handle can go onto it.
  • Why the name is yours: brand, business name, registered trademark, or the name you used before.
  • What you have already tried, if anything.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
