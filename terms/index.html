1. Asking is free

Messaging us or asking for a quote is free. It doesn’t book a service or commit you to anything.

2. We agree everything first

We confirm, by email, the scope, the price, the estimated timing and any refund terms. Those agreed terms are what apply to your job.

3. How payment works

Payment is by card, agreed in your chat or email first. Details are on payment and refunds.

4. What we need from you

  • For unbans: you own the account or are authorised to act for its owner.
  • Accurate details: the username or link, a screenshot of the notice and when it happened.
  • Your password and login codes stay with you.

5. Timing

The usual estimate is 7–14 days from the day work starts. We handle the process end to end; Meta makes the final decision.

6. Refunds

If a username claim fails, you get a full 100% refund. For other services, refund terms are agreed for each job alongside the price. See payment and refunds.

7. Independent service

Unbanz is an independent service, not affiliated with Meta, Instagram or Facebook. The platforms own and run their services.

8. Privacy

How we handle your details is in our privacy notice.

9. Contact
