How do I pay Unbanz?+

By card or crypto. The price, payment method and terms are agreed in your chat or email.

Messaging us or asking for a quote is free. It doesn’t book a service or commit you to anything.

We confirm, in your WhatsApp chat or email, the scope, the price, the estimated timing and any refund terms. Those agreed terms are what apply to your job.

We accept card and crypto; the method is agreed in your chat or email first. Details are on payment and refunds.
