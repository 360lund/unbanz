Facebook disabled in the UK: quick answers (2026)

Where do I appeal a disabled Facebook account in the UK?

From the notice you see when you log in, or from Meta Accounts Center. There is no separate form for the UK. Old contact-form links mostly redirect now; see the official links directory.

How long do I have to appeal?

Facebook says you can appeal a suspension within 180 days, and that the period varies by region. After that, or after a failed appeal, the account is permanently disabled.

Is there a Meta phone number in the UK?

No. Meta says it has no general public phone number for customer service. A number claiming to be Meta support is likely a scam.

What ID does Meta accept from the UK?

Meta usually accepts a UK passport, a photocard driving licence or a national identity card. Use the same name that is on the account.

What about my phone number?

Use the +44 number on the account. Drop the first 0 when you type it, so 07700 900123 becomes +44 7700 900123.

Does Meta Verified help a disabled account?

Meta Verified gives access to support for accounts you can still log in to. It does not reopen an account that is already disabled.

Is there an official body above Meta in the UK?

Ofcom enforces the Online Safety Act, but it does not decide individual account appeals. Since Brexit, UK users cannot use the EU’s Appeals Centre Europe under the DSA. You can ask Meta for your data with a UK GDPR subject access request; see the SAR guide.

Who do I report a hack or scam to?

Report a hacked account or a scam to Report Fraud at reportfraud.police.uk/ or 0300 123 2040. It replaced Action Fraud on 4 December 2025 for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101. The NCSC has a guide at ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/recovering-a-hacked-account.

Can someone in the UK get my account back for me?

Nobody can override Meta, and nobody legit needs your password. A service can help you prepare and follow up the appeal. Meta makes the final decision.

Meta does not process account appeals by email. Paste a short version of your letter into the appeal field shown in the app, Account Status or Business Support Home.

Good to know.

Can I call Facebook?+

Facebook does not offer phone support for disabled accounts. Numbers online that claim to be Facebook support are often scams.

Can the ICO restore my account?+

No. The ICO handles data protection complaints. It does not review bans.

Will a new profile help?+

Facebook allows one personal profile per person. A new one can be removed and can count against the appeal.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
