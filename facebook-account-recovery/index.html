Can you help with business Pages?+

We review restricted Pages and Page access cases. Include the Page URL and explain your relationship to the business.

Is a restricted ad account the same service?+

Ad account restrictions need different details. Use our ad account recovery page and send the affected ad account ID and restriction notice.

How long does a Facebook unban take?+

The estimate is 3–14 days from case acceptance and receipt of the details. We confirm your timeline first; complex cases can take longer.
