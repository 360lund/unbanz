Restricted

Limits on what you can do, such as spend or features. The ad account may still run.

Disabled

The ad account cannot run ads until the decision changes.

Find the asset

A restriction can sit on the business portfolio, the ad account, a Page or a personal profile. Open Account Quality.

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Which asset is affected

AssetWhat a restriction means
Business portfolioThe portfolio is not allowed to advertise.
Ad accountThe ad account, its ads and some advertising assets are disabled.
PageThe Page is not allowed to advertise.
Personal profileThat person cannot advertise or create new ads. Other people on the business may still be able to advertise.

This follows the structure in Meta’s About advertising restrictions page. Walkthrough: restricted ad account guide.

Statuses in plain language

StatusMeaning
Restricted or limitedSome features or ad spending are held back while the issue is fixed.
DisabledThe asset cannot be used until the decision changes. Look for a review option.
Disapproved adOne ad was rejected. The account itself may be fine.
WarningMeta is flagging a risk. Fix the cause before it becomes a restriction.

Where to look first

On a computer, open Meta Business Support Home and check Account status overview, or open Account Quality. Select the affected asset, screenshot the reason and the date, and note the policy named. Guide: Account Quality walkthrough.

Common reasons Meta lists

  • The advertiser does not follow the Advertising Standards, Community Standards or Commerce Policies.
  • Meta suspects the business portfolio or its assets were compromised.
  • Two-factor authentication requirements are not met.
  • Unusual payment or account activity, or failed or disputed payments.

Requesting a review

You need to be an admin on the account. Fix the cause first, for example secure the account or resolve a failed payment, then request a review once from the What you can do section. Repeated requests for the same item do not help. See rejected ad review and what to do after a rejected appeal.

Good to know.

What is the difference between a restricted and a disabled ad account?+

A restricted ad account has limits, such as spending or features. A disabled ad account cannot run ads until the decision changes. Both show their reason in Account Quality.

Can a personal profile restriction stop my ads?+

Yes. A restricted personal profile cannot advertise or create new ads, and ad accounts where that person is the only user may also be disabled. Other people on the business may still be able to advertise.

Where do I see why my ad account was restricted?+

In Meta Business Support Home under Account status overview, or in Account Quality. Select the asset and read the reason and the policy named.

Should I request a review straight away?+

Fix the obvious cause first, such as security or payment problems, then request a review once.

Can Unbanz recover my account?+

We help you submit a clean appeal through Meta’s official process and handle it end to end. Meta makes the final decision, so we tell you upfront whether we think it’s doable.
