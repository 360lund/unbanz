A fan page holds your Instagram username?

Fan pages are common and not always breaking a rule. The route depends on how it presents itself.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A fan page is allowed to exist, but not to pose as you or your brand. If it uses your exact name, photo or logo in a way that suggests it is official, an impersonation or brand report may apply. If it is clearly labelled as a fan page, a claim is harder and the route is different.

Read how it presents itself

Labelled fan page or posing as you?

Collect proof

Your ID, brand papers, or official accounts that link to you.

Do not message them aggressively

A friendly approach is not our call, but threats can backfire.

What decides the route

  • The bio says fan page, tribute or unofficial.
  • It posts as if it were you, uses your logo or sells in your name.
  • It has been inactive for a long time.
  • It uses a slightly different spelling of your name.

Every claim goes through the platform’s own processes. No bots, no false reports, no buying or selling accounts.

What to send

  1. The handle you want and the fan page handle.
  2. Screenshots of the fan page bio and recent posts.
  3. Proof you are the person or brand named.
  4. Anything that shows it is posing as you.

What to send

  • The exact @username you want.
  • Your current Instagram profile, so the handle can go onto it.
  • Why the name is yours: brand, business name, registered trademark, or the name you used before.
  • What you have already tried, if anything.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

How it works with Unbanz

  2. We tell you honestly whether this is something we can help with.
  3. We check the handle and pick the official route that fits it. Your password stays with you.
  4. We stay in touch while you wait. Meta makes the final decision, and nobody can promise the outcome.

Who this is for

  • Public figures and creators.
  • Brands with fan communities.
  • Musicians and artists.

Staying safe

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or claims to have a special way in, walk away. Unbanz never asks for logins and has no special access. We use the same official routes that are open to you.
