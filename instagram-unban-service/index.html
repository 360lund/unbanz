Can my account get banned again after it’s back?+

It can, if the same thing that caused the first action happens again. When your account is back, we share simple steps to keep it in good standing: turn on two-factor authentication, remove apps you no longer use that are linked to the account, and steer clear of whatever the notice named. Meta makes every decision, so nobody can promise an account is never actioned again, and anyone who does is overselling.

One complete, accurate appeal through the right official route works better, followed by clear replies whenever Meta asks for more, like an ID or a video selfie.
