What white label means here

You sell account recovery or username claims to your own clients, under your own name. Unbanz does the platform work in the background. In practice:

  • Your client deals with you. You stay the contact and keep the relationship.
  • You send us the details by WhatsApp or email, and we tell you honestly if we can help.
  • We prepare the appeal or username claim and send it through the platform’s official process.
  • What you charge your client is up to you. Terms between you and Unbanz are agreed before work starts.
  • Meta, or the platform concerned, makes the final decision. Nobody can promise an outcome.

Who it suits

  • Marketing and social media agencies whose clients lose access to Instagram or Facebook.
  • Resellers and freelancers who want an account recovery option without building the know-how.
  • Agencies that need a handle claimed for a client brand.
  • Not for accounts that were really used to scam or abuse people.

What you can hand over

  • Instagram and Facebook accounts that are disabled, suspended or hacked. See Instagram account recovery and Facebook account recovery.
  • Username claims for a client’s brand handle. See Instagram username claim. Our refund policy: 100% refund if a username claim fails.
  • Ad accounts that were restricted or disabled. See agency ad account recovery.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with the client’s username, a screenshot of the notice and the date. You do not need to name the client.
  2. We tell you honestly if we can help. Terms are agreed with you before any work starts.
  3. We prepare the appeal or username claim and send it through the platform’s official process.
  4. We keep you posted. You pass the update to your client in your own words.
