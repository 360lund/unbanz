Payment declined, then the ad account was restricted?

Clear the failed charge first. An appeal that ignores an unpaid balance is easy to reject.

Updated 4 November 2026

If a Meta ad payment declined and the ad account was restricted, open Billing and Account Quality, clear unpaid balance, and use a payment method in the business name. Remove failed cards. Request one review saying you cleared the balance and updated billing. Do not open a new ad account while this one is limited. Meta makes the final decision.

Pay what is owed in Billing first.

Fix the method

Then one review

Say what you changed, in a few lines.

Order that usually works

  1. Open Account Quality in Meta Business Suite and screenshot the notice before changing anything.
  2. Work out which asset is limited: the ad account, the business portfolio, a Page or an admin profile.
  3. Clear any unpaid balance and remove payment methods that are not in the business name.
  4. Make the business name and address match across the portfolio, the ad account, the website and your documents.
  5. Request one review, with a short note that explains what the business does.

Why the restriction follows a decline

Failed charges, chargebacks and cards used on other limited accounts are common triggers. Meta may restrict the ad account until billing looks clean. See Meta ad payment declined and fix the cause before the appeal. Related: Meta ad account restricted.

What not to do

  • Opening a second ad account on the same business to keep spending.
  • Adding random personal cards that are not in the business name.
  • Sending several review requests while billing is still failing.
  • Sharing Business Manager passwords with anyone offering a restore.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID, if you have one.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date visible.
  • What you were advertising and what changed just before the notice.
  • Who the admins on the account are.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Ad account questions people also ask (2026)

Business verification or ID check pending: what helps?

Use documents that match the business portfolio name and address exactly, such as an EIN letter (US), Companies House record (UK), CRA business number (Canada), KvK extract (Netherlands) or Kbis/SIREN (France). A name mismatch is one of the most common reasons verification fails.

What are the usual reasons for an ad account restriction?

The common ones are a declined or disputed payment, a billing country or currency change, a sudden spend increase, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new device or country, unverified business details, or an admin whose personal profile is itself restricted. Account Quality usually names the policy.

Where do I request the review?

Go to Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/accountquality), select the ad account, and use Request review if it is shown. For a personal ad account, open Accounts Center, then Account status.

The card works everywhere else. Why did Meta decline it?+

Should I remove the old card?+

Yes, if it keeps failing. Leave one working method that belongs to the business.
