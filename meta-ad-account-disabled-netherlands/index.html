Ad account disabled or restricted in the Netherlands?

Dutch advertisers usually have a clear business register entry, which helps an appeal when the details match. This page is for businesses in the Netherlands with a restricted Meta ad account.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A Dutch business with a restricted ad account should check the notice in Account Quality, make sure the details match the KVK extract, clear any balance and request one review. Unbanz can prepare the appeal and the documents Meta may request. Meta makes the final decision.

Use the KVK extract

The name and address on your Kamer van Koophandel entry should match Meta.

Add the BTW number

Enter your VAT number in billing if you are registered.

Check the site

A clear contact page and returns policy help with ad review.

Checks specific to the Netherlands

  • Use the trade name and legal name from the KVK extract.
  • Sole traders (eenmanszaak) can use the KVK extract and a bank statement in the business name.
  • Add your BTW-id in billing if you are VAT registered.
  • Keep the ad account in euro if you invoice in the Netherlands.
  • Make sure the website shows your KVK number and contact details.

Regulated advertising in Dutch

Finance, health, alcohol, gambling and housing ads have extra rules in the Netherlands. If you work in a regulated area, check your ads and pages against Meta’s policies and local rules before you appeal.

First steps, in order

  1. Open Account Quality and screenshot the notice and the date.
  2. Check the people on the portfolio and remove any admin you do not know. Turn on two-factor for the rest.
  3. Clear any unpaid balance and make sure the payment method is yours and current.
  4. Check the business name, address and website match your Dutch registration.
  5. Request the review once, with a short note on what you sell and what you changed.

Dutch advertisers selling across borders

Many Dutch shops sell into Belgium and Germany. Make sure prices, shipping and returns on the landing page match the country the ad targets, since mismatches cause rejections. Our Dutch pages are at unbanz.com/nl/.

Your rights in the EU

In the EU, the Digital Services Act may apply to decisions like this. Meta can give you a statement of reasons, and you may have access to an out-of-court dispute body as well as the normal review. Keep every message Meta sends you.

How Unbanz helps

We help Dutch businesses get the documents in line and prepare the appeal for Meta’s official review. You can read about the notice types on our page on restricted versus disabled and the main service page for disabled ad accounts. Meta makes the final decision.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
