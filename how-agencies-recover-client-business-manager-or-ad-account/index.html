How can marketing agencies recover a disabled Meta Business Manager or ad account for a client?

By the Unbanz team · updated November 20, 2026

Where the restriction sits, how to request a review in Account Quality, what to fix first, and when to hand a client’s Business Portfolio or ad account to a white-label partner.

Start agency onboarding →

Short answer

First find where the restriction sits: the ad account, the Business Portfolio (Business Manager), or the admin’s personal profile. Open Account Quality, read the exact reason and request a review from there, after fixing the cause (payment method, unverified business, policy-breaking ads). If the review fails or the whole portfolio is disabled, a white-label partner like Unbanz can run the case behind your agency. Meta makes the final decision.

1. Find where the restriction sits

  • Ad account disabled: one account can’t run ads; others in the portfolio may still work.
  • Business Portfolio restricted: every ad account inside is affected; often triggered by an admin profile or an unverified business.
  • Admin profile restricted: the person can’t advertise anywhere. Fix this first or every review will fail.

2. Fix the cause before you appeal

  • Payment: failed or disputed charges, a card the client doesn’t recognise, a new card added the same day as a spike in spend.
  • Verification: complete business verification with documents that match the legal name and website.
  • Ads: remove or edit the rejected ads named in Account Quality.
  • Access: remove unknown admins and partners added recently.

3. Request the review the right way

Request it from Account Quality, from an admin with a clean profile, once. Multiple reviews from different admins in a short time can slow things down. Keep screenshots of every reason and response.

4. When to hand it over

When the review is rejected, the portfolio is disabled with no review button, or the client’s spend is on hold for more than a few days. Send us the ad account ID or portfolio ID, Account Quality screenshots and the date. We work through Meta’s official process and send unbranded updates. The white-label rate is $1,200 per case (one account or handle). Agencies that send 5+ cases a month get volume pricing, agreed in writing after a first test case. Payment is upfront by bank transfer to our US or UK business bank account (no card payments). Usually 7–14 days from the day we start, with a 100% refund if the account isn’t reinstated within 14 days, paid the same business day. A signed agreement with a named authorised signatory is available before you pay.
