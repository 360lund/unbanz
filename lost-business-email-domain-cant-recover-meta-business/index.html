Common after a rebrand, a lapsed domain, or a deleted staff mailbox.

Get the email working again first: renew or re-register the domain, recreate the exact mailbox, and then use Meta’s normal recovery and verification emails. Meta sends codes and security notices to the address on file, so it does not matter that the mailbox is new as long as the address is the same. If the domain was bought by someone else, the old address is gone; update contact details from an admin profile that still works, or use “Request full control of a business portfolio” with your business documents. Steps

  1. Check the domain at your registrar. Renew it, or recover it during the grace period.
  2. Recreate the mailbox exactly as it was (same address, same domain).
  3. From a working admin profile, update the business email and contact details in Business Settings.
  4. If verification is needed, use documents that match the legal name, and a website on the same domain.
  5. If no admin can log in, request full control with proof such as company registration and domain ownership.

If someone else now owns the domain

  • Do not try to reach the old address any other way. You cannot get its codes.
  • Work from a still-working admin profile, or request full control.
  • Watch for phishing: new domain owners sometimes try password resets on accounts tied to the address.
