A fake Instagram account is using my photos. How do I get it removed?

If it pretends to be you, report it as impersonation; if it’s just using photos you own, use the copyright report. Show proof that you’re the person or the owner of the photos and the right form does the work.

Two different rules apply, and picking the right one matters. Using your face to pose as you is impersonation. Using photos you took without your permission is copyright, even if they’re not pretending to be you.

Which report to use

Posing as you is impersonation. Posting your photos as theirs is copyright, reported by the person who owns them.

Evidence that helps

Links to the fake profile and the stolen posts, original photos with dates, and your real profile link.

While you wait

Tell your followers from your real account and do not reply to the fake one. Reporting works better than arguing.

Which report to use

  • Pretending to be you or your business. Impersonation report or form.
  • Posting your photos as theirs. Copyright report, from the person who owns them.
  • Fake profile used for scams. Report as a scam or fraud as well.

Evidence that helps

  1. Links to the fake profile and the stolen posts.
  2. Original photos with dates or metadata that show you took them.
  3. Your real profile link and ID if the form asks.

While you wait

  • Tell your followers from your real account.
  • Make your photos harder to scrape: set the account to private if it suits you.
  • Don’t reply to the fake account. Reporting works better than arguing.
