Meta ad account restricted from advertising?

“Restricted from advertising” can hit an ad account, a profile or a whole Business Portfolio. This page is for advertisers who see that notice in Meta Business Suite and need to know which one is blocked and how to get it reviewed.

Updated 29 October 2026

Find out what is restricted first. Meta can restrict the ad account, your personal profile’s ability to advertise, or the Business Portfolio. Each has its own Request review button in Account Quality. Appeal the right one, once, with a clear explanation.

Which one is restricted

The ad account, your personal profile’s ability to advertise, or the Business Portfolio. Each has its own Request review button.

What to try first

Read the reason Meta gives, verify your identity if asked with ID that matches your profile and remove admins you do not know.

What not to do

Do not create new ad accounts or portfolios, ask friends to run ads for the same Page, or send five reviews in a row.

Which one is restricted?

  • Ad account: only that account can’t run ads. Others in the portfolio still work.
  • Your profile: you can’t advertise from any account, even ones you just joined as admin.
  • Business Portfolio: every ad account inside it is stopped.

Account Quality lists each restricted asset separately. Screenshot it before you change anything.

What to try first

  1. Read the reason Meta gives. “Unusual activity” and “advertising policies” are handled differently.
  2. Verify your identity if Meta asks. Use an ID that matches the name on your profile.
  3. Remove admins you don’t recognise and turn on two-factor for everyone left.
  4. Request the review from the asset that’s actually restricted.

What not to do

  • Don’t create new ad accounts or portfolios to get around it.
  • Don’t ask friends to run ads for the same page from their profiles.
  • Don’t send five reviews in a row. Wait for the answer.

When to get help

Get help if the review was denied, there’s no review option, or the restriction came back after it was lifted. Unbanz prepares the appeal, documents and context, and handles it through Meta’s official process. More on ad account recovery.

What to send

  • Screenshots of Account Quality showing each restricted asset.
  • Ad account ID and Business Portfolio ID.
  • What changed recently: new admin, new card, new offer.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What does “restricted from advertising” mean on Meta?+

It means Meta has limited an ad account, a profile or a Business Portfolio from running ads. Account Quality shows which asset is restricted and why.

How long does a Meta ad account review take?+

Meta doesn’t publish a set time. Some reviews come back in a day, others take weeks.

Can I still run ads from another account?+

Not if your profile is restricted. Getting around a restriction can make it permanent.

Does Unbanz work with agencies?+

Yes. Unbanz helps advertisers, agencies and media buyers with restricted Meta ad assets through Meta’s official review.

Is Unbanz affiliated with Meta?+

No. Unbanz is an independent service.

Should I open a new ad account while I wait?+

Usually not. A new account opened after a restriction can look like an attempt to get around Meta’s systems and can put the new account at risk too. Read can I make a new ad account after a ban before you decide.

What should I avoid while a review is open?+

Do not send repeated requests, add new cards or admins to the account, or run ads from another account tied to the same business. Pay any unpaid balance, answer any request for documents, and keep screenshots of every notice.
