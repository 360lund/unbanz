The first 48 hours

  1. Screenshot the notice, including the date and any appeal deadline shown.
  2. Log in from the phone you normally use and follow the appeal from the notice. Write in plain language what the business sells and why the decision looks wrong.
  3. If Instagram asks for ID or a video selfie, complete it once, in good light, with the name on your ID matching the account owner.
  4. Check Accounts Center. If the linked Facebook profile is also restricted, deal with that first, because it can block the Instagram appeal.
  5. Pause any ads running from the account in Ads Manager so you are not paying for traffic to a profile nobody can see.

Keeping the business running

Post a short note on your website and email list saying the Instagram account is temporarily unavailable and how to order. Update the link on your Google Business Profile. If customers DM you for orders, give them a phone number or email they can use. None of this affects the appeal.

What not to do

  • Do not create a new business account with the same email, phone or device. Instagram can treat it as an attempt to get around the decision.
  • Ignore strangers in your DMs who ask for your password or codes. Nobody can guarantee a reinstatement.
  • Do not delete posts in bulk. It does not lift a suspension and can remove evidence that the account was genuine.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Questions people also ask (2026)

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.

Good to know.

How long do I have to appeal?+

Instagram usually shows the deadline on the notice. Check what your notice says and act well before it ends.

Will my followers be lost?+

While the account is disabled it is hidden, not deleted. If the appeal succeeds, followers and posts usually come back with it.

Can I run my shop from a new account meanwhile?+

It is risky. A new account linked to the same person or device can be removed for the same reason. Use your other channels instead.

Do I need to share my Instagram password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
