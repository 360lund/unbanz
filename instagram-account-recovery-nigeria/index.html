Instagram account recovery in Nigeria.

Instagram account hacked or disabled and you’re in Nigeria? Here are the official steps first, where to report fraud, and how Unbanz helps you appeal through Meta’s official process.

Short answer

Instagram account recovery in Nigeria follows Instagram’s own routes: request a review on a disabled notice, or use instagram.com/hacked if someone took over the account. If money or blackmail is involved, report it locally too. Unbanz helps you submit a clean appeal through Meta’s official process; Meta makes the final decision.

Official steps that come first

  1. If you see a disabled or suspended notice, tap it and request a review. Finish every step, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password was changed, go to instagram.com/hacked on a device and network you have used before.
  3. Check your email, including spam, for a message from Instagram about the change and use its undo link if it still works.
  4. If Instagram asks for a video selfie, record it in good light and turn your head as shown. Then wait for the reply to the email on the account instead of sending repeat appeals.

Checks that matter in Nigeria

  • Your +234 number: text codes go to the number on the account. SIMs are linked to a NIN, so a replacement SIM is issued through your network’s service centre with your NIN. Do that first or the codes go nowhere. If the SIM was swapped without you, tell your bank too.
  • Phishing is the usual way in: fake “verification badge”, grant and giveaway messages, and links sent from a friend’s hacked account. Never enter your login on a page you reached from a message.
  • Blackmail or fraud: do not pay. Save screenshots and links, then report through the Nigeria Police Force National Cybercrime Centre e-reporting portal at nccc.npf.gov.ng/ereport/. Financial fraud can also be reported to the EFCC. The old incb.npf.gov.ng address has been replaced.
  • Brands and online sellers: if the account sells products, keep your business name, CAC registration if you have one, and product photos to hand if you are asked to show the business is real.
  • Everything in English: we work over WhatsApp or email in English. You send the notice as it appears on your screen.

Not sure which problem you have? Disabled or suspended, hacked and hacked, then disabled each have their own steps. If someone is threatening you with private photos, read what to do about sextortion on Instagram.

What not to do

  • Don’t pay or message anyone from a DM who says they can “open” your account.
  • Don’t share your password or login codes with anyone, including us.
  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Instagram, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Nigeria?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

Where do I report a hacked Instagram account in Nigeria?+

To Instagram first, through instagram.com/hacked. If there was fraud, blackmail or stolen money, also report it through the Nigeria Police Force National Cybercrime Centre e-reporting portal, and tell your bank at once if money moved.

What if I lost my SIM or the number on the account?+

Get the SIM replaced through your network with your NIN, then use instagram.com/hacked. Instagram offers other ways to prove it is you, such as a video selfie. Send us what you have and we tell you which route fits.
