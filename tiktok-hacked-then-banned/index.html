Steps

  1. Secure the email account linked to TikTok.
  2. Find TikTok emails about changed email, phone or password, and screenshot them.
  3. Report the hack via Report a problem or tiktok.com/feedback.
  4. Appeal the ban, stating the dates the account was out of your control.
  5. Once restored, remove unknown devices and change the password.
