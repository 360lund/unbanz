# Unbanz: competitor research and launch decisions

Research date: 15 October 2026. Prices below are advertised by the businesses, not verified transaction prices. Their testimonials, recovery rates, platform relationships and result claims were not independently verified. This is a review of public positioning and contact flows, not a ranking or traffic audit.

Some reviewed pages included visible placeholders or inconsistent presentation. Those are reasons to evaluate their positioning critically, not evidence about their service results. Unbanz should use genuine proof supplied by its owner, not borrowed testimonials or invented metrics.

## What changed on Unbanz

The homepage states the core offer first: Instagram and Facebook unbans, Meta ad account recovery and ban requests. Four visible service rows replace hidden service details. Each has an immediate WhatsApp link and a deeper service page. Instagram-only username claims remain secondary. Verification and follower services stay removed.

WhatsApp is the main conversion path. Each link starts a draft with the service name and space for the account and issue. Email is visible as an alternative. No website signup, lead database, API, Firebase or admin dashboard is needed. The old /apply/ URL directs visitors to contact. Privacy copy now describes the actual WhatsApp/email flow.

The imagery keeps the glass platform logos and returns to upright sans-serif type. Posts use short recovery or legitimate ban-request messages rather than a list of unrelated offers.

## Pricing decision

The public site now offers a free initial case check and a quote before work starts. This is a deliberate launch model, not an accidental missing price. Competitor pricing alone cannot establish a profitable service price when the rep cost, failed-case cost and refund arrangement are unknown.

For ordinary recovery, $300–450 is a useful low-to-mid-market range to test against costs, based on the observed $350–450 examples. It is a research benchmark, not a live Unbanz price or a market-wide average. The premium agencies advertise much higher prices. Ad account, complex restriction, ban and username work should stay individually quoted until supplier rates are confirmed.

Before adding numerical starting prices, provide the rep's charge for Instagram, Facebook, ad accounts and removal cases, plus who pays if a case fails. For ads, contribution per accepted case must cover the rep cost, payment fees, support time, expected refunds and the cost of acquiring that customer. Do not advertise a money-back or no-result/no-fee promise unless that is the actual agreed policy.

## SEO work completed

- Separate, crawlable HTML pages for Instagram recovery, Facebook recovery and Meta ad account recovery.
- Existing /unban/ hub, /ban/ and Instagram-only /username/ retained with distinct purposes.
- Useful platform-specific content: problem types, what to send, scope, pricing approach and FAQs.
- Unique page titles and descriptions; canonical URLs under https://unbanz.com/.
- Organization, WebSite, Service and breadcrumb structured data where relevant. Service markup helps describe the site; it does not create a promised Google rich result.
- A sitemap containing the indexable pages, working internal links, Open Graph/Twitter preview images and a crawlable contact page.
- Locally served fonts and optimized images; hero logo assets are embedded to avoid extra fetches.
- Primary calls to action are ordinary links and work without JavaScript. Content also remains visible without JavaScript.
- No fake review schema, keyword-stuffed city pages, unsupported result claims or FAQ rich-result promises. Google limits FAQ rich results chiefly to authoritative government/health sites.

## Search and ad launch steps that require the owner's accounts

1. Deploy the single static website package and connect unbanz.com as the primary Vercel domain. Verify that the HTTPS domain serves the homepage and all service pages; canonical tags already use that domain.
2. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console using its supplied DNS record. Submit https://unbanz.com/sitemap.xml and inspect the homepage and the three recovery URLs.
3. Send Instagram recovery ads to /instagram-account-recovery/, Facebook ads to /facebook-account-recovery/, ad-account leads to /ad-account-recovery/ and impersonation leads to /ban/.
4. Keep ad text and destination text aligned. Use actual recovery screenshots/testimonials only with permission. Add real case evidence when available.
5. Use campaign URL parameters for source attribution. The site appends supplied campaign labels to WhatsApp drafts without installing tracking pixels. No Pixel, Conversions API, analytics account or ad campaign is connected by this work.
6. Track manually: service, campaign, qualified conversation, quote, accepted case, rep cost and result. Judge ads by cost per accepted case and contribution, not just clicks.
7. Review Search Console's real queries after indexing. Expand content around questions customers actually ask. No keyword volume, backlink strength or ranking forecasts were inferred from these pages.

Google does not promise indexing or rankings. The private ChatGPT preview is not the public SEO deployment. The separate unbanz-red.vercel.app project cannot be updated here without its repository/access; this package is the deployable handoff for that project.
