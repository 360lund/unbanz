What usually sits behind it

  • A new device, number and place all at once.
  • Too many login attempts.
  • A VPN on the new phone.
  • A phone that was previously used for a banned account.

What to do

  1. Turn off any VPN.
  2. Log in once from the official app.
  3. Use the unlock form and mention the new phone.
  4. Send us the message if the lock stays.

What to send

  • Your Snapchat username.
  • A screenshot of the lock or ban message, with the date visible.
  • Which phone number or email is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What phone you use and what happened before it started.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Email us with your Snapchat username and a screenshot of the message.
  2. We tell you honestly whether this is something we can help with.
  3. We prepare the appeal and the details to go through Snapchat’s own support channels.
  4. We stay in touch while you wait. Snapchat makes the final decision, and nobody can promise the outcome.

Who this is for

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or wants to be paid in gift cards, walk away. Can I unlock Snapchat by making a new account?

Not while the device is banned. New accounts on the same device can be locked too.
