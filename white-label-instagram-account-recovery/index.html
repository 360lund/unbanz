Your client’s Instagram account is disabled. You keep the client, and Unbanz prepares the appeal quietly behind you.

White label Instagram account recovery means an agency passes a client’s disabled, suspended or locked account to Unbanz and stays the only contact. Unbanz prepares the appeal through official routes, under the agency’s brand. Instagram makes the final decision, and no result can be promised.

Your brand

Official routes

What the service covers

  • Accounts disabled or suspended for a policy reason.
  • Accounts restricted, limited or flagged for review.
  • Accounts locked behind an identity check the client cannot pass alone.
  • Accounts left in a loop after a failed appeal.

The difference between disabled, suspended and restricted is explained in disabled vs suspended vs restricted.

The handoff in four steps

  1. The client sends you the screenshot, the username and the date.
  2. You pass the details to Unbanz on WhatsApp or by email. No client name is required.
  3. Unbanz prepares and files the appeal through the official routes and reports back to you.
  4. You tell the client where things stand, in your own words.

What to tell your client about expectations

Instagram decides, and sometimes the answer is no. Tell the client that the process has been started and that you will report each step, but do not give a date or a result. Some accounts are removed for good. A calm, factual update is worth more than a prediction.

What is never part of the service

  • No passwords or login codes are requested from you or the client.
  • No buying, selling or renting accounts.
  • No fake or mass reports.
  • No claims of inside contacts at Meta.

Good to know.

Can an agency resell Instagram account recovery?+

Yes. The agency stays the contact and Unbanz prepares the appeal behind the scenes under the agency’s brand.

What does Unbanz need to start?+

The username or link, a screenshot of the notice with the date, the email and number on the account and the client’s go-ahead. Never a password.

Does the client see Unbanz?+

No. Instagram sends its own emails and checks to the account owner, but Unbanz does not contact the client.

Will the account definitely come back?+

No result can be promised. Instagram decides every appeal.

Does this cover business and creator accounts?+

Yes, personal, business and creator accounts follow the same appeal routes.
