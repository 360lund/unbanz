Restricted after sensitive-category ads. What now?

Health, housing, finance and similar ads have extra rules. Fix the ad setup before you request a review.

Updated 13 November 2026

A Meta ad account restricted after sensitive-category ads usually needs the ads and landing pages fixed first, then one clear request for review in Business Support Home. Special Ad Categories (housing, employment, credit, social issues, elections or politics) limit targeting. Health and wellness ads face claim and before-and-after limits. Do not open a new ad account to get around it. Meta makes the final decision.

What counts as sensitive here

  • Special Ad Categories: housing, employment, credit, and social issues, elections or politics. Wrong targeting or missing declaration is a common trigger.
  • Health and wellness: cure claims, before-and-after body images, personal-attribute targeting. See also health and wellness ads restricted.
  • Finance and products: financial products ads.

Before you request a review

  1. Open Business Support Home and read the exact reason.
  2. Pause or edit the ads named in the notice.
  3. Align the landing page with the ad: contact details, policy pages, no bait claims.
  4. Confirm Special Ad Category is set correctly if the offer falls in those areas.
  5. Request a review once and say what you changed.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Will choosing Special Ad Categories fix a restriction by itself?+

No. You still need compliant ads and, if offered, a request for review.

Can I keep running non-sensitive ads on the same account?+

If the whole ad account is restricted, no ads run until that restriction is cleared.

Is a new Business Portfolio safer?+

Creating new portfolios or ad accounts to avoid a restriction can be treated as circumventing systems.
