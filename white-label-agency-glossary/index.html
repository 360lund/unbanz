Account and platform terms

Disabled

The platform has switched the account off. The owner sees a notice and may have an option to appeal.

Suspended

The account is paused, often for a set time or until a check is passed.

Restricted

The account works, but some actions are limited.

Appeal

A request asking the platform to review its own decision. See Instagram account disabled appeal.

Account Quality

A Meta screen that shows the state of advertising and business assets, including restrictions.

Business portfolio

The Meta tool, formerly Business Manager, that holds Pages, ad accounts and access for a business. See business portfolio recovery.

Model terms

White label

The agency presents the service as its own. The partner works behind the scenes and does not contact the client. See white label account recovery.

Referral

The agency passes the client to another company, which deals with the client directly.

In-house

The agency does the recovery work with its own team.

Partner

The company the agency works with behind the scenes. Here, Unbanz.

The three models are compared in white label vs referral vs in-house.

Process terms

Intake

Collecting the details needed to start a request. See the intake checklist.

Handoff

Passing the details from the agency to the partner.

SOP

Standard operating procedure: the written steps a team follows each time. See the SOP template.

Go-ahead

The client’s permission for the agency to act on the account.

Problem terms

Impersonation

An account pretending to be someone else. See impersonation removal.

Hacked account

An account where someone else changed the login details or took control. See hacked account recovery.

Username claim

A request for a handle held by another account, using an official route such as a trademark report. See username claim.
