How to get a banned TikTok account back.

A step-by-step plan for a banned or permanently banned TikTok account, from the first appeal to what to do if it is rejected.

Updated 11 November 2026

Appeal from the ban notice in the TikTok app first. Open TikTok, tap the ban notice, and submit the appeal once in plain words. If you cannot open the app, use the web form at tiktok.com/feedback. Do not create a new account while the appeal is open, do not use a VPN, and do not pay anyone who claims a “private TikTok link”. Step by step

  1. Screenshot the full ban notice, including the reason and the date.
  2. Open the notice in the app and tap the option to appeal. If there is no button, go to Settings and privacy and look for account status or Report a problem.
  3. Write three or four sentences: who you are, that the account is yours, what you think went wrong, and that you have read the Community Guidelines.
  4. If you cannot log in, submit the same facts through tiktok.com/feedback, using the email or phone number on the account.
  5. Wait. Check the email and the app inbox linked to the account. Do not send a second appeal unless TikTok asks for one.
  6. If the appeal is rejected, collect the rejection message and decide whether to escalate or get help.

Temporary or permanent?

A temporary ban or restriction ends on its own; a timer usually shows in the notice. A permanent ban has no end date and usually says the account was permanently banned. Appeal both, but treat a permanent ban as urgent: the longer it sits, the more likely the account is scheduled for deletion. See permanent vs temporary TikTok bans.

What makes it worse

  • Creating a new account on the same phone, which can be treated as ban evasion.
  • Logging in through a VPN or a borrowed phone.
  • Sending many appeals, or the same text again and again.
  • Giving your login to anyone who promises an “inside” unban.
  • Deleting videos in a panic, which removes context TikTok may look at.
