How a claim works

Instagram has no “inactive” button and doesn’t sell usernames. Every claim goes through the platform’s own processes, matched to the handle: Meta’s intellectual property report if it matches your registered trademark, the impersonation report if the account pretends to be you or your brand, account recovery if the handle used to be yours, and the platform’s own processes for abandoned or squatted handles.
