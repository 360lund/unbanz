Is that Instagram security email real or a scam?

Real Instagram security emails come from instagram.com addresses and also appear inside the app. Fakes push you to log in through a link.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A real Instagram security email comes from an address ending in mail.instagram.com and is also listed in the app under Accounts Center, Password and security, Emails from Instagram. If the email says your email or password was changed and you did not do it, use the secure this account link in that real email straight away.

Check the sender

Real ones end in mail.instagram.com.

Check the app

Real emails are listed in Accounts Center.

Act on the real one

Use the secure link to undo changes.

How to check

  1. Do not tap any link yet. Look at the full sender address.
  2. In the Instagram app open Accounts Center, Password and security, then Recent emails or Emails from Instagram.
  3. If the email is listed there, it is real. If it is not, treat it as phishing and delete it.
  4. If you cannot log in, compare the sender and look for the email on another device.

If the change was not you

A real email about a changed email address usually includes a link to revert the change and secure the account. Use it from the original email. Then reset the password, turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, and remove sessions you do not recognise in Where you are logged in. If the link has expired, go to hacked Instagram account recovery.

Signs of a fake

  • Threats that the account will be deleted within hours.
  • Links to pages that are not instagram.com.
  • Requests for your password, login code or a payment.
  • Copyright or verification badge emails asking you to log in.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
