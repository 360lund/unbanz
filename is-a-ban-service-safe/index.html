Is it legal to pay someone to report an account?+

Yes. Reporting accounts that break the rules is exactly what platform reporting is for. We just help you do it properly, with the right evidence. We don’t touch accounts that aren’t breaking the rules.

Are my details kept private?+

Yes. Your details stay private.

Can Unbanz ban anyone?+

No. Nobody outside Instagram, Facebook or Snapchat can ban an account. We help you document and report accounts that actually break the rules, like scammers, impersonators, fake accounts, harassment and stolen content. The platform looks at the report and removes the account if it violates policy. If an account isn’t breaking any rules, we won’t take it on.

Is using Unbanz against Instagram’s terms?+

No. Reporting accounts that break the rules is exactly what Instagram’s reporting tools are for, and appealing a decision you think is wrong is what the appeal process is for. We use those same official channels. We don’t hack accounts, mass-report or buy and sell accounts.
