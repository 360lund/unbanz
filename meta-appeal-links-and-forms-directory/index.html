Meta appeal links and forms, in one place.

The official Instagram, Facebook and Meta ad account links, what each one is for, and which old forms now redirect. Checked 4 November 2026.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

There is no single Meta appeal form. A suspended or disabled Instagram or Facebook account is appealed from the notice you see when you log in, or from Accounts Center. A hacked account starts at instagram.com/hacked or facebook.com/hacked. A restricted or disabled ad account is reviewed from Business Support Home (Account Quality), by an admin. Meta has no general public phone number. Many old help/contact form links now redirect to Account Status or the Meta AI support assistant. Meta makes the final decision.

Official links only

Every link here goes to Meta or an official dispute body.

Which link do you need?

  • You see “We suspended your account” or “Your account has been disabled”: appeal from that screen. See disabled vs suspended vs restricted.
  • You cannot log in and see no message: use the hacked or find-your-account link.
  • Your ads stopped and the ad account says restricted or disabled: Business Support Home.
  • You want a username held by an inactive account: see username claims.

Old forms that no longer work the way they used to

Many help/contact links passed around in forums and videos were built years ago. Some now open a help article or the Meta AI support assistant instead of a form. If a link drops you on a help page, go back to the notice in the app or to Accounts Center. Do not pay anyone for a “private” appeal link. There is none.

Before you use any of them

  1. Screenshot the full notice with the date.
  2. Use the phone or computer you normally use.
  3. Appeal once, clearly. Do not open a new account while you wait.
  4. Keep every email from Meta, including any reference number.

If you are in the US, UK, Canada, the Netherlands or France

The in-app routes are the same. EU users, including in the Netherlands and France, can also use Digital Services Act routes after the internal appeal. See DSA appeal rights, Instagram suspended in the US, in the UK, in Canada, Facebook disabled in the Netherlands and in France.

How to spot a fake appeal link

  • Official Meta pages are on facebook.com, instagram.com, meta.com, threads.com or business.facebook.com. Check the address bar before typing anything.
  • Meta does not ask for your password, login codes or payment to process an appeal.
  • Messages from “Meta Support” in DMs, comments or WhatsApp are not how Meta contacts you about a disabled account.
  • Forms promising a “priority” or “private” appeal are not Meta forms.
