Business verification rejected the same documents again.

Re-uploading the same file rarely helps. Fix the mismatch first.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If Meta rejected business verification with the same documents, change what does not match before you upload again. Common mismatches are legal name vs trading name, address vs registration address, blurry scans, expired IDs, or a document for a different country than the Business Portfolio. Upload once, clearly. Meta makes the final decision.

What to change before trying again

  1. Compare the Business Portfolio legal name with the name on the company document letter by letter.
  2. Use the registered address Meta asks for, not a PO box or home address unless that is what the registry shows.
  3. Upload a full, colour scan or photo of an official document Meta lists for your country (registry extract, tax letter, utility bill as allowed).
  4. If a personal ID is required for an admin, use a current passport or government ID that matches the admin’s Facebook name.
  5. Remove extra pages and screenshots of screenshots.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone for a “private” or “direct” appeal link. There is none.
