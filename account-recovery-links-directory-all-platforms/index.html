Official appeal and recovery links for every platform.

Instagram, Facebook, Meta ads, Threads, WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube and Snapchat, on one page.

Updated 13 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Every major platform starts appeals inside its own app or notice, and none offers a general phone line for banned accounts. Instagram and Facebook appeals start from the login notice, Meta ad accounts from Business Support Home, TikTok from the in-app appeal or tiktok.com/feedback, YouTube from the termination appeal form, Snapchat from accounts.snapchat.com, and WhatsApp from Request a review. The official links for each are below.
