When recovery becomes a PR issue

  • A brand account disabled right before an announcement.
  • An executive profile hacked and posting scam links.
  • A fake account imitating the company and answering customers.
  • A Page unpublished during a media cycle.

In each moment, the first hour is about communication. The platform appeal is a parallel track that Unbanz can run for you.

What to say publicly while the account is down

Keep it short and factual: the account is temporarily unavailable, here is where updates will appear. Do not blame the platform and do not promise a date. Do not mention the recovery partner. The private client briefing can be plainer.

Internal client briefing, short form

The account is disabled and the appeal is filed. We do not know the platform’s timing. Until then, announcements go out on the website and the newsletter. We will update you at each step and will not predict the outcome.

Confidential material

PR firms often work under confidentiality terms. Send only what the appeal needs: the username, the notice and its date. You do not need to share campaign plans or internal documents. Read client confidentiality and NDAs before you send anything sensitive.

After the decision

Brief the client, then tighten the account: second admin, authenticator app, recovery email owned by the company. See the prevention checklist.

Why would a PR firm use white label recovery?+

PR firms need a quick way to respond to a disabled or hacked client account. White label lets them answer under their own name without building the skill themselves.

Should the public statement mention the recovery partner?+

No. Keep the statement to what the client’s audience needs to know, and keep partner details private.
