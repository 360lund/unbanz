Instagram account disabled, but your ads are still spending?

Ads usually live in an ad account, not in the Instagram profile. That means you can often see and stop them from Ads Manager.

Check and pause

  1. Log in to Ads Manager or Meta Business Suite from an admin profile that still works.
  2. Filter campaigns by the Instagram account they use as identity.
  3. Pause active campaigns and boosted posts that point to the disabled account.
  4. Download invoices from Billing and payments for your records.
  5. Check Account Quality to see whether the ad account itself is also restricted.

If you cannot reach Ads Manager

If the only admin was the disabled account, or the linked Facebook profile is restricted too, you may not be able to pause anything. Contact your bank about the card only if charges look unauthorised, and be aware that chargebacks on Meta billing can lead to the ad account being restricted. See unpaid balance on a disabled ad account.

Questions people also ask (2026)

Is there anywhere to go after a final no?

In the EU, Appeals Centre Europe can review Facebook and Instagram account decisions. It is free and non-binding, and Meta’s internal appeal must be finished first. Outside the EU there is no equivalent body for account decisions.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

Good to know.

Do ads stop on their own when Instagram is disabled?+

Not always. Ads can keep running with a Facebook Page identity, and some placements may still deliver. Check rather than assume.

Will pausing ads hurt my appeal?+

No. Pausing spend is a billing decision and does not affect the review of the Instagram account.

Can someone else on the business portfolio pause them?+

Yes. Any admin with the right access in the business portfolio can pause campaigns.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
