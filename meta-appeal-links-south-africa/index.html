The Meta routes

The Meta routes themselves do not change by country: appeal from the notice you see at login (or in Accounts Center), use www.instagram.com/hacked/ or www.facebook.com/hacked for a takeover, and ask for ad account reviews in business.facebook.com/accountquality. The full list is in the Meta appeal links directory. What follows is only what differs here.

Where to report in South Africa

LinkWhat it is for
Information Regulator
inforegulator.org.za/		Complaints under POPIA.
SAPS
www.saps.gov.za/		Report hacking, fraud and extortion under the Cybercrimes Act.
SABRIC
www.sabric.co.za/		Banking crime guidance if money left your account.
Film and Publication Board
www.fpb.org.za/		Complaints about harmful and prohibited content.

POPIA

POPIA gives you a right to ask whether a responsible party holds your personal information and to request corrections. If a request goes nowhere, complain to the Information Regulator.

Language

Appeal in English unless another official language is clearer for you.
