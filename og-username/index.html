What makes a handle OG

Three or four letters, a single word, your first name, or a clean brand name with no numbers or underscores. The kind of handle that makes a profile look established.

How we get them

We check who holds the handle and whether it can be claimed, tell you the price, then run the claim on your existing profile. No account trading, just the handle.

Can you get a three-letter handle?+

Sometimes. Most three-letter handles sit on active accounts, and those can’t be claimed. The ones on dead or squatted profiles sometimes can. Send a few and you’ll hear which are realistic.

What if the OG handle I want is in use?+

If the owner still posts or logs in, it isn’t claimable. You’ll be told straight away, with close alternatives if there are any.

Can Unbanz get me an OG Instagram username?+

Often, yes, when it’s on an inactive or squatted account. Send the handle and we’ll tell you if it’s doable.

Is an OG username claim the same as buying an account?+

It’s better: we claim the handle onto your own profile, so you keep everything you’ve built.

How much does an OG username claim cost?+

It depends on the handle. You get a price before we start.
