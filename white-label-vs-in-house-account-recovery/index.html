White label vs in-house account recovery for agencies.

Doing recovery yourself sounds simple until the third lockout. Here is what in-house actually asks of an agency.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

In-house account recovery means your own staff handle every appeal. It gives control but needs training, time and upkeep, because platform menus and rules change. White label gives you one trained specialist behind your brand. In-house suits steady volume and a dedicated person. White label suits occasional lockouts.

Time

Appeals take focus. Every hour a strategist spends on one is an hour not billed elsewhere.

Upkeep

Menus, forms and rules change. Someone has to keep checking them.

Control

In-house gives you full control. White label gives you control of the client, not the platform work.

What in-house requires

  • One named person who owns appeals and knows the routes for each platform and problem type.
  • A routine for collecting details from clients, so appeals are complete the first time.
  • Time to follow up, and to repeat the work if a request is rejected.
  • A way to stay current when the platform changes its forms or wording.
  • A calm voice for clients who are frightened about losing their audience.

If you already have this, you may not need a partner. If you do not, writing the routine is the first step. Use the SOP template either way.

Costs that people forget

  • The second attempt. A rejected request often needs a different approach, not a copy of the first.
  • The client call. Clients want to talk, and a lockout is an emotional topic for a creator or founder.
  • The documentation. If your person leaves, the knowledge leaves with them.
  • The context switch. Your best strategist is probably not your best appeal writer.

The hybrid that works for some agencies

Handle simple, well-understood problems in-house, such as walking a client through instagram.com/hacked. Send disabled accounts, restricted ad accounts and handle claims to a partner. Write down which is which in your SOP so everyone on the team makes the same call.

When to switch from in-house to white label

  • Appeals pile up on one person.
  • Your clients start asking and nobody wants to own it.
  • You notice you are guessing at the right route.
  • You would rather spend the hour on client strategy.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Should my agency build account recovery in-house?+

Only if lockouts are frequent and one person can own the routes and keep up with changes. For occasional lockouts a white label partner is usually less work.

What does in-house account recovery cost an agency?+

The cost is mostly time: training, keeping up with platform changes and repeating work after rejections. It also ties up your most senior people.

Can I use white label for hard accounts only?+

Yes. Some agencies handle simple lockouts themselves and send disabled accounts, ad accounts and handle claims to a partner.

Do I lose control with white label?+

You keep control of the client, the terms and the message. The platform work is done by the partner, and the platform decides the result.

How do I decide?+

Count how many lockouts you saw in the last twelve months, then ask who on your team would own them. If the answer is nobody, white label is the safer choice.
