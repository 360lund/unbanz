The lockouts social media agencies actually meet

  • A client Instagram account disabled after a scheduled post or a sudden wave of follows.
  • A Facebook Page unpublished or restricted, which stops all scheduled content.
  • A hacked account where the email and number were changed and the agency lost its own access.
  • A login blocked by a code sent to a phone number the client no longer has.
  • A brand handle that the client wants and a stranger holds.

Each one has a different official route. Instagram, Facebook and hacked account pages explain the routes.

How the handoff fits your day

  1. Pause scheduled posts that could make things worse, and note what was live when the notice appeared.
  2. Ask the client for the notice screenshot and the date, and for their go-ahead for you to act.
  3. Send the details to Unbanz. You do not have to name the client or the company.
  4. Tell the client, in your words, that a specialist route has been started and that the platform decides.
  5. Update the client with the status templates so you are not writing from scratch.

What to keep on file for every client

  • Who owns the account and who the admins are.
  • The login email and whether the client still has access to it.
  • The phone number on the account and who holds it.
  • Whether two-factor authentication is on, and where the backup codes are.

This is the same list as the lockout prevention checklist. Collect it at onboarding and the first lockout becomes shorter.

Fitting it into your offer

Social media agencies usually package this in one of three ways: as an included safety net in a retainer, as an add-on called something like account protection, or as a pay-per-incident service. None of these needs you to promise a result. See pricing models for reselling recovery.

What to say to the client

Short message for the first reply

I have seen the notice and I am starting the recovery process now. I will need a screenshot of the notice and the date it appeared. Please do not try random logins or share any codes. I will update you as soon as there is movement. The platform makes the final decision, so I will not promise an outcome, but I will keep you informed at each step.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Why should a social media agency offer account recovery?+

Clients call the agency first when an account stops working. Having a clear service to offer keeps the client with you and protects the content calendar.

Do I have to hire a recovery specialist?+

No. With white label, Unbanz does the platform work behind the scenes while you stay the contact. You handle intake and client updates.

What should I collect from a client before contacting Unbanz?+

The username or profile link, an unedited screenshot of the notice and its date, the email and number on the account and the client’s go-ahead. Never ask for a password.

Can I include recovery in my retainer?+

Yes, usually as a defined add-on. Do not promise a result, because the platform decides every appeal.

What if the client’s account cannot be recovered?+

Tell them plainly, and offer the next best step, such as setting up a new account correctly. The platform’s decision is final for that request.
