Lost the Facebook email and phone at the same time?

When both contact methods are gone, Facebook leans on devices it already trusts and on identity checks.

Updated 4 November 2026

If you lost both the email and the phone on your Facebook account at once, start login help on a phone or computer you have used before. Facebook may offer an ID upload, a video selfie or trusted contacts. Do not create a new profile on the same device while you try. Meta makes the final decision on recovery.

Usual device

Start from a phone Facebook already knows.

Login help

Follow the on-screen options once.

ID if asked

A clear document that matches the profile.

Steps when both contacts are gone

  1. On a familiar device, open Facebook and choose “Forgotten password” or the login help link.
  2. When it asks for email or phone, say you no longer have them and continue through the options shown.
  3. Complete any ID or selfie check with a document that matches the profile name.
  4. If trusted contacts are offered and you still have people set up, use that path once.
  5. If someone else changed the email and phone, treat it as a hack and also use facebook.com/hacked.

Why both can disappear together

A hacker often changes email and phone in one session. A number that was recycled by a carrier, or an email you deleted years ago, can leave you in the same place even without a hack. See lost access to the email or phone.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Questions people also ask (2026)

What if a hacker changed the email and phone?

Check the old inbox for a security email from Meta about the change. It can include a link to undo it. On Instagram, use “My login details were changed” in the login help, or instagram.com/hacked.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

What if I no longer have the email or phone on the account?

Start from facebook.com/login/identify or the Instagram “Forgot password” screen on a phone or computer you have logged in from before. Meta may offer other options there, such as a code to a different contact, a video selfie or an ID photo. Which options appear depends on the account.

Good to know.

Can I call Facebook?+

Facebook does not offer phone support for disabled accounts. Numbers online that claim to be Facebook support are often scams.

Can a friend reset it for me?+

Only if they are set as a trusted contact and Facebook offers that option. Otherwise the owner has to complete the checks.

Should I make a new Facebook account?+

Not while recovery is open. A new profile on the same device can be linked to the old one.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
