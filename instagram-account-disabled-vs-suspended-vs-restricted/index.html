Disabled

A disabled account is offline. According to Instagram’s Help Center, accounts that do not follow Meta’s Community Standards may be disabled, and you see a message about it when you try to log in. If you think it was a mistake, you may be able to ask for a review by logging in and following the on-screen steps. There is no separate unban email address. Related: Instagram disabled account appeal.

Suspended

A suspended account shows a similar notice, often with a date. The date is the deadline to use the Disagree or Appeal option, which is usually 180 days. Use it once and keep the screenshots. Related: 180-day suspension.

Restricted or action blocked

A restriction limits a feature, such as messaging new people or going live. You can still log in. Account Status shows the feature and the end date. These limits are usually temporary. Related: Action blocked message.

Hacked

A hacked account often shows no enforcement notice at all. Look for an email from Instagram about a changed email, phone number or password and use the undo link in it. Then use instagram.com/hacked on a device you have used before. Related: Hacked Instagram recovery.

Deleted or deactivated

Deleted and deactivated are not the same. A deactivated account is hidden by choice and Instagram says you can bring it back by logging in. A deleted account cannot be restored. Related: Is my account deleted or disabled?.

Questions people also ask (2026)

Is there anywhere to go after a final no?

In the EU, Appeals Centre Europe can review Facebook and Instagram account decisions. It is free and non-binding, and Meta’s internal appeal must be finished first. Outside the EU there is no equivalent body for account decisions.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

Good to know.

What is the difference between a disabled and a suspended Instagram account?+

Both mean account-level action and the account is offline. A suspended notice usually carries a deadline to disagree or appeal, often 180 days. A disabled notice tells you the account was disabled and may offer a review option.

Is a restricted Instagram account the same as a ban?+

No. A restricted account still logs in. A feature such as messaging or live video is limited for a set time. Account Status shows which feature and when it ends.

How do I tell if my Instagram was hacked or disabled?+

A disabled account shows a notice when you log in. A hacked account often shows none, and the password simply stops working or the email on the account has changed. Use instagram.com/hacked for the second situation.

Can a deleted Instagram account be restored?+

Instagram says a deleted account cannot be restored. A deactivated account is different: it is hidden and you can bring it back by logging in.
