What the service covers

  • Accounts banned or permanently banned after a Community Guidelines decision.
  • Hacked accounts where the email, phone or password was changed by someone else.
  • Accounts whose in-app appeal was already rejected.
  • Business and creator accounts your agency runs content or ads for.

The direct page for account owners is TikTok account help. This page is the agency version.

TikTok notices and what to collect

What the client seesWhat it usually meansWhat to collect

The handoff in four steps

  1. Your client sends you the screenshot, the @username and the date.
  2. You email the case to Unbanz with your own reference. No client name is needed.
  3. We confirm whether we can take it; you pay the fee upfront.
  4. We work the case and send you unbranded updates to pass on in your own words.

Partner terms

  • Each case is paid upfront, and each case that fails gets a 100% refund.
  • Partners who send cases regularly or in batches get volume pricing, agreed in writing before the work starts. We do not publish those numbers.
  • A dedicated contact for your agency at team@unbanz.com, with your own case references.
  • Private-label updates: plain reports with no Unbanz name or logo, ready to forward or paste into your own template.
  • Unbanz never contacts your client and never mentions Unbanz to them.

What is never part of it

  • No passwords or login codes, from you or your client.
  • No buying, selling or renting accounts.
  • No fake or mass reporting.
  • No promised outcome: the platform makes the final decision.

Start

Email team@unbanz.com with your agency name, the platforms your clients use, which TikTok cases you see most and roughly how many cases you expect each month. We reply with written terms before the first case.
