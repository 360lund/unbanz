Questions people also ask (2026)

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

Hacked and then disabled: which comes first?

Start with the hacked route (instagram.com/hacked or facebook.com/hacked), not the normal appeal, so the record shows the account was taken. Secure the linked email and phone first, then appeal the disable if a button appears. Report the scam where you live (table below).

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.

Is there anywhere to go after a final no?

In the EU, Appeals Centre Europe can review Facebook and Instagram account decisions. It is free and non-binding, and Meta’s internal appeal must be finished first. Outside the EU there is no equivalent body for account decisions.
