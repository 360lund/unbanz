Before you message.

Do you recover banned or disabled accounts?+

Yes. We recover banned, suspended and disabled Instagram and Facebook accounts, plus restricted Meta ad accounts. Send the notice and we review your case.

How much does an unban cost?+

You get a fixed quote after we review your account. You approve the price before we start.

How long does it take?+

Most cases take 3–14 days after we accept them. We confirm your timeline in the chat.

What should I send?+

Your username, a screenshot of the notice and when it happened. For ad accounts, add the account ID. Never send passwords or login codes.

Do you handle ban requests?+

Yes, it is one of our core services. Send the account link and what happened, and we quote and handle the reports for you.

Is recovery guaranteed?+

Recovery is guaranteed once we accept your case. We review every account first and only take cases we can win.
