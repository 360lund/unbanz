QUICK ANSWERS

Before you message.

Can you help if my account is banned or disabled?+

We review banned, suspended and disabled Instagram and Facebook accounts, plus restricted Meta ad accounts. Send the notice you received so we can check your case.

How much does an unban cost?+

The initial case check is free. We quote after reviewing the account and the reason for the restriction. You agree to the price before work starts.

How long does it take?+

Our estimated turnaround is 3–14 days after we accept the case and receive the details. We confirm the estimate in your chat; some cases take longer.

What should I send?+

Your username or account link, a screenshot of the ban notice and when it happened. For ad accounts, include the account ID and the restriction notice. Never send passwords or login codes.

Can you get a fake account banned?+

Send us the account link and what happened. We’ll review the request and explain what we can do.

Is recovery guaranteed?+

We check whether we can take the case first. The platform makes the final decision. We agree the scope, price and any refund terms with you before starting.
