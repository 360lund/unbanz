Local questions, answered (2026)

Why do ad accounts in France get restricted?

The usual triggers are a declined or disputed card, a billing country or currency change, a spend spike, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new country, or an admin whose own profile is restricted.

What documents pass business verification in France?

Use a recent Kbis extract or a SIREN/INSEE notice, with the legal name and address matching the business portfolio exactly.

Where do I request the review?

Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/accountquality). Select the ad account and use Request review if shown. Meta does not state a review time.

Is there an outside appeal in France?

Once Meta’s own appeal is done, EU users can go to Appeals Centre Europe, which has reviewed suspensions since 2025. It is free and non-binding. Arcom is the French Digital Services Coordinator but does not decide individual appeals.
