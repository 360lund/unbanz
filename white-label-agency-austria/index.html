Details that matter in Austria

These are the points that differ for clients based in Austria.

  • Notice language: Notices may appear in German. The options are the same in German and English.
  • Phone number: An Austrian mobile written 0664 1234567 is entered as +43 664 1234567, without the zero.
  • Business details: Have the company name, website and registration number ready if the platform asks for proof the business is real.
  • If fraud or blackmail is involved: The police are on 133, and the Federal Criminal Police Office takes cybercrime tips at against-cybercrime@bmi.gv.at. Watchlist Internet (watchlist-internet.at) lists current fake shops and phishing campaigns. That runs alongside the platform appeal.
  • Platform rules: Under the Digital Services Act, KommAustria supervises platforms in the country. It does not decide an individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe accepts some account decisions from EU users, but not hacked accounts, and Instagram’s own review comes first.
  • Client data: The Datenschutzbehörde is the Austrian data protection authority. Send us only what the appeal needs.
  • Advertising rules: The Österreichischer Werberat is the Austrian advertising self-regulatory body. Meta’s own ad policies still apply on top.
