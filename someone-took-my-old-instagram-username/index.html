Someone took my old Instagram username. Can I get it back?

Sometimes. If it was taken through a hack, recovering the account usually brings the handle back; if someone grabbed it after you renamed or deleted, you need a reason Instagram accepts, like impersonation, your trademark or a dead squatter account.

Short answer

How you lost it decides what’s possible. A hacker changing your handle is a recovery problem. Someone picking it up fairly after you let it go is harder, but not always hopeless.

If you were hacked

Look for Instagram’s email about the username change and use the “secure your account” link. Then go to instagram.com/hacked. Once you’re back in, rename to the original if it’s still free. Our hacked account guide covers the steps.

If you renamed or deleted and someone grabbed it

  • They’re pretending to be you. That’s impersonation, and it can be reported.
  • It matches your registered trademark. Meta’s IP process can apply.
  • They grabbed it and left it dead. Dead or squatting accounts can sometimes be claimed.
  • An unrelated active person uses it fairly. Usually there’s nothing to do. We’ll say so.
