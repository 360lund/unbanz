What a business portfolio is

Business portfolio

The Meta tool, formerly Business Manager, that holds a business’s Pages, ad accounts, pixels and the people who have access. See business manager disabled for the direct service page.

Because everything hangs from one place, the portfolio should belong to the client business, not to the agency. When the agency owns it, ownership disputes complicate any recovery.

Why portfolios get restricted

  • Business verification documents that do not match the business details.
  • Policy decisions on one asset that affect the whole portfolio.
  • A sudden change of admins or payment methods.
  • Links to another restricted portfolio or account.

The notice wording tells you which applies. Collect it first.

A word on who owns the portfolio

Some agencies create portfolios for clients and keep the owner role. That makes handover difficult and can create trouble if the agency relationship ends. The better approach is for the client business to own it and the agency to be a partner with the roles it needs. See red flags for agencies for related warning signs.
