Can a permanently banned Snapchat account be recovered?

Sometimes. If Snapchat offers an in-app appeal on the lock message, use it; accounts locked by mistake do get restored, but there’s no way to unlock a permanent lock yourself.

Short answer

A permanent lock means Snapchat decided you broke its Community Guidelines. You can’t just log in and unlock it. The official route is the appeal in the app or on Snapchat’s support pages, when it’s offered.

Why permanent locks happen

  • Third-party apps, mods or “ghost” clients.
  • Spam, harassment, impersonation or scams.
  • Sharing content that breaks the Guidelines.
  • A false report that was never corrected.

What to do

  1. Read the lock message and take a screenshot.
  2. Uninstall any modded or third-party Snapchat apps.
  3. Use the appeal option if you see one, with a short, honest explanation.
  4. Don’t open lots of new accounts. A device ban can follow.

Chances

Better if you were flagged by mistake or after a false report. Worse if you used third-party apps repeatedly or have a history of serious violations. We won’t take accounts involved in anything harmful.
