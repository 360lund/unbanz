Checks for Canada

Phone formats, where to report fraud and any appeal rights differ by country. The sections below cover Canada.

Official steps that come first

  1. If you see a suspended or disabled notice, log in and follow the prompts to disagree with the decision. Facebook gives most suspended accounts 180 days, but trust the date on your own screen.
  2. If you cannot log in because details were changed, go to facebook.com/hacked on a device and browser you have used before.
  3. Search for your account at facebook.com/login/identify and choose the option for no longer having access to your email or phone.
  4. Check your email, including spam, for a Facebook message about the change and use its undo link first.
  5. If Facebook asks for ID or a video selfie, use your real details and follow the instructions exactly.

Checks that matter in Canada

  • Marketplace and group scams: a buyer who asks to “verify” you by sending a code, or a courier link, is the usual way in. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501) lists current scams and takes reports.
  • Pages, groups and ads: if the profile is disabled, a Page where you are the only admin can be lost and linked ad accounts can stop working. Make sure a trusted person has full control. For ad account problems see ad accounts disabled in Canada.
  • Your Canadian number: it uses +1 like the USA, so enter all ten digits. Ask your carrier to lock the number if the SIM is lost or a port was attempted.
  • News on Facebook: since August 2023 Meta has blocked news content for Canadian users. If your Page posts news links, they will not show to Canadians even when your account is fine.
  • French notices: a Facebook notice may appear in French. The options are the same. Screenshot it as shown and send it to us in English or French.

What not to do

  • Don’t pay or message anyone from a DM who says they can “open” your account.
  • Don’t share your password or login codes with anyone, including us.
  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Facebook, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

How do I recover a hacked Facebook account in Canada?+

Go to facebook.com/hacked on a device and browser you have used before, or search for your account at facebook.com/login/identify and choose the option for no longer having access. Check the email on the account first for a message from Facebook with a link to undo a change.

What happens if I miss the 180 days on a Facebook suspension?+

Facebook says the account is then permanently disabled and you cannot request another review. The time to appeal can differ, so check the date on your own notice and act early.

Where do I report Facebook fraud in Canada?+

Report to Facebook first, then to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and your local police. If money moved, call your bank straight away.
