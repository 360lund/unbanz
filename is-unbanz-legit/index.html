How to spot account recovery scams: what Unbanz never asks for

  • Passwords or login codes. Unbanz never asks for your password, 2FA codes or backup codes. Anyone who does is not us.
  • Guaranteed results. Nobody can promise Meta will switch an account back on. Unbanz is independent, not affiliated with Meta, never guarantees an outcome, and refunds 100% if a case fails.
  • Recovery offers in DMs or comments. Unbanz does not cold-message people offering recovery. Strangers who reach out first after you post about a ban are a common scam.
  • Payment before terms. Fees from $1,000, paid by bank transfer, with a 100% refund if the case fails. Pressure to pay first, or by gift card, transfer or crypto, is a warning sign.
  • Contacts you can check. The only Unbanz contacts are unbanz.com and team@unbanz.com. Anything else is not us.
