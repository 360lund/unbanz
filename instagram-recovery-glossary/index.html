Instagram and Meta recovery glossary.

Short definitions of the words that appear on Instagram and Meta notices, each linked to the guide that goes deeper.

This glossary defines the terms used on Instagram and Meta account notices: disabled, suspended, restricted, strike, appeal, video selfie, hacked, username claim, trademark report, Account Quality and ad account restriction. Each entry is one or two sentences and links to the guide for that topic.

Notice terms

Disabled, suspended, restricted, strike and appeal.

Access and identity

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Terms

Disabled account
An account Instagram has taken offline for not following its rules. A notice appears at login and the profile is not visible to others. Read more
Suspended account
An account under account-level action with a deadline, usually 180 days, to disagree or appeal. Read more
Restricted account or feature limit
An account that still logs in but cannot use a feature, such as messaging new people, for a set period. Read more
Action blocked
The message Instagram shows when it limits an action such as following or commenting. Read more
Account Status
The Instagram settings screen that lists removed content, feature limits and recommendation eligibility. Read more
Strike
A recorded violation. More strikes lead to longer restrictions and, after repeated violations, possible removal. Read more
Appeal or request for review
The button or form on a notice that asks Meta to look at a decision again. Read more
Video selfie
An identity check where Instagram asks you to record a short video of your face, turning your head in different directions. Read more
Hacked account
An account where someone else changed the password, email or phone number. Recovery starts at instagram.com/hacked. Read more
Memorialized account
An account kept as a tribute after a person has died. Read more
Username claim
Getting a specific handle onto your own profile through a platform process, such as a trademark or impersonation report. Read more
Trademark (IP) report
A report to the platform that a handle infringes a registered trademark. Read more
Impersonation report
A report that an account is pretending to be you or your brand. Read more
Squatted username
A handle held by an account that is parked or unused, often to resell it. Read more
Business portfolio
Meta’s container for a business’s Pages, ad accounts and Instagram accounts. Read more
Account Quality
The Meta page that shows restrictions, reasons and review options for business assets. Read more
Ad account restriction
A limit on advertising placed on a business portfolio, ad account, Page or personal profile. Read more
Meta Verified
Meta’s paid subscription that includes access to account support for eligible profiles. Read more

What does disabled mean on Instagram?+

The account is offline for not following Instagram’s rules. A notice appears at login and the profile is not visible to others.

What is an Instagram strike?+

A recorded violation. More strikes lead to longer restrictions, and after repeated violations an account may be removed.

What is a username claim?+

Getting a specific handle onto your own profile through a platform process such as a trademark or impersonation report.

What is Account Quality?+

The Meta page that lists restrictions on business assets, the reason shown and, where available, a request review button.

Can Unbanz recover my account?+
