Local reporting in USA

If money was taken, ads were run on your card or you are being threatened, report to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and the FBI’s IC3 if money was taken and tell your bank about any card charges.

Tips that help

  • Use the phone or computer you normally log in from.
  • Use the same network you normally use, not a VPN.
  • Have a government ID ready in the exact name on the profile.
  • Write in English, briefly and factually.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Local questions, answered (2026)

Which ID does Facebook accept in the US?

Meta’s list changes, but a US driver’s license, state ID or passport is the usual choice. Make sure the name matches the profile. Often Facebook asks for a video selfie instead of a document.

Disabled and Facebook shows no ID option?

Do the video selfie if offered, once, in good light. If nothing is offered, screenshot the screen with the date and avoid opening new accounts while you wait.

Hacked and disabled in the US: who do I tell?

Use facebook.com/hacked first, then appeal the disable. Report the hack or scam to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov and the FBI’s IC3 at ic3.gov.

Where do I check status in Accounts Center?

Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status. If you cannot log in, the notice on the login screen has the review button.

Is there an outside appeal in the US?

There is no outside appeal body for Meta account decisions in the US, so Meta’s own review is the only formal route.

The Facebook appeal still starts from the notice you see when you log in: choose the option to disagree or request a review, and complete any check Meta asks for, such as a video selfie or an ID upload. Meta states that you generally have 180 days to appeal a suspension. If the screen offers no appeal, see what to do with no appeal button and the Facebook appeal links directory.

I emailed my appeal letter to Meta. Did it arrive?

Meta does not process account appeals by email. Paste a short version of your letter into the appeal field shown in the app, Account Status or Business Support Home.
