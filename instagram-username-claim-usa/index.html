Instagram username claims in the US: quick answers (2026)

Can I get an inactive Instagram username in the US?

Sometimes. Instagram has no public form to request a username held by an inactive account. Inactive handles are not released on a schedule.

Is there an official route?

Yes, if you own a registered trademark for the name: Instagram’s trademark report, in the Help Center under intellectual property. Without a trademark there is no official request form.

What trademark counts in the US?

A registered US trademark from the USPTO (uspto.gov/) is the strongest proof for a trademark-based username request.

Should I buy a username from a seller?

No. Instagram’s terms do not allow buying or selling usernames, and sellers often scam or hand over an account that gets taken back.

Do I need to give my password?

No. Your password stays with you. The username is moved to an account you control.

Is it legal?

Using Instagram’s own processes is. Hacking, buying or pressuring the holder is not something we do.
