How to choose a white label recovery partner.

Handing client accounts to a partner is a trust decision. This checklist helps you test one before you commit.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Choose a white label recovery partner by testing five things: the official routes it uses, how honest it is about outcomes, how it protects your clients’ details, how clear its terms are and whether it stays behind your brand. Avoid anyone who promises results, asks for passwords or claims inside contacts.

Method

Official routes, explained plainly.

Honesty

No promised results.

Discretion

Stays behind your brand.

TermsClear and in writingVague or verbal only

Questions to ask

  1. What do you do when the platform says no?
  2. What do you need from my client, and what never?
  3. Do you ever contact my client?
  4. What happens with the details I send?
  5. What are your terms, and can I have them in writing?

A small test before you commit

Start with a single request, ideally a straightforward one, and judge how clear the communication is. Reply times matter less than clarity. A provider who tells you plainly when a request has no honest route is worth more than one who says yes to everything.

Good to know.

What matters most when choosing a partner?+

Honesty about outcomes and respect for your client’s details. Everything else is secondary.

Should the partner ever contact my client?+

In a white label arrangement, no.

How should I test a new partner?+

Start with one straightforward request and judge the clarity of communication.

Is a cheaper partner better?+

Price is one input. Clear terms and honest answers matter more.

Can I ask for references?+

You can ask. Be wary of anyone who offers ratings or reviews you cannot verify.
