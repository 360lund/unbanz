Facebook hacked and the email changed?

When the email changes, the hacker gets your reset links. The old email may still hold a way back.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

When a hacker changes the email on your Facebook account, the reset links go to their address. Check your original inbox for a message about the change, because it often includes a link to undo it. If that link has expired or is missing, use the hacked-account flow from the login screen.

Search your old inbox

Look for Facebook messages dated around the hack.

Use the undo link soon

It can expire.

Do not keep requesting resets

They go to the hacker’s address.

What to look for

  • A message that your email was changed.
  • A message that a new device logged in.
  • A message about a changed password.
  • A message about a new two-factor method.

Check spam, promotions and trash too.

Steps

  1. Open the undo link in the original email on a device you trust.
  2. If there is none, use the hacked-account help flow from the login screen.
  3. Complete the identity step with a video selfie or ID.
  4. Change your passwords and log out other sessions after access returns.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Staying safe
