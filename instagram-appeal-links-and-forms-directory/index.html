Instagram appeal links and forms.

Every official Instagram link for a disabled, suspended, restricted or hacked account, with a plain note on what each one is for.

Updated 5 November 2026

Instagram has no standalone appeal form for disabled or suspended accounts. The appeal starts from the message you see when you log in, or from Account Status if you can still use the app. A hacked account starts at instagram.com/hacked. Instagram has no customer service phone number or email for appeals. Old contact-form links shared online now usually open a help page or Meta’s AI support assistant. Meta makes the final decision.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone for a “private” or “direct” appeal link. There is none.

If the notice says “account integrity”

Account integrity is a broad label Meta uses when it believes an account was used in a way that breaks its rules, often linked to spam-like activity, automation, bought followers or links to other removed accounts. The appeal still starts from the notice. Write a short, factual explanation: who uses the account, what it is for, and anything unusual that happened, such as a login from a new place or a third-party app you have since removed. See account integrity guide.

Can I email Instagram?

There is no public email address that accepts appeals for disabled Instagram accounts. Messages sent to addresses found on forums are usually not read, and some of those addresses belong to scammers. If you write an appeal letter, paste it into the appeal box in the app. Unbanz is an independent service, not Instagram, so an appeal letter sent to us does not reach Meta.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is there an Instagram appeal form for disabled accounts?+

Not a standalone one. The appeal starts from the message you see when you log in to the disabled account.

Does Instagram have a support phone number or email?+

No. Instagram does not offer phone or email support for appeals. Contacts claiming to be Instagram support are often scams.

What if the disabled screen has no appeal button?+

Try logging in on the app and on a browser on your usual device. If there is still no button, the window may have closed; see our no appeal button guide.

Do I need to give anyone my password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you.
