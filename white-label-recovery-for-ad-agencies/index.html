Where the restriction really sits

Ad agencies often start by assuming the ad account is the problem. In practice the restriction can sit on the ad account, the business portfolio, a Facebook Page or a personal profile that holds a role. Meta’s Account Quality area shows what is limited. Collect a screenshot of it before you contact anyone. The detail is in white label Facebook ad account recovery.

What stays with the agency

  • The client conversation, including the hard one about paused spend.
  • The decision on whether to move budget to other accounts that are in good standing.
  • Any change to creative or landing pages the notice points to.
  • The report to the client.

A recovery request is not a place for shortcuts. Do not open new accounts to dodge a restriction, and do not use another business’s assets to keep spending. That kind of workaround tends to make things worse, and it is not something Unbanz does.

A handoff routine for the media buying team

  1. Pause what is not already stopped and keep a record of live campaigns.
  2. Open Account Quality and screenshot every notice.
  3. Send the details to Unbanz with the date and the business details.
  4. Keep the client updated with plain facts.
  5. When there is a decision, update the plan for budgets.

Good to know.

Can an ad agency offer ad account recovery under its own name?+

Yes. With white label, Unbanz prepares the appeal behind the scenes while the agency stays the contact and keeps the client relationship.

What should an ad agency collect first?+

A screenshot of the Account Quality notices, the date, the admins on the business portfolio, and the client’s go-ahead. Never a password.

Will recovery restore my campaigns?+

Nobody can promise that. Meta decides the appeal, and campaigns only run again if the restriction is lifted.

Should I open a new ad account while I wait?+

Do not use new accounts to dodge a restriction. It can make matters worse. Talk to the client about moving budget only to accounts in good standing.

Does the client see Unbanz?+

No. Unbanz works for the agency behind the scenes and never contacts the client.
