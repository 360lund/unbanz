What is Unbanz?

Unbanz (unbanz.com) is a service that recovers banned Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat accounts, handles account bans/takedowns and username claims.

Unbanz is a brand name, not a typo for “unban”. We also go by Unbanz HQ, and you’ll find us as @unbanzhq on Instagram and @unbanzhq on X.

What we do

  • Unbans. Banned, disabled, suspended or hacked Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat accounts.
  • Bans. Fake accounts, impersonators, scammers and harassers taken down on Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat.
  • Username claims. Inactive or taken handles on Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat.
  • Ad accounts. Restricted Meta ad accounts and Business Portfolios.

Our focus is Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat and Meta ad accounts.

How it works

Send the username and a screenshot of the notice on WhatsApp or email. You get a price before we start. Say go and we get it done. The usual estimate is 3–14 days from the day we start. Full process.

Can you trust us?

Yes. We’re an independent service, not affiliated with Meta. You talk to us directly, you know the price before we start, and your password and login codes stay with you. Real results are on our proof of work page. More on whether Unbanz is legit.

The guarantee

Guaranteed. If we take it on, we get it done. Payment and refund terms are agreed with you before we start. See payment and refunds.

Where we work

Fully remote, in English, over WhatsApp or email. Clients in the USA, the UK, the UAE, Canada, Australia, Europe and the Middle East.
