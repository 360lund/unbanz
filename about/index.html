The official routes we use

Every request goes through the platform’s own channels. Unbans go through the official appeal and review request processes. Fake, impersonator and scam accounts are reported through the platforms’ own reporting tools. Username claims use the platform’s own processes, matched to the handle: a trademark report, an impersonation report, or the route for an inactive account. The platform makes the final decision on every request.
