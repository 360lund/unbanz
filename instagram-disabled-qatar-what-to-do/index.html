Read the notice first

The notice names the rule Instagram says was broken and usually shows how long you have to disagree. Write both down. A suspension with an appeal button and a final “disabled” decision without one need different next steps; see disabled vs suspended vs restricted.

Appealing from Qatar

  1. Appeal from the phone you normally use, ideally on your usual connection rather than a VPN.
  2. Write in Arabic or English, plainly: who you are, what the account is for, and why the decision looks wrong.
  3. If asked for ID, use a government document where the name matches the account owner.
  4. If the linked Facebook profile is restricted in Accounts Center, deal with that too, as it can block the Instagram appeal.

If the account is your business

Tell customers on your website, WhatsApp Business or email how to reach you. Pause any ads tied to the account in Ads Manager. You can also ask Meta for a copy of the data it holds on you under Qatar’s personal data privacy law (Law 13 of 2016).

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Is there an Instagram office in Qatar I can visit?+

Meta does not handle individual account appeals in person. Appeals go through the app, the notice and Accounts Center.

Does using a VPN cause suspensions?+

Logging in from unusual locations can trigger security checks. It is better to appeal from your normal connection.

What if there is no appeal button?+

Then the decision may be final on Instagram’s side. Check whether you have any other route, such as a data request, before giving up.

Do I need to share my Instagram password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
