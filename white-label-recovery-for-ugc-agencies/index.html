A UGC agency touches dozens of accounts at once: brand profiles, creator profiles and ad access. Lockouts are a matter of when.

Who the client is, situation by situation

In UGC the person who owns the account may be the creator, not the brand. Before sending anything, work out who owns the account and who gives the go-ahead. The account owner must agree to the appeal, because the platform may need to contact them. Add that to your creator agreement: a short line that you may arrange recovery help on their behalf if their account is locked.

A routine for creator lockouts

  1. The creator tells you and sends a screenshot of the notice.
  2. You confirm what content was live for a brand and tell the brand a delay is possible.
  3. You send the username, the notice and the date to Unbanz.
  4. The creator completes any identity check the platform sends them.
  5. You report back to the brand without revealing private details.

What to put in your creator agreements

Ask a lawyer to word it. In plain terms, include who may act on a locked account, what information the creator agrees to share and that the platform makes the final decision. See what to put in your client terms for plain suggestions that are not legal advice.

Keeping creators safe

Remind creators never to share logins with the agency by chat. Use platform roles and partner access where available. The lockout prevention checklist covers it.

Good to know.

Can a UGC agency offer recovery to its creators?+

Yes. Under white label the agency is the contact for the creator and for the brand, and Unbanz handles the appeal behind the scenes.

Who has to give the go-ahead?+

The account owner, who is often the creator. The platform may contact them directly.

What if a brand profile is locked, not the creator’s?+

The same routine applies. Collect the notice, the date and the brand’s go-ahead, and send the details to Unbanz.

Can you claim a handle for a creator brand?+

Sometimes through official routes. No handle can be promised.

Do creators see Unbanz?+

No. Unbanz works for the agency and never contacts creators or brands.
