Instagram account recovery in Portugal.

Instagram account hacked or disabled and you’re in Portugal? Here are the official steps, how to file an electronic complaint, and how Unbanz helps you appeal through Meta’s official process.

Short answer

Instagram account recovery in Portugal starts with Instagram’s own routes: request a review on a disabled notice, or use instagram.com/hacked if someone took over the account. Crime reports can be filed online through Queixa Eletrónica. Unbanz helps you appeal through Meta’s official process; Meta makes the final decision.

Official steps that come first

  1. If a disabled or suspended notice appears, tap it and request a review once. Go through every step it shows, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password changed, go to instagram.com/hacked on a phone or computer you have used with Instagram before.
  3. Search your inbox and spam for an Instagram email about the change. Use its undo link first.
  4. If the Portuguese number on the account is no longer working, replace the SIM first.
  5. Send one complete appeal and wait for the reply to the account’s email.

Checks that matter in Portugal

  • Your +351 number: Portuguese numbers have nine digits and no leading zero, so a mobile is +351 912 345 678. MEO, NOS, Vodafone and other operators replace a lost SIM after you identify yourself.
  • Online complaints: the Queixa Eletrónica portal (queixaselectronicas.mai.gov.pt) lets you file a complaint about certain crimes online. The PSP and GNR take reports in person, and the Polícia Judiciária investigates cybercrime. Call 112 in an emergency.
  • Cybersecurity centre: the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (cncs.gov.pt) publishes advice on phishing and account security.
  • Victim support: APAV, the Portuguese victim support association, gives free help to victims of crime, including online crime.
  • Consumer advice: DECO PROTESTE is a consumer association that gives guidance on online fraud. It does not restore accounts.

Your rights as an EU user

Under the Digital Services Act, Instagram and Facebook must offer an internal complaint route against many account decisions and tell users about out-of-court dispute bodies. In Portugal, ANACOM is the Digital Services Coordinator. It supervises platforms and does not decide your individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe is one certified body that accepts some content and account decisions from EU users. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app review. Use the in-app route first.

Portuguese speakers: the notice options are the same in Portuguese and English. We work in English over WhatsApp and email.

  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Instagram, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Portugal?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

Where do I report a hacked Instagram account in Portugal?+

To Instagram first, through instagram.com/hacked. If there was fraud, blackmail or stolen money, file a complaint through Queixa Eletrónica or at a police station and tell your bank at once.

Can I take an Instagram or Facebook decision to an independent body?+

If you live in the EU, the Digital Services Act lets you use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe for some decisions about content or an account. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app appeal.
