Instagram appeal form not working? What to try.

The appeal button is missing, the form won’t submit, or nothing happens after you send it. Here are the checks that fix most of it and what to do when they don’t.

What “the form” usually is

For a disabled account, Instagram puts the review inside the login flow. You log in, tap the notice and request a review. There’s no separate public form to find and fill in, so a link from a random site is often a scam. If you can still log in, Account Status and Support Requests in the app show what’s been flagged and where a review is offered.

Fixes to try, in order

  1. Update the Instagram app, or reinstall it, then try again.
  2. Try the same login on instagram.com in a browser, or the reverse if the browser fails.
  3. Turn off any VPN and use your normal network.
  4. Use a phone or computer you’ve logged in from before, with the date and time set automatically.
  5. Use the email, username or phone exactly as they are on the account.
  6. Finish every step in the flow, including any code or video selfie. Skipping one can stop the request.

If it still doesn’t work

  • No appeal button at all: see no appeal button.
  • Selfie won’t be accepted: record again in good light with your face fully in frame. See video selfie verification.
  • Submitted, no reply: check spam for mail from Instagram and reply on that same thread if there is one. Instagram doesn’t publish a response time.
  • Denied: see Instagram appeal denied.
  • Meta Verified subscribers: Instagram’s Help Center says you can contact Meta Verified support from the app, where it’s available.

What not to do

  • Don’t send the same appeal over and over. It can slow things down.
  • Don’t pay anyone who says they can reach “Instagram staff” or open a hidden form.
  • Don’t give your password or login codes to anyone, including us.

Good to know.

Why is the Instagram appeal form not working?+

Often it’s the app, the network or the device: an old app version, a VPN, or a device Instagram doesn’t recognise. Update the app, try instagram.com in a browser, and use a device you’ve logged in from before.

Is there a separate Instagram appeal form?+

For most disabled accounts, no. The review is part of the notice shown when you log in. Be wary of any outside link that claims to be a hidden appeal form.

I appealed and nothing happened. What now?+

Check the email on the account, including spam, and reply on that thread if there is one. Instagram doesn’t publish a response time, so send one complete appeal and wait rather than resubmitting.

Why is there no appeal button on my account?+

The notice can differ by account or decision, and some decisions don’t offer a review. Our no appeal button guide covers what to check next.

Can Unbanz help if my appeal won’t go through?+

Yes. We check what the notice says and handle the appeal through Meta’s official process. Meta makes the final decision, so we tell you upfront whether we think it’s doable.
