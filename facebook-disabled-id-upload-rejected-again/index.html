Facebook keeps rejecting your ID upload?

A rejected upload does not close the matter; it just asks for a better one. Fix the cause before sending it again.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Facebook rejects ID uploads that are blurry, cropped, expired or show a different name from the profile. Photograph the original document on a plain surface in good light, with all corners visible and no glare. If the rejection repeats, check the name and the document type against the list on the request screen before trying again.

Plain surface and good light

No glare, no shadows across the text.

All corners visible

Do not crop the edges.

Check the list

The request screen names the documents it accepts.

What leads to a rejection

  • Glare on the plastic or lamination.
  • A photo of a screen or a photocopy.
  • A different name from the profile name, with no explanation.
  • An expired or unsupported document type.

Each rejection should show a reason. Read it and fix that one thing.

How to retry

  1. Place the document on a dark, plain surface and use daylight.
  2. Take a straight-on photo, without flash.
  3. Check that the name and date of birth are readable.
  4. Upload once and wait for the answer.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
