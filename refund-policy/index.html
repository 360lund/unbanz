Getting a price

Send the account and the notice. We tell you if it’s doable and what it costs. Asking for a quote doesn’t book the service or commit you to anything.

How payment works

Before any work starts we confirm, in your chat or email, the scope, the price, the payment terms, the estimated timing and any refund terms. You only pay on the terms agreed there.

The guarantee

Guaranteed. If we take it on, we get it done. We tell you upfront if a job is doable, before you pay.

Refunds

Refund terms are agreed with you for each job before we start, alongside the price. That’s what applies to your job. If something isn’t right, email team@unbanz.com with your WhatsApp number or email thread and we’ll sort it under those terms.
