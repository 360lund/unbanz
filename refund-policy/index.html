Does Unbanz give refunds?+

Yes. Every service is paid upfront, and if the case fails you get a full 100% refund.

What if my username claim fails?+

You get a full 100% refund. If the username isn’t successfully claimed onto your profile, you get all of your money back.

Is Unbanz an official Meta partner?+

No. Unbanz is an independent service, not affiliated with Meta. We work through the standard reports and processes. What you pay for is knowing which route fits your case and getting it handled properly, with payment upfront and a 100% refund if it fails.

If the inactive account is removed, does the handle come to me?+

Not on its own. A freed handle can go back into the open pool, where anyone can take it. That’s why a claim only counts once the handle is on your profile. If it doesn’t get there, the refund policy covers you.

Can I pay by card?+

Yes. Card is how you pay. Card gives you your bank’s buyer protection. Either way, the price and refund policy are in writing before you pay.

Do I need escrow for a username claim?+

You don’t need one. The price and refund policy are in writing in your chat or email, and if the case fails, you get a 100% refund. Paying by card also keeps your bank’s buyer protection.

What counts as a failed username claim?+

A claim fails when the username isn’t successfully claimed onto your profile. If that happens, you get a full 100% refund. The handle, the price and the estimated timing are written in your chat or email before any work starts, so you both know exactly what counts.

Someone quoted me $3k to claim an inactive username. Is it a scam?+

The price alone doesn’t tell you. How they work does. Walk away if they ask for your password or login codes, offer to move someone else’s account over to you, or won’t put the handle, price and refund policy in writing. With Unbanz, the handle, price, estimated timing and refund policy are in writing before any work starts, your password stays with you, and if the case fails you get a full 100% refund. Nothing is charged for asking for a quote.

Will Unbanz send me an escrow website to pay through?+

No. Unbanz never sends escrow links. Look-alike escrow websites are a known trick in this space, so be careful with any link like that. You pay by card, and you can reach us at team@unbanz.com. Paying by card keeps your bank’s buyer protection, and any case that fails is refunded in full (100%).

Does Unbanz accept gift cards?+

No. Unbanz takes card payment. Nobody at Unbanz will ask for gift cards, deposits, “release fees” or investments. If someone asking for those says they’re Unbanz, email team@unbanz.com to check.

How do I pay Unbanz?+

By card. Nothing is charged for asking. Unbanz never asks for your password, login codes, gift cards or deposits. If someone says they’re Unbanz and asks for any other kind of payment, email team@unbanz.com to check.
