Snapchat account locked: how to unlock it or appeal

Snapchat locks accounts for a few different reasons, and the fix depends on which lock you got. Here's how to tell them apart and what actually works. Clients share video reviews of working with us.

Temporary or permanent?

Read the message in the app first. It tells you which lock you have.

What you seeWhat it means
Temporarily locked for a third-party app or pluginA timed lock. The message or your email says when it ends.
Temporarily locked for another reasonSnapchat says to wait 48 hours, then log in again.
Permanently locked for breaking the Community GuidelinesYou can't unlock it yourself. Your only official option is an in-app appeal, if one is offered.
Device bannedThe phone itself is blocked, often alongside a permanent lock.

Unlocking a temporary lock

Once the lock period is over, log in to the Snapchat Accounts Portal and tap Unlock. If the lock was for a third-party app, delete apps like tweaked Snapchat clients or plugins first and update Snapchat. Log in too early and you can end up locked again.

Appealing a permanent lock

Open Snapchat, try to log in and look for Appeal Decision on the pop-up. If it's there, tap it and explain clearly why the lock is wrong. If there's no button, Snapchat says that lock isn't eligible for an appeal.

Two things to know. You only get one appeal per lock, so make it count. And Snapchat says support tickets outside the app won't speed anything up.

If your device is banned

Snapchat can ban the device as well as the account. If your appeal is granted, the device ban goes too. Otherwise Snapchat generally won't lift it, even on a second-hand phone.

What not to do

  • Don't make a new account to dodge the lock. Snapchat bans people whose accounts were terminated from opening new ones.
  • Don't send your password to anyone. Snapchat staff never ask for it, and neither do we.
  • Don't spam support. Extra tickets don't change the outcome.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a Snapchat temporary lock?+

It depends on the reason. Third-party app locks show an end date. Other temporary locks usually need a 48-hour wait.

Can a permanently locked Snapchat account be unlocked?+

Only through the in-app appeal, if Snapchat offers one. You get one appeal per lock.

Will Snapchat support unlock it for me?+

No. Snapchat says its support team can't unlock permanently locked accounts.

Do you need my Snapchat password?+

No. Never share your password or login codes with anyone.
