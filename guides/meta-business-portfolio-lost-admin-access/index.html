Lost admin access to your Meta business portfolio: what are your options?

Short answer: ask a current full-control admin to add you. If nobody can, use Meta’s request for full control through Business Support Home and have ID and business proof ready.

A portfolio holds your Pages, ad accounts, catalogs and payment information. Losing admin access stops everything, and the longer it lasts, the more risk to your assets.

What to do first

Find out who still has access. Check old invitation emails, ask staff and agencies, and look at who’s listed on your Pages. Contact them with a clear written request to be made a full-control admin.

Step 1: Check you’re logged in correctly

Make sure you’re using the personal profile that was originally invited. A different login or a work email might show nothing. Try opening Business Settings directly from the profile that you used to manage ads.

Step 2: Request full control

Meta’s help center explains how to request full control of a business portfolio if there’s no admin who can grant it. Expect to confirm your identity and give evidence that you represent the business.

Step 3: If the portfolio was hacked

If someone else has taken control, use Meta’s hacked business portfolio steps. Secure your personal profile first, because that’s usually how access is lost. Then check ad accounts for charges you didn’t make and contact your bank about any.

Step 4: Prepare documents

Typical items include government ID, business registration, domain or tax records, invoices connected to ads and a signed letter. Names must be consistent across documents.

Triage: what is the risk right now?

Before the paperwork, work out what’s exposed. Are ads still running? Is the payment method still attached? Are other people able to edit your Pages? If money is at stake, contact your bank to review charges or temporarily suspend the card. For ad accounts you can still reach, lower or pause budgets. Then work on the access request with a clear head.

Write a one-page timeline: when you last had access, who had it, what changed and when you noticed. A tidy timeline speeds up any conversation about the portfolio.

What not to do

  • Don’t set up a duplicate portfolio and try to move assets informally. Assets can’t be reassigned without access.
  • Don’t pay anyone who promises to “recover” the portfolio.
  • Don’t delay if ad accounts are still billing.

Prevention

Keep two or more full-control admins, tied to different real people. Record the date each role was assigned and review them whenever staff change.

Frequently asked questions

Can Meta restore access without documents?+

Not through official channels. Expect to prove who you are.

What if the admin refuses?+

If you own the business, the official request for full control is the route. Keep your messages as a record.

Will I lose my ad history?+

Not by asking for access. The assets stay with the portfolio.
