How to remove a fake Instagram account using your photos or name

Fake accounts using your photos are common and frustrating. Instagram does remove them, as long as the report goes to the right place.

By the Unbanz team · Published 17 October 2026 · Updated 17 October 2026

  2. No Instagram account? Use the form
  3. Warn your followers

  6. Frequently asked questions

Report it as impersonation

On the fake profile, tap the three dots, then Report, then the option that says the account is pretending to be someone. Choose "Me" if it's you.

No Instagram account? Use the form

Instagram's Help Center has an impersonation form that anyone can use. You may be asked for a photo of your ID.

Warn your followers

Post a story saying the account is fake and ask people not to send money or click links. Ask friends to report it as impersonating you, but only if it really is.

If it keeps coming back

Keep a list of every fake username with screenshots. Make your real account easy to identify with a clear bio. If it keeps happening, we can handle the takedowns for you.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a fake Instagram account removed?+

Yes. Instagram removes accounts that impersonate real people when the report is filed correctly.

Do I need to send my ID?+

Sometimes. Instagram may ask the person being impersonated to confirm who they are.

What if the fake account is a parody?+

Clearly labelled parody can be allowed. Accounts that try to fool people are not.
