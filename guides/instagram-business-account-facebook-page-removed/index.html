Instagram business account after your Facebook Page is removed or disconnected

Your Instagram account looks fine, but ads won't run, the shop disappeared, scheduling tools lost access or Meta Verified was cancelled. Often the cause is the Facebook Page your Instagram professional account was connected to. Here is what depends on that link and how to rebuild it safely.

What depends on the Page connection

Instagram's help page on connecting a Facebook Page says that when a Page is connected to your professional account, you can run Instagram and Facebook ads from the Page, promote posts and stories, share between the platforms, and pay for promotions using the payment method you use on Facebook. It also notes that in some cases an active Meta Verified subscription may be cancelled if you disconnect a subscribed Facebook Page and Instagram account.

In practice, these can stop working when the Page link breaks:

  • ads created in Ads Manager that use the Instagram account;
  • Instagram Shopping and product tags tied to a catalog;
  • third-party scheduling, inbox and CRM tools that connect to Instagram through the Page;
  • shared insights and cross-posting;
  • Meta Verified on a linked Page and account.

Your Instagram posts, followers and messages are not deleted just because the Page link breaks.

Common reasons the link breaks

  • The Page was unpublished, restricted or removed for violations, or deleted by an admin.
  • The only Page admin lost access, for example because their Facebook profile was disabled, hacked or memorialized.
  • Someone disconnected the Page, accidentally or during a portfolio clean-up.
  • The Instagram account was switched back to a personal account. Instagram's professional account setup page says that if you switch to a personal account, any Facebook Page connected to your professional account will be disconnected.
  • The assets ended up in different business portfolios. Meta's business help page says the Instagram account must be in the same business portfolio as the Page, and that Instagram accounts can only be in one business portfolio at a time.

Step 1: Find out what happened to the Page

  1. Log in to Facebook with an admin profile and open the Page. Note any notice saying it is unpublished, restricted or removed.
  2. Check Page Quality for violations, and Business Support Home for advertising restrictions.
  3. In Meta Business Suite settings, check which business portfolio owns the Page and the Instagram account.
  4. On Instagram, go to Edit profile and look under Public business information (business accounts) or Profile information (creator accounts) for the Page field.

Step 2: Restore the connection

If the Page still exists and you have access

Reconnect it: on Instagram, open Edit profile, tap Page, and connect the Page. If it fails, move both assets into the same business portfolio first.

If the Page was restricted or removed for violations

Request a review of the Page in Page Quality or Business Support Home, if one is offered. Meta's Removing Pages and groups page says it will tell you whether you can request another review when it disables a Page you manage. While the review runs, don't create a lookalike Page to swap in. Meta's Account Integrity policy covers Pages created to evade a previous removal.

If the Page is gone for good

Connect a different, legitimate Page that genuinely represents the same business, owned in the same portfolio. Keep its name and details consistent with the Instagram account. Then reconnect catalogs, ad accounts and tools one by one.

If the admin lost access

Recover the admin's Facebook profile first, using our guides to locked Facebook accounts or disabled Facebook accounts. If the Page sits in a business portfolio, another portfolio admin may be able to reassign it.

How to stop this happening again

  • Give the Page and the business portfolio at least two admins on separate, secured profiles.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication for every admin.
  • Keep the Instagram account, Page, ad accounts and catalog in one business portfolio.
  • Don't switch the Instagram account to personal to “test” something.
  • Review Page Quality monthly so violations don't build up unnoticed.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does my Instagram account get deleted if my Facebook Page is removed?+

No. Removing or disconnecting the Page breaks the features that depend on it, such as Page-based ads and some tools, but the Instagram account itself is separate.

Why can't I reconnect my Instagram account to my Page?+

A common reason is that the Instagram account and Page are in different business portfolios. Meta says they must be in the same one, and an Instagram account can only be in one portfolio at a time.

Will disconnecting the Page cancel Meta Verified?+

Instagram says that in some cases an active Meta Verified subscription may be cancelled if you disconnect a subscribed Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Can I just create a new Facebook Page to replace a removed one?+

Only if it is a legitimate Page for your business, not a copy made to evade the removal. Meta's policies cover Pages created to get around enforcement.
