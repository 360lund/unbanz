How to report an Instagram account so it actually gets banned

Instagram bans accounts for breaking its rules, not for getting lots of reports. Here's how to file a report that gets looked at properly.

How to report a profile

  1. Open the profile and tap the three dots.
  2. Tap Report, then choose the reason that matches.
  3. Follow the prompts and pick the most specific option.

Pick the right reason

Impersonation, scams, harassment, hate speech and intellectual property each go to a different review path. A wrong category is the most common reason a report gets closed. Impersonation of you or your business should use the "pretending to be someone" option.

Evidence that helps

  • Screenshots with dates and the profile URL visible.
  • Links to the specific posts or messages.
  • For impersonation, your genuine profile and ID if requested.
  • For copyright or trademark, Instagram's dedicated forms.

Why mass reporting doesn't work

Getting friends to report the same account doesn't force a ban. Reviews look at whether a rule was broken. One accurate report with evidence beats a hundred vague ones. It can also break the rules on coordinated behaviour.

After you report

You can check the status in Settings under Support requests. If the account stays up, we take it down for you.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How many reports does it take to ban an Instagram account?+

There is no number. Instagram bans accounts for violations, not report counts.

Does Instagram tell the person who reported them?+

No. Reports are confidential.

Can I report an account without an Instagram account?+

Some forms, like impersonation and copyright, can be filed from the Help Center without logging in.
