Check the username

Try the name in Settings, then Username. Search for it and look at the profile. Snapchat limits how often you can change your username, so check before you commit.

If it looks inactive

An unused account doesn't mean a free name. Snapchat doesn't publish a public form for releasing inactive usernames, so there is no sure route.

If it's a fake or copycat

If the account pretends to be you or your brand, you can report it as impersonation inside the app. See our guide on reporting a fake Snapchat account.

What to avoid

Don't buy a username from a stranger and never give out your password or login codes. Sellers often vanish, or the account is taken back later.

Alternatives

Add a short tag or your city and keep it the same across platforms.
