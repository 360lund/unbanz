How to get your Instagram account back (disabled, suspended or hacked)

The fix depends on what happened. Work out which situation you're in first, then use the matching route.

Work out what happened

  • Disabled or suspended: Instagram took action for a rule issue. You see a notice when you log in.
  • Hacked: someone else changed your password, email or phone.
  • Locked: a security check. You need to confirm it's you.

If it was disabled or suspended

Open the app, read the notice and use the option to disagree or request a review before the deadline. Read the full steps in Instagram account disabled and how to appeal a suspension.

If it was hacked

Check your email for a message from security@mail.instagram.com and use the link to reverse the change. If that doesn't work, go to instagram.com/hacked. Full steps: hacked Instagram account recovery.

If it was locked

Follow the prompts, confirm the code and reset your password. See Instagram account locked.

What to avoid

Don't pay anyone who DMs you offering a recovery, and never share your password or login codes. See recovery scams.
