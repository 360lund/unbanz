Check what's taking it

Try the username on your profile or Page settings. Open facebook.com/yourname in a browser and see what loads. Sometimes it's a Page, sometimes a profile, sometimes nothing.

Your options

  • Try small variations that still read cleanly.
  • If a Page or profile is impersonating you or your brand, report it.
  • If you own a trademark, you may be able to report infringement.
