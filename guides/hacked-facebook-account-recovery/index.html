Hacked Facebook account: how to get it back

Start at facebook.com/hacked

On a phone or computer you've used for Facebook before, go to facebook.com/hacked and follow the steps. A familiar device and network make it easier for Facebook to recognise you.

If the email or phone was changed

Check your inbox for an email from Facebook about the change. It usually has a link to undo it and secure the account. If you can't find one, use Find your account at facebook.com/login/identify with any email, phone number or username you've ever had on the profile.

Confirm it's you

Facebook may ask for a video selfie, which it compares with the photos on your profile, or a photo of your ID. Use good light, follow the prompts exactly and only send it inside Facebook's own flow.

Lock it down once you're back

  • Change your password and the password on the linked email.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app.
  • Log out of devices you don't recognise in Where you're logged in.
  • Remove unknown admins from your Pages and ad accounts.
  • Tell friends to ignore messages sent while you were locked out.

If it got disabled while hacked

Hackers often post spam or scams that get the profile disabled. Then you need to appeal the disable as well. See our Facebook account disabled guide for that part.

Keep your recovery safe

Use Facebook’s official recovery flow first, and keep your password and login codes private. If you want help, pick a service you contact yourself and agree the price upfront.
