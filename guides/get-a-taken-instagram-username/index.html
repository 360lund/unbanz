Check if it's really taken

Try the handle in Settings, then Edit profile, then Username. Search for it too. A handle can show as unavailable with no visible profile, for example if the account is deactivated or the name was only just released.

  • Look for variations like dots, underscores or a number.
  • Check the profile: is it active, or abandoned?
  • Note when it last posted. It helps if you ask for help later.

Who holds it, and why it matters

A squatter, an inactive account or a copycat of your brand is the sweet spot for a claim. Those are the handles worth going after.

Routes that actually work

  • Contact the owner politely if they are a real person. Never offer to buy the account.
  • Report impersonation if the account pretends to be you or your business.
  • Use the trademark report form if you own a registered mark and the account infringes it.

What to avoid

Go for a claim, not a purchase. A claim puts the handle on your own profile and your password stays with you. More on whether a username claim is safe.

Good-enough alternatives

Add a short tag like hq, official, studio or your city, and use it everywhere. Matching handles across platforms matter as much as the exact name.

Not on demand. A real person using their own name keeps it. A squatter or impersonator is a different story.

Can I buy an Instagram username?+

No. Instagram doesn't allow buying or selling usernames.

Does the handle free up if the account is deleted?+

Sometimes, but Instagram doesn't publish the timing.
