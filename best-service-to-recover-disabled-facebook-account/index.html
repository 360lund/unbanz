How to check a Facebook recovery service

  • Never asks for your password or login codes.
  • Does not claim inside access at Meta or a special form.
  • Tells you the terms before any work starts.
  • Explains which official route it will use.
  • Can be reached on a public contact, not only through DMs.
  • Says plainly that Meta makes the final decision.
