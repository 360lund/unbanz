Ad account disabled or restricted in New Zealand?

New Zealand advertisers often run lean setups with one owner and one card. This page is for NZ businesses whose Meta ad account was disabled or restricted.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A New Zealand business with a restricted ad account should open Account Quality, check that the name matches the Companies Office entry and NZBN, clear any balance, and request one review. Unbanz can prepare the appeal and help with the documents Meta may ask for.

Use your NZBN

The New Zealand Business Number and Companies Office name should match what you entered.

Check GST

Add your GST number in billing if you are registered.

Keep NZD

Currency changes require a new ad account.

Checks specific to New Zealand

  • Use the exact registered name from the Companies Office and your NZBN if you have one.
  • Sole traders can use a bank statement or IRD document in the trading name.
  • Add your GST number in billing if you are registered for GST.
  • Keep the ad account in New Zealand dollars if you trade in NZ.
  • Check that the website shows an NZ contact route and clear returns terms.

Regulated advertising in New Zealand

Finance, health, alcohol, gambling and housing ads have extra rules in New Zealand. If you work in one, check your ads and pages against both Meta’s policies and local rules before you appeal.

First steps, in order

  1. Open Account Quality and screenshot the notice and the date.
  2. Check the people on the portfolio and remove any admin you do not know. Turn on two-factor for the rest.
  3. Clear any unpaid balance and make sure the payment method is yours and current.
  4. Check the business name, address and website match your New Zealand registration.
  5. Request the review once, with a short note on what you sell and what you changed.

Small teams and shared logins

Many New Zealand teams share one login between the owner and a contractor. Meta flags shared logins and logins from different places. Give each person a profile of their own on the portfolio.

How Unbanz helps

We help New Zealand businesses get the documents in line and prepare the appeal for Meta’s official review. You can read about the notice types on our page on restricted versus disabled and the main service page for disabled ad accounts. Meta makes the final decision.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
