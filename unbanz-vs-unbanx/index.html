The short answer

Unbanz and Unbanx are one letter apart and that is all they share. We are not connected to Unbanx or unbanx.me in any way.

  • Unbanx (with an x): not an account service and not us. The old Unbanx was an Irish rewards app that is no longer trading, and unbanx.me presents itself as a crypto investment platform.

Reading reviews? Check the spelling

Search engines sometimes swap the two names. If a review, rating page or scam checker says Unbanx or unbanx.me, it is about a different business. Unbanz client reviews, on camera with full transcripts, are on our reviews page.

Not unban.tools either

Unbanz never sells software, scripts or downloads. We work through the platforms’ own appeal and reporting processes, and your password stays with you.

Unbanz only helps with Instagram and Facebook accounts.
