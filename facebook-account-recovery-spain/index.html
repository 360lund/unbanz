Facebook account recovery in Spain.

Facebook account hacked, suspended or locked out and you’re in Spain? These are the official steps, the Spanish help options, and how Unbanz helps you appeal through Meta’s official process, in English.

Short answer

Facebook account recovery in Spain follows Facebook’s own routes: disagree with the decision on a suspension notice within the time shown, or use facebook.com/hacked for a hijacked account. Call 017 for free cybersecurity advice. Unbanz helps you appeal through Meta’s official process; Meta makes the final decision.

Official steps that come first

  1. If a suspended or disabled notice shows, log in and follow the prompts to disagree with the decision. Read the date on your own notice.
  2. If login details changed, open facebook.com/hacked on a device and browser you have used before.
  3. You can also search for your account at facebook.com/login/identify and choose the option for no longer having access.
  4. Look in your inbox and spam for a Facebook email about the change and use its undo link at once.
  5. If ID or a video selfie is requested, use your real details, follow the instructions, and wait for the answer.

Spanish details worth knowing

  • Free advice: call 017, INCIBE’s cybersecurity helpline, for confidential help with hacked accounts and scams. It does not restore accounts.
  • Reporting a crime: the Policía Nacional (denuncias.policia.es) and the Guardia Civil take reports of fraud and identity misuse. Tell your bank at once if money moved. Call 112 in an emergency.
  • Second-hand sales: a buyer who sends a link to receive payment or asks you to log in again is a known trick on Facebook Marketplace.
  • Your +34 number: nine digits, no leading zero. Movistar, Orange, Vodafone, Digi and others replace a lost SIM after ID.
  • Pages and groups: a local business or club Page run by one person is easy to lose with a hacked profile. Name a second admin.

Your rights as an EU user

Under the Digital Services Act, Instagram and Facebook must offer an internal complaint route against many account decisions and tell users about out-of-court dispute bodies. The CNMC is the Digital Services Coordinator for Spain. Appeals Centre Europe is one certified body that accepts some content and account decisions from EU users. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app review. Use the in-app route first.

Good to know.

How do I recover a hacked Facebook account in Spain?+

Go to facebook.com/hacked on a device and browser you have used before, or search for your account at facebook.com/login/identify and choose the option for no longer having access. Check the email on the account first for a message from Facebook with a link to undo a change.

Can the AEPD or INCIBE help me get Facebook back?+

No. INCIBE gives advice and the AEPD handles personal data and intimate content removal. A disabled or hacked account goes through Facebook’s own process.

Can I take an Instagram or Facebook decision to an independent body?+

If you live in the EU, the Digital Services Act lets you use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe for some decisions about content or an account. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app appeal.
