This is a scam on top of a hack. Paying seldom returns the account and often brings more demands.

If someone who holds your account asks for payment to return it, do not pay. Nothing says the account comes back, and it often leads to further demands. Report the messages, document them, and use the official hacked-account recovery flow. If the hacker threatens to publish private content, tell the police.

How the demand works

  • The hacker keeps the account and asks for a fee.
  • Some pose as helpers who say they can restore it for money.
  • Others threaten to delete or publish content.
  • After a payment the account may not be returned.

The same applies to offers from strangers who say they can get your account back for a fee through private contacts.

What to do

  1. Stop replying to the person and keep the messages.
  2. Report the account and messages as scams.
  3. Start the hacked-account recovery flow with the selfie steps.
  4. If threats are involved, contact the police and tell us what you can share.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Staying safe

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or claims to have a special way in, walk away. Unbanz never asks for logins and has no special access. We use the same official routes that are open to you.
