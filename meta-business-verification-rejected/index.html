Meta rejected my business verification. What do I do?

Almost always it’s a mismatch: the business name, address or document doesn’t line up with what you entered. Fix that to the letter and try again, and check that your website shows the same details.

Talk to us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Short answer

Verification is mostly matching. Meta compares what you typed with a document and your website. Small differences like “LLC” vs no “LLC”, or an old address, can fail it.

Why it gets rejected

  • The legal name doesn’t match the document.
  • The address is different, abbreviated or out of date.
  • The document is blurry, cropped or the wrong kind.
  • The domain isn’t verified or the website has no business details.
  • The phone or email doesn’t match anything public about the business.

How to fix it

  1. Copy your legal name and address exactly from your registration document.
  2. Use a clean, full, in-date document.
  3. Make sure your website shows the same name, address, phone and email.
  4. Verify your domain in Business settings.
  5. Resubmit once, not five times.

Why it matters for ads

Unverified or failed verification is a common reason ad accounts stay restricted or can’t be reinstated. See Meta ad account restricted and Business Portfolio restricted.
