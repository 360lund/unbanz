Your backup Instagram account was suspended as well?

This is one of the most common things we hear. The new account is linked to the old one, so it inherits the problem.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Instagram links accounts by device, phone number, email, payment details and Accounts Center. When one account is disabled, a new or backup account linked to it is often removed too, sometimes for circumventing the original decision. Stop creating accounts, appeal the main one and keep the evidence that it was genuine.

Stop opening accounts

Each new one can count against you.

Appeal the original

That is the decision that matters.

Keep proof

Screenshots, invoices and the notice dates help.

Why the backup went too

Meta’s rules let it remove accounts created to get around an enforcement action. An account made on the same phone, with the same email or number, or added to the same Accounts Center, looks like that to an automated system even when you only wanted to keep in touch with customers.

What to do now

  1. Stop creating new accounts on the same device or details.
  2. Open each notice and note which account was removed for what reason. The original reason and the backup reason are often different.
  3. Appeal the original account first. If the backup notice offers its own appeal, use it and say plainly that it was created to keep your business reachable.
  4. If your linked Facebook profile is affected, check its status in Accounts Center.
  5. Save the dates of every notice and decision.

If you are in the EU or UK

EU users can take a final Instagram decision to a certified out-of-court dispute body such as Appeals Centre Europe within six months. UK users can ask Meta for a copy of their personal data. See your rights when an account is banned.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Will a new phone fix it?+

Not on its own. Accounts are linked by more than the device, and using a new phone to open new accounts can still count as getting around the decision.

Should I appeal the backup account too?+

Yes, if the notice offers an appeal. Keep it short and honest about why the account exists.

Can I ever open a new account?+

If the original decision stands after review, the terms you agreed to may not allow a new account. Read the notice and Meta’s terms before you try.

Do I need to share my Instagram password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
