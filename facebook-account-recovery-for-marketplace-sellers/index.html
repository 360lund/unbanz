Facebook recovery for Marketplace sellers

When the account goes, the listings, the messages and the seller reputation go with it.

Marketplace sellers depend on one profile for listings and buyer chats. If it is disabled or hacked, gather your listing screenshots, order records and the message from Facebook, then use the official recovery route. Tell buyers through another channel and avoid selling from a second profile meanwhile.

Save your listing proof

Screenshots and receipts.

Tell active buyers

Use a phone or email you already have.

Avoid a second profile

It can look like evasion.

What is at risk

  • Active listings and buyer messages.
  • Seller ratings built over time.
  • Payouts or payments in progress.
  • Page and group roles tied to the profile.

What to gather

  1. Screenshots of recent listings and buyer messages.
  2. The message from Facebook, with the date.
  3. The email and phone linked to the account.
  4. A list of Pages or groups you manage.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your Facebook profile link and what happened to the account.
  2. We tell you honestly whether this is something we can help with.
  3. We prepare the recovery request and the supporting details for Facebook’s official process.
  4. We stay in touch while you wait. Meta makes the final decision, and nobody can promise the outcome.

Who this is for

  • Casual and regular sellers.
  • Small resellers.
  • People selling local goods.

Staying safe

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or claims to have a special way in, walk away. Unbanz never asks for logins and has no special access. We use the same official routes that are open to you.
