Instagram asked for ID on a business account?

A business account still has a person behind it, and the request is about that person. Here is how to handle it.

Updated 13 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

On a business account, the ID request is usually about the person who controls the account, not the company. The account owner sends their own document, and a staff member should not send the director’s ID. If the request also asks for business details, send what is shown on the request screen.

The owner sends it

Not a colleague, and not an agency.

Business papers if asked

Only the documents the screen names.

Keep admin roles clean

Know who has access before you start.

Why it is asked

  • The account was flagged for unusual login activity.
  • The account was reported for impersonation or misleading claims.
  • A hacker may have taken control and the owner must be verified.
  • The business connected a verified Page or ad account.

How to handle it

  1. Decide who the account owner is and have that person do the request.
  2. Use the official screen only and send the document it lists.
  3. If it asks for company details, use the registration paperwork that matches the name on the account.
  4. Share the screens with us, but never send the ID in a message.

Agencies managing client accounts should have the client submit their own ID.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Questions people also ask (2026)

Stuck on “we’re reviewing your info” after verification?

That screen means the selfie or document was received. Do not resubmit unless asked, and do not create new accounts while waiting, which can be treated as getting around the decision. If nothing changes for weeks, an EU user can look at Appeals Centre Europe once Meta’s own appeal is done.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.
