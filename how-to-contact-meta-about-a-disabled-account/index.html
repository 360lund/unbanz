How to contact Meta about a disabled account.

The real ways to reach Meta about a disabled, suspended or hacked Instagram or Facebook account, and the ones that do not exist.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

You contact Meta about a disabled account by appealing from the notice in the app. That is the route Meta reads. There is no general Meta phone number and no email address that accepts appeals. If you can log in, the in-app help and Meta AI support assistant can answer questions, and Meta Verified subscribers can request human support. Third-party services, including Unbanz, are not Meta: a letter addressed to Meta and sent to anyone else does not reach Meta.

The routes that exist

RouteWhen it helps
The appeal on the disabled or suspended noticeAlways first. It is the only route that reopens the decision.

Hacked pages: instagram.com/hacked, facebook.com/hackedTakeovers, changed email or phone.

Business Support HomeAd accounts, Pages and Business Portfolios. See ad account links.

Routes that do not exist

  • A Meta customer service phone number for account recovery.
  • An email address that accepts appeals.
  • A “private” Meta appeal link or an insider who can unban for a fee.
  • A Meta account on WhatsApp or Telegram that restores accounts.

If someone offers one of these, treat it as a scam. See is this Instagram email real.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone for a “private” or “direct” appeal link. There is none.

If you wrote an appeal letter

Many people write a letter “to the Meta team” and send it to every address they can find. It only reaches Meta through the appeal box or the support assistant. Shorten it to a few clear sentences and paste it there. Keep a copy for your records.
