Sending recovery cases in bulk.

Ten disabled creator accounts, a Business Portfolio with twenty ad accounts, a backlog of handles: send them as one batch.

Updated 18 November 2026

Email us ↗︎

Send a batch as one email or spreadsheet with one row per account: platform, link or ID, the notice, the date and your own reference. Unbanz triages every row, tells you which cases we can take, and you pay upfront for the cases you want to start. Each case is refunded 100% if it fails, so one failure never affects the rest of the batch. Partners who send cases regularly or in batches get volume pricing, agreed in writing before the work starts. We do not publish those numbers.

How a batch moves

  1. You send the batch to team@unbanz.com.
  2. We reply with a row-by-row triage: take, need more detail, or not something we can help with.
  3. You choose which cases to start and pay upfront for those.
  4. Each case gets its own unbranded updates under your reference.
  5. Cases that fail are refunded individually.

Which cases to send first

  • Ad accounts and Business Portfolios that are stopping client revenue.
  • Hacked accounts, where time since the takeover matters.
  • Accounts with an open appeal window shown in the app.

Partner terms

  • Each case is paid upfront, and each case that fails gets a 100% refund.
  • Partners who send cases regularly or in batches get volume pricing, agreed in writing before the work starts. We do not publish those numbers.
  • A dedicated contact for your agency at team@unbanz.com, with your own case references.
  • Private-label updates: plain reports with no Unbanz name or logo, ready to forward or paste into your own template.
  • Unbanz never contacts your client and never mentions Unbanz to them.

Start

Email team@unbanz.com with your agency name, the platforms your clients use, a sample of the cases in the format above and roughly how many cases you expect each month. We reply with written terms before the first case.
