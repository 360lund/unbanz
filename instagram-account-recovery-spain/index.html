Checks for Spain

Phone formats, where to report fraud and any appeal rights differ by country. The sections below cover Spain.

Official steps that come first

  1. If a disabled or suspended notice appears, tap it and request a review once. Go through every screen, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password changed, open instagram.com/hacked on a phone or laptop you have used with Instagram before.
  3. Search your inbox and spam for an Instagram email about the change. Its undo link expires, so use it first.
  4. If your Spanish SIM was lost or ported, ask your operator for a replacement, since codes go to that number.
  5. Send one complete appeal and wait for the reply to the email on the account.

Checks that matter in Spain

  • Your +34 number: Spanish numbers have nine digits and no leading zero, so a mobile is +34 612 345 678. Movistar, Orange, Vodafone, Digi and other operators replace a lost SIM after you identify yourself.
  • Free helpline: INCIBE’s cybersecurity helpline, 017, gives free and confidential advice on hacked accounts, scams and online harassment. It does not restore the account.
  • Police reports: the Policía Nacional (denuncias.policia.es) and the Guardia Civil take reports of fraud, threats and identity misuse. Call 112 in an emergency. Tell your bank straight away if money moved.
  • Intimate content: the Spanish data protection agency, the AEPD (aepd.es), runs a priority channel for asking for the removal of sexual or violent content shared without consent.
  • Phishing bait: messages posing as a courier, a bank or the tax agency are a common way in. Never sign in from a link in a message.

Your rights as an EU user

Under the Digital Services Act, Instagram and Facebook must offer an internal complaint route against many account decisions and tell users about out-of-court dispute bodies. In Spain, the CNMC is the Digital Services Coordinator. It supervises platforms and does not decide your individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe is one certified body that accepts some content and account decisions from EU users. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app review. Use the in-app route first.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Spain?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

What is the 017 number in Spain?+

It is the cybersecurity helpline run by INCIBE. It is free and confidential and can advise you on a hacked account, scams or online harassment. It cannot give an Instagram account back, which goes through Instagram’s own review.

Can I take an Instagram or Facebook decision to an independent body?+

If you live in the EU, the Digital Services Act lets you use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe for some decisions about content or an account. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app appeal.
