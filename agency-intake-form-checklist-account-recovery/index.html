Agency intake checklist for account recovery requests.

A clean intake saves days of back and forth. This is the list to collect before you contact a recovery partner.

Email us ↗︎

Before passing a recovery request to a partner, collect the platform, the username, a screenshot of the notice with its date, the email and number on the account, who else has access and the client’s go-ahead. Never ask for a password or a login code, under any circumstances.

What never to ask for

  • A password.
  • A login code or recovery code.
  • Access to the client’s email.
  • A payment card.

If a provider asks for any of these, see red flags for agencies.

How to ask

Use a form, an email or a short chat message with the list. A client under stress does better with a clear list than with an open question. If the client cannot find a field, tell them to skip it and send what they have.

Sending it to Unbanz

Send the details by email to team@unbanz.com. No client name is needed. Unbanz replies with the route and what to expect, and does not contact the client.

Keeping the material tidy

Store the screenshots and the date in a folder named after the matter, not the client. Delete them when the matter is closed. See client confidentiality.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What is the minimum needed to start?+

The platform, the username, a screenshot of the notice with the date and the client’s go-ahead.

Why do you need the email and phone number?+

The platform may send checks to them, and the details help identify the account. Passwords are never needed.

What if the client has no screenshot?+

Ask them to describe the notice word for word and to try to capture it again, without logging in repeatedly.

Do I need the client’s name?+

No.

Can I use my own form?+

Yes. Keep the fields the same.
