Checks for Poland

Phone formats, where to report fraud and any appeal rights differ by country. The sections below cover Poland.

Official steps that come first

  1. If a disabled or suspended notice appears, tap it and request a review once. Complete each step, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password changed, go to instagram.com/hacked on a phone or laptop you have used with Instagram before.
  3. Search your inbox and spam, including Wirtualna Polska or Onet mail if you used it, for an Instagram email about the change. The undo link expires.
  4. If your Polish SIM was lost or ported, ask your operator to block it and send a replacement.
  5. Send one complete appeal and wait for the reply to the account’s email.

Checks that matter in Poland

  • Your +48 number: Polish numbers have nine digits and no leading zero, so a mobile is +48 512 345 678. Orange Polska, Play, Plus, T-Mobile and other operators replace a lost SIM after you identify yourself.
  • Phishing messages: CERT Polska (cert.pl) takes reports of phishing, and you can forward a suspicious text to 8080.
  • Reporting a crime: the police take reports of fraud, threats and identity misuse at any station. Call 112 in an emergency or 997 for the police. Tell your bank straight away if money moved.
  • Privacy: the UODO (uodo.gov.pl) is the Polish data protection authority. The UOKiK handles consumer protection. Neither restores accounts.
  • Polish notices: the review options are the same in Polish and English. Screenshot the notice as it appears. We work in English over WhatsApp or email.

Your rights as an EU user

Under the Digital Services Act, Instagram and Facebook must offer an internal complaint route against many account decisions and tell users about out-of-court dispute bodies. If you think a platform has not followed the Act, you can complain to the national authorities. They do not decide your individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe is one certified body that accepts some content and account decisions from EU users. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app review. Use the in-app route first.

What not to do

  • Don’t pay or message anyone from a DM who says they can “open” your account.
  • Don’t share your password or login codes with anyone, including us.
  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Instagram, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

How do I recover a hacked Instagram account in Poland?+

Use the undo link in Instagram’s email about the change if there is one, then go to instagram.com/hacked on a device you used before and follow the steps. Instagram may ask for a code or a video selfie. If you see a disabled notice, request a review once.

What is the 8080 number in Poland?+

It is the number CERT Polska asks people to forward suspicious text messages to. It helps fight phishing. It does not restore an Instagram account, which goes through Instagram’s own review.

Can I take an Instagram or Facebook decision to an independent body?+

If you live in the EU, the Digital Services Act lets you use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe for some decisions about content or an account. It does not cover hacked accounts or decisions based on how an account behaved, and it does not replace the in-app appeal.
