Lost your Instagram username during a rebrand?

Releasing a handle during a rebrand can let someone else take it. Here is what to check.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

When you change your Instagram handle, the old one is released and others can register it. If a rebrand left you without your original name, check whether the new holder is active, whether it matches your trademark or business name, and which official route fits. Do not buy it from the holder.

Check who holds it

Active, empty or copying your brand each point to a different route.

Use your brand papers

Registration or trademark details support a brand report.

Do not pay the holder

Paying for a handle can put your own account at risk.

What may apply

  • An empty or inactive account on the handle may fit the inactive account route.
  • An account copying your brand may fit a brand or impersonation report.
  • A normal active account owned by someone else is hard to claim.
  • If the handle is available, you can simply change back.

Every claim goes through the platform’s own processes. No bots, no false reports, no buying or selling accounts.

What to send

  1. The old handle and your current handle.
  2. What the old handle was used for and for how long.
  3. Brand or business registration details, if any.
  4. A screenshot of the handle’s current profile.

What to send

  • The exact @username you want.
  • Your current Instagram profile, so the handle can go onto it.
  • Why the name is yours: brand, business name, registered trademark, or the name you used before.
  • What you have already tried, if anything.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
