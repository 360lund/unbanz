Why a case quote?

A disabled Instagram profile, a restricted Meta ad account and an impersonation report require different work. We check the notice and history before putting a price on the case.

What you get upfront

We confirm the accepted scope, price, payment terms, estimated timing and any applicable refund terms in your chat or email before work starts. Asking for a quote does not book the service.

What to send

Your account link or ID, a screenshot of the notice and when it happened. For a ban request, send the profile link and evidence of the violation.
