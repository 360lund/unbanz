How the white label arrangement works

The client sees your agency. Behind it, Unbanz handles the Instagram or Facebook side. For an agency in Germany that means:

  • You keep the client relationship, the communication and the invoice to your client.
  • Intake is by email, between you and us.
  • We handle the appeal or the username claim through the official process of the platform.
  • The platform decides. We cannot promise the account or handle will come back, and neither can you.

Details that matter in Germany

These are the points that differ for clients based in Germany.

  • Notice language: Notices may appear in German. The options are the same in German and English.
  • Phone number: A German mobile written 0151 1234567 is entered as +49 151 1234567, without the zero.
  • Business details: Have the company name, website and registration number ready if the platform asks for proof the business is real.
  • If fraud or blackmail is involved: Each federal state runs an Onlinewache where fraud, threats or identity misuse can be reported. The Verbraucherzentrale consumer advice centres give free guidance on phishing and account takeovers. That runs alongside the platform appeal.
  • Platform rules: Under the Digital Services Act, the Bundesnetzagentur supervises platforms in the country. It does not decide an individual appeal. Appeals Centre Europe accepts some account decisions from EU users, but not hacked accounts, and Instagram’s own review comes first.
  • Client data: Data protection is supervised by the federal and state data protection authorities. Send us only what the appeal needs.
  • Advertising rules: The Deutscher Werberat is the German advertising self-regulatory body. Meta’s own ad policies still apply on top.

Before you message us

Collect this from your client first. It saves a round of questions.

  • The username or profile link, and the email and phone number on the account.
  • An unedited screenshot of the notice, and the date it appeared.
  • A short note on what happened: hacked, disabled, suspended or a handle they want.
  • The client’s go-ahead for you and Unbanz to act on their account. Never ask for their password or login codes.
  • For a business account in Germany: the company name, website and registration number.
