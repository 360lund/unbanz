Pricing models for reselling account recovery.

There is no single right way to charge for recovery. These are the usual models, with what each one means for you.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Agencies usually resell account recovery in one of four ways: per incident, as a monthly protection add-on, as a line inside a retainer, or included in a package. Each shifts risk differently. Whatever the model, price the work and coordination, never a promised outcome, because the platform decides.

The four models

ModelHow it worksGood for
Per incidentClient pays when a lockout happensAgencies who rarely see lockouts
Monthly add-onClient pays a small recurring amount for protection and handlingAgencies with many active accounts
Retainer lineRecovery handling is a named line in the retainerLarger clients
IncludedAbsorbed as a service benefitAgencies using it to retain clients

Where the risk sits

Under per incident pricing, the client carries the cost when it happens. Under add-ons and included models, the agency carries the cost of unlucky months. Choose the model that suits your cash flow and how much risk you want. The partner’s terms feed into this.

Principles that apply to all

  • Price the effort, intake, coordination and communication.
  • Say clearly that the platform decides and nothing is promised.
  • State what happens if the account is not restored, so there are no surprises.
  • Keep a written record of what was agreed with the client.
  • For username claims, remember our refund policy of a 100% refund if a claim fails, and agree how you pass that through.

What not to do

  • Do not price against the result, such as success fees promising recovery.
  • Do not advertise a fixed outcome.
  • Do not hide that the work is done with a partner if the client asks.

Good to know.

Which pricing model is best?+

It depends on how many accounts you manage and how much risk you want to carry. Per incident is the simplest to start with.

Should I charge a success fee?+

Avoid anything that implies the result is certain. Charge for the work, and keep the platform’s decision separate.

Does Unbanz set my client price?+

No. You set your own price. Unbanz agrees its own terms with you.

What if recovery fails?+

Say in advance what the client pays and what they do not.

Does this page give prices?+

No. Prices depend on your market and your costs.
