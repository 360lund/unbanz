How long does a WhatsApp ban last?

A temporary ban shows a countdown on the screen, and a permanent one doesn’t. WhatsApp publishes no fixed length, so use “Request a review” in the app as soon as you see the notice.

Temporary vs permanent

  • Temporary. Shows a time left, often after spam-like behavior or too many reports in a short time.
  • Permanent. “Your phone number is banned from using WhatsApp.” Needs a review request.

What to do

  1. Uninstall any modified WhatsApp (GB WhatsApp, WhatsApp Plus and similar).
  2. Install the official app.
  3. Tap “Request a review” and keep your message short and honest.
  4. Don’t re-register the number again and again.

While you wait
