Your client’s account got banned. Start here.

For agencies, SMMA owners, freelancers and white-label resellers handling disabled, restricted or hacked client accounts.

Updated 5 November 2026

When a client account is disabled, find out which asset is affected and who holds admin access. A personal profile is appealed by its owner from the login notice. Pages, ad accounts and Business Portfolios are reviewed in Business Support Home by an admin. Agencies can work with Unbanz directly or white-label: you keep the client relationship, we work on the recovery under your brand. Meta makes the final decision.

What to send us

Message us on WhatsApp or email team@unbanz.com with:

  • The account link or username, and which platform it is on.
  • A screenshot of the exact notice, with the date.
  • Whether an appeal was already submitted, and what happened.
  • Who holds admin access: the client, your agency, or both.
  • The Business Portfolio ID or ad account ID, if ads are involved.
  • Never your password or login codes.

Good to know.

Can an agency appeal on behalf of a client?+

For Pages, ad accounts and Business Portfolios, an admin can request a review in Business Support Home. A disabled personal profile is appealed by its owner.

Can we keep Unbanz invisible to our client?+

Yes. In a white-label setup you speak to the client and we work behind your brand.

Does Unbanz need the client’s password?+

No. Never share passwords or login codes.
