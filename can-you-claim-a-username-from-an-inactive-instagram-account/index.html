Can you claim a username from an inactive Instagram account?

Not with a button: Instagram has no form that says “this account is inactive, give me the name”. It can still be done in some situations, through official routes matched to the handle.

Talk to us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Short answer

Being quiet for years doesn’t give anyone a right to a handle, and Instagram doesn’t publish a timer that frees them. What changes things is why the account is sitting there.

What can open a route

  • It uses your name or brand to mislead people. That’s impersonation, and it can be reported.
  • It matches your registered trademark. Meta’s intellectual property process applies.
  • It’s parked by a squatter. A blank account holding a handle to resell is a different job from a quiet personal profile.
  • The owner deletes or renames. Then it’s first come, first served.

What usually doesn’t work

  • Reporting a quiet account as “inactive”. There isn’t a report reason for that.
  • Messaging the owner repeatedly.
  • Paying someone to “release” it. That’s a common scam.

How to tell if yours is worth trying

  1. Look at the profile: any posts, stories or activity in the last year or two?
  2. Check whether it uses your name, brand or logo.
  3. Check if it has the same handle on other platforms, which suggests a squatter.
