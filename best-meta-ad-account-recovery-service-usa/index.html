Local questions, answered (2026)

Why do ad accounts in the US get restricted?

The usual triggers are a declined or disputed card, a billing country or currency change, a spend spike, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new country, or an admin whose own profile is restricted.

What documents pass business verification in the US?

Use an EIN confirmation letter (CP 575 or 147C), articles of incorporation or a utility bill in the business name, with the legal name and address matching the business portfolio exactly.

Where do I request the review?

Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/accountquality). Select the ad account and use Request review if shown. Meta does not state a review time.

Is there an outside appeal in the US?

There is no outside appeal body for Meta account decisions in the US, so Meta’s own review is the only formal route.
