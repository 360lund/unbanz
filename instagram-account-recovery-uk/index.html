Official steps that come first

  1. If you see a disabled or suspended notice, tap it and request a review. Complete every step, including any selfie check.
  2. If the email, number or password was changed, go to instagram.com/hacked on a device and network you have used before.
  3. Check your email, including spam, for a message from Instagram about the change and use its undo link if it works.
  4. Send one complete appeal, then watch the email on the account for the reply.

Checks that matter in the UK

  • Your +44 number: text codes go to your UK number. If you lost the SIM or the number was ported, speak to your network first.
  • Reporting fraud: if the hack led to scams, blackmail or lost money, report it through Report Fraud (reportfraud.police.uk) in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or call Police Scotland on 101. If money left your bank account, tell your bank straight away.
  • Practical guidance: the National Cyber Security Centre publishes a plain-English “Recovering a hacked account” guide at ncsc.gov.uk, which is worth reading alongside Instagram’s steps.
  • Business accounts: have your trading name, website and Companies House details to hand if you’re asked to show the business is real.

What not to do

  • Ignore strangers who DM you first saying they can “open” your account. Unbanz never cold-messages people and never asks for your password.
  • Don’t share your password or login codes with anyone, including us.
  • Don’t create new accounts to plead with Instagram, and don’t send the same appeal over and over.

Local questions, answered (2026)

Which ID does Instagram accept in the UK?

Meta’s list changes, but a UK passport, photocard driving licence or biometric residence permit is the usual choice. Make sure the name matches the profile. Often Instagram asks for a video selfie instead of a document.

Disabled and Instagram shows no ID option?

Do the video selfie if offered, once, in good light. If nothing is offered, screenshot the screen with the date and avoid opening new accounts while you wait.

Hacked and disabled in the UK: who do I tell?

Use instagram.com/hacked first, then appeal the disable. Report the hack or scam to Report Fraud (which replaced Action Fraud on 4 December 2025) on 0300 123 2040, or Police Scotland on 101.

Where do I check status in Accounts Center?

Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status. If you cannot log in, the notice on the login screen has the review button.

Is there an outside appeal in the UK?

Ofcom regulates platforms under the Online Safety Act but does not decide individual appeals. Appeals Centre Europe is set up under EU law and is aimed at users in the EU.
