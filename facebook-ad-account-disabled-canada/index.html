Checks specific to Canada

  • Use the legal name from your provincial or federal registration, and your CRA Business Number if Meta asks for it.
  • Add your GST/HST number in billing so tax shows correctly on invoices.
  • Quebec businesses: French-language ads are fine, but the landing page should match the ad language.
  • Cannabis and some financial products have stricter rules in Canada. Check both Meta’s policy and the provincial rules.

Then do this

  1. Read the reason in Account Quality.
  2. Turn on two-factor for every admin and remove anyone you don’t know.
  3. Clear any unpaid balance.
  4. Request the review once, with a short note on what you sell and what you fixed.

Regulated industries

Finance, health, alcohol, gambling and housing ads have extra rules in Canada. If you’re in one of these, make sure your ads and landing pages meet both Meta’s policies and local rules before you appeal.

Good to know.

Can Unbanz help businesses in Canada with disabled ad accounts?+

Yes. Unbanz helps businesses and agencies in Canada appeal disabled and restricted Meta ad accounts through Meta’s official review.

What documents does Meta accept for business verification?+

Usually official registration documents. In Canada that means your provincial or federal registration, with the legal name and address exactly as registered.

Does my GST/HST number affect my ad account?+

Wrong or missing tax details won’t usually disable an account, but they can cause billing problems that do. Keep them accurate.

Do I need to share my login?+

No. Unbanz works from the ad account ID and the notice.

Is Unbanz affiliated with Meta?+

No. Unbanz is an independent service.

What if Meta turns the review down?+

Read the reason in Account Quality and do not resend the same request unchanged. Some notices still have a next step. See what to do after a rejected appeal. Meta makes the final decision.

What does Account Quality show?+

It lists the assets Meta has limited, the reason given and whether a review can still be requested. Take a screenshot before you change anything on the account.
