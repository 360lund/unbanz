What is specific to the UK: regulators, reporting lines and rights.

In the UK, the escalations are the ICO for data requests Meta ignores, Report Fraud (which replaced Action Fraud on 4 December 2025) for hacking and scams, and the Online Safety Act framework that Ofcom enforces, although Ofcom does not handle individual complaints. The Meta routes

The Meta routes themselves do not change by country: appeal from the notice you see at login (or in Accounts Center), use www.instagram.com/hacked/ or www.facebook.com/hacked for a takeover, and ask for ad account reviews in business.facebook.com/accountquality. The full list is in the Meta appeal links directory. What follows is only what differs here.

Subject access request under UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you can ask Meta for a copy of the personal data it holds about you, including records linked to an account decision. The normal deadline is one calendar month, extendable by two further months for complex requests. If the reply is missing or incomplete, the ICO is the next step. A SAR does not reinstate an account, but it can show what Meta relied on.

The Online Safety Act and Ofcom

Ofcom enforces the Online Safety Act 2023, which requires services such as Instagram and Facebook to run complaints procedures, including for users who think content was wrongly taken down. Ofcom does not decide individual cases, so it will not restore your account, but it collects complaints to spot systemic failures. Write to Meta through the in-app appeal first.

Common UK situations

  • Hacked accounts used to run fake ticket or crypto offers to your followers. Get a Report Fraud reference before you appeal.
  • Under-18 accounts placed into Teen Account settings and adults wrongly flagged as teens after an age check.
  • Sole traders whose Instagram shop and ad account are restricted together.

Timing

The UK is on GMT in winter and BST in summer. Reviews run worldwide, so submission time does not matter; what matters is sending one complete appeal.

Good to know.

Is Action Fraud still the place to report?+

No. Since 4 December 2025, Report Fraud has replaced Action Fraud in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101.

How long does Meta have to answer a subject access request?+

One month, extendable by up to two more months for complex requests.

Can Ofcom get my Instagram back?+

No. Ofcom regulates systems and does not handle individual account cases.
