Check these first

  1. In Account Quality, select the portfolio and read the reason.
  2. Look at the People list. Remove anyone you don’t know. A hacked admin is one of the most common triggers.
  3. Check business verification. A legal name, address or website that doesn’t match your documents can block it.
  4. Make sure your website domain is verified in the portfolio.
  5. Request the review once, from the portfolio.

Why portfolios get disabled

  • An admin profile was compromised or restricted.
  • One ad account inside broke advertising policies repeatedly.
  • Meta linked the portfolio to one that was disabled before.
  • Verification documents didn’t match the business details.

Disabled vs restricted portfolio

A restricted portfolio can usually still hold assets but can’t advertise. A disabled one can lock you out of managing assets too. If pages are stuck inside, you may need to move access to a new admin after the review, not before.

Ad account questions people also ask (2026)

What are the usual reasons for an ad account restriction?

The common ones are a declined or disputed payment, a billing country or currency change, a sudden spend increase, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new device or country, unverified business details, or an admin whose personal profile is itself restricted. Account Quality usually names the policy.

Where do I request the review?

Go to Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/accountquality), select the ad account, and use Request review if it is shown. For a personal ad account, open Accounts Center, then Account status.

Business verification or ID check pending: what helps?

Use documents that match the business portfolio name and address exactly, such as an EIN letter (US), Companies House record (UK), CRA business number (Canada), KvK extract (Netherlands) or Kbis/SIREN (France). A name mismatch is one of the most common reasons verification fails.

Country guides: US, UK, Canada, Netherlands, France.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is Business Manager the same as Business Portfolio?+

Yes. Meta renamed Business Manager to Business Portfolio. The restriction and review process is the same.

Can I make a new Business Manager instead?+

Making a new one to get around a disabled portfolio can be treated as circumventing systems. Appeal the original first.

Does business verification help a disabled portfolio?+

It often does, as long as the legal name and address match your registration documents exactly.

Can Unbanz help with a disabled Business Manager?+

Yes. Unbanz helps businesses and agencies appeal disabled or restricted Business Portfolios through Meta’s official review.

How do I contact Unbanz?+

Email team@unbanz.com.

Should I open a new ad account while I wait?+

Usually not. A new account opened after a restriction can look like an attempt to get around Meta’s systems and can put the new account at risk too. Read can I make a new ad account after a ban before you decide.

What should I avoid while a review is open?+

Do not send repeated requests, add new cards or admins to the account, or run ads from another account tied to the same business. Pay any unpaid balance, answer any request for documents, and keep screenshots of every notice.
