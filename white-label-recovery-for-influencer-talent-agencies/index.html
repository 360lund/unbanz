Why talent accounts get locked

  • Large audiences attract hacking attempts, phishing messages and impersonators.
  • Rapid growth and busy posting can trip automated systems.
  • Creators use many accounts and collaborators, which raises the risk of a lost login.
  • Brand deals involve shared access with managers and editors.

The platform’s own decision is final every time, and the guidance is the same for every account type.

What the agency keeps

You keep the relationship with the creator and with the brands who book them. You decide what the creator is told, what the brand is told and whether to pause a campaign. Unbanz does not contact either side. For the brand side, a plain note about a pause is usually enough, and you can borrow wording from the status update templates.

Information to collect from the creator

  • The username and the email and number on the account.
  • Screenshots of the notice and any emails from the platform.
  • The date the account was last used normally.
  • Whether the account is also linked to a business portfolio or a Page.
  • Who else has access, such as editors or assistants.

Use the intake checklist and never ask for a password.

Reducing the chance of a repeat

After any recovery, help the creator turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, store backup codes somewhere safe and remove old collaborators. The prevention checklist has the full list.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Why would a talent agency use white label recovery?+

It lets the agency respond to a creator’s lockout under its own brand without building the skill in-house, and it keeps the creator relationship with the agency.

Does the creator find out about Unbanz?+

No. Unbanz works for the agency behind the scenes and does not contact creators.

What if a creator’s account is gone for good?+

The platform’s decision is final for that request. Tell the creator plainly and plan around a new account or other channels.

Can you claim a handle for a creator?+

Sometimes, through official routes such as a trademark report or a recovery route when the handle was the creator’s before. No handle can be promised.

Do you need the creator’s password?+

Never. Passwords and login codes stay with the creator.
