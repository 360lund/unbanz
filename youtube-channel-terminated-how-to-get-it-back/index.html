How to get a terminated YouTube channel back.

The order to work in after a YouTube channel termination, from the email to the appeal form.

Updated 11 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Appeal the termination through the form linked in YouTube’s termination email or from the Channel or account terminations help page. Sign in with the Google Account that owned the channel, explain briefly why the decision is wrong, and submit once. Do not create or use other channels while terminated; YouTube treats that as circumvention. If the appeal is rejected, Unbanz handles YouTube channel terminations for $1,200, paid upfront, with a 100% refund if it fails.

One appeal

YouTube reviews one termination appeal.

No new channels

Using other channels counts as circumvention.

Read the email

It names the policy and the form.

Step by step

  1. Find the termination email and screenshot it, including the policy named.
  2. Sign in to the owning Google Account and open the termination appeal form from the email or the help page below.
  3. Write a short, specific explanation: what the channel is, why you believe the policy was misapplied, and what you will change if anything.
  4. Submit once and wait for the reply by email.
  5. If rejected, decide whether to escalate with a stronger case.

Most common termination reasons

  • Three Community Guidelines strikes within 90 days.
  • Three copyright strikes.
  • A single case of severe abuse, such as spam, scams or harmful content.
  • A channel linked to another terminated channel.
  • A channel taken over by hackers and used for scams.
