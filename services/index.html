Yes. Banned, disabled or suspended, send us the username and a screenshot of the notice and we'll take it from there.

My Instagram account was disabled for no reason. What now?+

It happens a lot, often after a login from a new phone or an automated flag. Message us and we'll take it from there.

How do I get my Instagram account back after a hack?+

Try instagram.com/hacked first. If the email and phone were changed and that doesn't work, we'll help you get back in.

Can you unban a Facebook account?+

Yes. Disabled profiles, restricted Pages and Marketplace bans. Send the notice and we'll handle it.

Will the person know I reported them?+

No. Reports are anonymous and we never share your details.

Can you fix a restricted Meta ad account?+

Yes. Restricted ad accounts and Business Portfolios. Send the account ID and the message you see.
