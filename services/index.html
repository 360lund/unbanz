YOUR QUESTIONS

Unban and ban questions.

Can you unban my Instagram account?+

Yes. Banned, disabled or suspended, send us the username and a screenshot of the notice and we'll get it back.

My Instagram account was disabled for no reason. What now?+

It happens a lot, often after a login from a new phone or an automated flag. Message us and we'll take it from there.

How do I get my Instagram account back after a hack?+

Try instagram.com/hacked first. If the email and phone were changed and that doesn't work, we'll help you get back in.

Can you unban a Facebook account?+

Yes. Disabled profiles, restricted Pages and Marketplace bans. Send the notice and we'll handle it.

Can you ban an Instagram account?+

Yes, if it breaks the rules. Fake profiles, impersonators, scammers and harassers. Send the link and we'll take it down.

Can you take down a fake or impersonation account?+

Yes. Send the fake account and your real one and we'll get it removed.

Will the person know I asked for the ban?+

No. Reports are anonymous and we never share your details.

Can you fix a restricted Meta ad account?+

Yes. Restricted ad accounts and Business Portfolios. Send the account ID and the message you see.

Can you help me claim an Instagram username?+

Sometimes. If the handle is inactive or was taken from you in a hack, send it over and we'll tell you what's possible.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the account. You'll know the price before we start.

Do you need my password?+

No. Never send passwords or login codes to anyone, including us.

Is it guaranteed?+

Yes. If we take it on, we get it done.
