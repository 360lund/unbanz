Your clients do not know Unbanz, and that is how the arrangement is meant to work. Here is how to handle their details sensibly.

How the arrangement is meant to work

Unbanz works for the agency, behind the scenes, under the agency’s brand. The agency keeps the client relationship. Unbanz does not contact the agency’s clients and does not mention them elsewhere. The agency decides what its client is told.

What to share and what to hold back

Usually neededUsually not needed
Username or profile linkThe client’s name or company
Screenshot of the notice and its dateContracts or campaign plans
The email and number on the account, if a check is likelyPayment details of the client
The type of business, when it matters to the noticeOther accounts the client holds

What the platform will still send the client

The platform sends its own emails, codes and identity checks to the account owner. Those come from the platform, not from Unbanz, and no one at Unbanz asks for them. Tell your client to expect them and to complete any identity check themselves. See what your client sees.

Raising an NDA

Some agencies need a non-disclosure agreement for client work. This is a matter to agree between the two companies. Raise it before you send any client detail, and ask Unbanz in writing. This page is not legal advice, and any document should be reviewed by your own lawyer.

Your own side of confidentiality

  • Check your own client contracts for anything about subcontractors, and add a line if needed.
  • Keep notice screenshots in a folder with limited access.
  • Delete material you no longer need when a matter closes.
  • Never post a client’s account problem in a public group.

A plain clause for your terms is in what to put in your client terms.
