The routes, in the order to try them

  1. In-app review or appeal (free). Log in, read the full notice and use the Disagree, Appeal or Request review button once. Add a short, factual note if there is a text box. Guide: how to appeal a suspension.
  2. instagram.com/hacked (free). For accounts where the password or email was changed by someone else. Use a device you have logged in from before. Guide: hacked Instagram account recovery.
  3. Meta Verified support (paid subscription). If you already have an active Meta Verified subscription, Instagram’s Help Center says you can contact support from the app where it is available. Compare it with the other routes on Compare options.
  4. Identity checks (free). If Instagram asks for a code or a video selfie, complete it. Many reviews wait on this step. Guide: video selfie verification.
  5. An independent service such as Unbanz (paid). We read the notice, say whether an appeal looks realistic, prepare it and handle it through Meta’s official process.

Do not repeat the same request

Sending the same appeal again and again does not help. Submit once, keep a screenshot of the confirmation and watch your email and the app for the answer. If the answer is no, read what it says and see what to do after a denied appeal.

What does not work

  • Giving your password or codes to a stranger who messages you first.
  • Sharing your password or login codes with anyone.
  • Buying a replacement account, or an account that someone says is “restored”.
  • Filing false or mass reports against other accounts.

What is the best way to get a banned Instagram account back?+

Try the free in-app review first. If the account was hacked, use instagram.com/hacked. If you subscribe to Meta Verified, contact its support. If those are closed or did not work, an independent service can handle the appeal for you.

An independent service can prepare and handle the appeal, but nobody can guarantee a decision, because Meta makes it. Unbanz has no special access to Meta. Unbanz is independent and not affiliated with Meta. Payment is upfront, with a 100% refund if it fails.

How many times should I appeal?+

Once per notice. Repeated identical requests do not help. Submit, keep the confirmation and wait for the reply.

Can my account get banned again after it’s back?+

It can, if the same thing that caused the first action happens again. When your account is back, we share simple steps to keep it in good standing: turn on two-factor authentication, remove apps you no longer use that are linked to the account, and steer clear of whatever the notice named. Meta makes every decision, so nobody can promise an account is never actioned again, and anyone who does is overselling.

Will sending lots of appeals get my account back faster?+

No. Sending the same appeal over and over can be treated as spam and push your request further back. One complete, accurate appeal through the right official route works better, followed by clear replies whenever Meta asks for more, like an ID or a video selfie. That’s how we handle it, and Meta makes the final decision.
