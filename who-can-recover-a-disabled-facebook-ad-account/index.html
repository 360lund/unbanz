Who can recover a disabled Facebook ad account?

Only Meta can reinstate an ad account, through Account Quality and its review process. What helps is getting that review request right, which you can do yourself or with an independent service like Unbanz.

Talk to us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Short answer

Meta’s chat support often sends you back to Account Quality, and ad agency contacts can’t override a decision. So the request itself has to be solid: right reason, right documents, right asset (ad account, profile or Business Portfolio).

Your options

  • Request review yourself in Account Quality. Free, one shot per restriction in most situations.
  • Meta Business support chat, if it’s available for your account.
  • An independent service that prepares the request and handles it through Meta’s official process.

Check these first

  1. Which asset is restricted: the ad account, your profile, or the whole portfolio.
  2. Whether a payment failed or a card was flagged.
  3. Whether business verification is done with exact legal details.
  4. Whether an admin profile was hacked.

Who to avoid

Anyone claiming to be a Meta employee or “partner rep” who can flip a switch, anyone renting ad accounts, and anyone who wants admin access to your Business Portfolio. That last one is how people get locked out for good.
