How can I tell if my Instagram account is deleted or disabled?

Try logging in: a disabled account shows a message saying it was disabled, a deactivated one lets you back in, and a deleted one says the account doesn’t exist. Only the first kind has an appeal.

Short answer

From the outside, all three show “Sorry, this page isn’t available”. The login screen is where you find out which one you’re looking at.

The three states

  • Disabled by Instagram. You see a message that your account was disabled or suspended, often with a “Disagree” or appeal option. This is the one you can appeal.
  • Deactivated by you. Logging in turns it back on. Nothing is lost.
  • Deleted. If you asked for deletion, you usually get a window to cancel it, then it’s gone. Instagram says the process can take up to 90 days.

How to check properly

  1. Log in from the app, not the web, so you see the full message.
  2. Check the email on the account for notices from Instagram.
  3. Check Account Center on a linked Facebook account if you have one.

If it’s disabled

Don’t make a new account while you appeal, and don’t wait too long. The notice usually gives a deadline. The steps are in Instagram account disabled and suspended, 180 days.
