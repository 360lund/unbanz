Where to report in Canada

  • Fraud and cybercrime: your local police first, then the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and the RCMP’s National Cybercrime Coordination Centre through reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca, or 1-888-495-8501 (weekdays, Eastern time).
  • Money left your account: call your bank or card issuer straight away.
  • Practical guidance: the Government of Canada’s Get Cyber Safe site.
  • Your personal data: you can ask the platform for your information; the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada explains your rights under PIPEDA.
  • A child is being exploited online: Cybertip.ca. Never pay anyone to deal with this.

Checks that matter in Canada

  • Your +1 number: codes go by text. If the SIM was swapped or the number ported, call your carrier first.
  • Business proof: keep your CRA business number, provincial or federal registration and a recent utility bill or bank statement in the business name. The name must match exactly.
  • Bilingual accounts: keep the appeal in one language and match the name on your ID.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is there a Canadian phone number for Instagram or Facebook?+

No. Meta doesn’t run a public phone line for account appeals, and numbers claiming to be Meta support are a common scam. Use the review button, the hacked-account pages or Meta Verified support if you subscribe.

Where do I report a hacked account in Canada?+

If money, extortion or identity theft is involved: your local police and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca or 1-888-495-8501. Then follow the platform’s own recovery steps.

Can I appeal in French?+

Yes. Write the appeal in the language you use on the account and keep the name exactly as it appears on your ID.

How long does it take?+

The platforms don’t publish fixed times. When Unbanz handles a case it usually takes 7–14 days once we start, with a full refund if the account is not reinstated within 14 days.

How do I contact Unbanz?+

Email team@unbanz.com with your username or profile link, what happened and a screenshot of the notice.
