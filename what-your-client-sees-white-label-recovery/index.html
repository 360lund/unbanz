What reaches the client, and what does not

ItemDoes the client see it?
A message from UnbanzNo. Unbanz writes only to the agency.
An Unbanz invoice or receiptNo. Terms and payment are between you and Unbanz. You invoice your client yourself.
The Unbanz name or logoNo. Nothing is branded for the client.
Emails and codes from Instagram, Facebook or SnapchatYes. They go to the email or number on the account.
An ID or selfie video request from the platformYes. Only the owner can complete it.
Your updatesYes. You write them in your own words.

How to prepare your client for platform messages

A client who gets a surprise email from Meta may panic or click the wrong link. Tell them in advance which messages are coming, that they should open them only from the official app or site, and that they should never forward a code to anyone, including you and including us.

A short note you can send

We have started the process for your account. You may get emails or codes from the platform on the account email or phone. They are real and expected. Please do not share any code with anyone. Tell me when one arrives and I will tell you what to do next.

Describing the service in your own words

You can call it your account recovery service, your platform support or whatever matches your own packaging. Stay honest about the two things that never change: a specialist does the platform work behind the scenes, and the platform decides. If a client asks you directly whether someone else is involved, answer truthfully. Using subcontractors is normal business.

What data the client shares, and with whom

The client gives the agency the username, the notice screenshot and their go-ahead. The agency passes only what the appeal needs to Unbanz. Passwords and login codes are never part of that. More on handling client details is in client confidentiality and NDAs.

The only times the client might hear about Unbanz

If you decide to tell them. Otherwise never. Unbanz does not contact your client, does not look for them and does not mention agency clients anywhere else.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Does my client see anything from Unbanz?+

No. Unbanz writes only to the agency, and nothing from Unbanz is sent to the client. The client deals with the agency alone.

Will my client get emails from Meta?+

Possibly. Meta or the platform concerned can send emails, codes or identity checks to the account owner during an appeal. Tell your client to expect them and to complete them personally.

Who invoices the client?+

The agency does. Unbanz agrees terms with the agency only, and what the agency charges its own client is its own decision.

Should I tell my client a specialist is involved?+

That is your decision, but do not deny it if asked directly. Stay honest about the outcome, which the platform decides.

Does Unbanz contact my client directly?+

No. Unbanz works with the agency only and does not contact the agency’s clients.
