No appeal button on Instagram and you live in Europe?

When the button is missing, the app is not the only route. Where you live decides what else is open to you.

Updated 30 October 2026

If Instagram shows no appeal button and you live in the EU, find the decision in your email or Account Status and use the Digital Services Act routes: the internal complaint, then a certified dispute body such as Appeals Centre Europe. In the UK, a data subject access request can show what Meta holds about the account.

Why the button disappears

  • The appeal window has closed.
  • An earlier appeal was already decided.
  • The account is linked to another account that was removed.
  • The app version or device does not show it. Try the latest app, then a browser.

In the EU

  1. Find the decision email or the notice in Account Status. Under the DSA the platform has to give you a statement of reasons.
  2. Use the complaint link in that notice or the help centre form. The DSA says the internal complaint route must stay open for at least six months after the decision.
  3. If the complaint fails, or no route is offered, take the decision to Appeals Centre Europe.
  4. Keep dated screenshots of the screen with no button.

In the UK

The UK is outside the DSA. The main tool is a data subject access request under UK GDPR, which can show what Meta holds about the account. See UK GDPR subject access request. If the account was hacked, report it to Action Fraud in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or to Police Scotland.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Does the DSA force Instagram to restore my account?+

No. A dispute body gives a decision the platform has to engage with in good faith, but it is not a court order.

Can I use Appeals Centre Europe from the UK?+

It handles disputes from people in the EU. If you live in the UK, use the UK routes above.

What if the account was hacked first?+

Say so clearly. Explain when it happened and what changed, and include any police reference number.

Do I need to share my Instagram password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
