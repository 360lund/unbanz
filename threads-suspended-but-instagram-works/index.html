My Threads account is suspended but my Instagram works. Why?

Threads has its own checks, so a Threads suspension can sit on its own while Instagram works. Appeal in the Threads app, and know that a disabled Instagram account takes Threads down with it.

Judged separately

What to do

Open Threads, tap the notice, request a review and check Account Status in Instagram. Stay off Threads until it is sorted.

Careful with the link

If Instagram gets disabled later, Threads goes with it. Do not delete Instagram to reset Threads.

Common reasons

  • Lots of identical replies or repeated links.
  • Reports for harassment or hate speech.
  • Follow and unfollow in bulk, or automation tools.
  • Posting content that breaks the Community Guidelines.

What to do

  1. Open Threads and tap the suspension notice.
  2. Choose to request a review and confirm your details.
  3. Check Account Status in Instagram for anything linked.
  4. Stay off Threads until it’s resolved. Posting on a new profile can look like evasion.
