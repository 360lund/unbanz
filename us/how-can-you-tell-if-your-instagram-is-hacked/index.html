How can you tell if your Instagram is hacked?

By the Unbanz team · updated November 19, 2026

Login alerts, posts you didn’t make, a changed email or phone, unknown devices: how to tell if your Instagram is hacked and what to do in the first hour.

Get help with my case →

Short answer

Check three places. Your email for messages from security@mail.instagram.com about a changed email, phone or password; Accounts Center → Where you’re logged in for devices you don’t recognize; and your profile for posts, stories or DMs you didn’t send. Any of these means act now: change the password and log out other sessions.

Step by step

  1. Open your email and search for messages from security@mail.instagram.com. If your email was changed, that message has a link to undo it.
  2. In Accounts Center, open Where you’re logged in and remove any device or location you don’t know.
  3. Change your password and turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app.
  4. Look at your sent DMs, recent posts and stories, and remove anything you didn’t post. Warn friends if “you” sent crypto or giveaway links.
  5. Check Apps and websites and remove access you didn’t give.
  6. If you can’t log in at all, go straight to instagram.com/hacked.

Good to know in the USA

  • Common US scams: fake “Instagram support” DMs, “vote for me” links from a friend’s hacked account, and crypto investment posts.
  • If the hacker is asking you for money: don’t pay. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov.
  • If your identity is being used: IdentityTheft.gov builds a recovery plan.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Does Instagram tell you when someone logs in?+

Usually. You get a login alert for new devices, and the email on file gets a message when the email, phone or password changes.

Can someone be logged into my Instagram without me knowing?+

Yes. Check Where you’re logged in. Removing a session and changing the password logs them out.

My friends got strange DMs from me. Am I hacked?+

Very likely. Change your password, log out other devices and warn your contacts.

The email on my account changed. Can I undo it?+

Use the link in the message Instagram sent to your old email. If that doesn’t work, go to instagram.com/hacked.

When should I get help?+

If the hacker changed your email and phone and Instagram’s recovery keeps failing. Unbanz handles that through the official process.
