The basics

White label account recovery

A service where an agency presents account recovery as its own while a partner does the platform work behind the scenes.

The long version is on the white label account recovery page, and the process is in how it works.

About your clients

Your clients do not know Unbanz. Unbanz works for your agency and does not contact them. The platform sends its own emails and identity checks to the account owner, and the client should complete those. See what your client sees.

About outcomes

The platform decides every request. Unbanz cannot promise a result, a date or a restored account. Be honest with clients about that. See how to tell a client.

About price and terms

Unbanz agrees its terms with you in writing before work starts. Your own client price is your decision. See pricing models and how to price. The refund policy covers Instagram username claims: a 100% refund if a claim fails.

About safety

Unbanz never asks for passwords or codes, uses only official routes, does not buy or sell accounts and does not use fake or mass reports. See red flags.

Platforms and starting
