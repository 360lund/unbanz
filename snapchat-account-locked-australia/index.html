Temporary or permanent?

Log in at accounts.snapchat.com from a browser. If it shows an Unlock option, the lock is temporary. If it says the account is permanently locked for breaking the Community Guidelines, you need to contact Snapchat Support through help.snapchat.com.

Common causes we see from Australia

  • Unofficial Snapchat versions or apps that save snaps or add features.
  • Adding many people in a short time, for example after sharing your username in groups.
  • Logging in from a new phone or a VPN, or while travelling.
  • Reports from other users.

If someone else got into it

If your email or phone was changed by someone else, use the hacked-account steps, not the unlock flow. See Snapchat account hacked. For extortion or threats, report to ReportCyber and Scamwatch.

What to send

  • Your Snapchat username.
  • A screenshot of the lock or ban message, with the date visible.
  • Which phone number or email is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What phone you use and what happened before it started.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Can I unlock Snapchat by making a new account?

Not while the device is banned. New accounts on the same device can be locked too.
