Agency ad account recovery for Meta.

Agencies get hit twice: a client’s ad account goes down, and your own profile or portfolio can get pulled in with it. This page is for marketing agencies, media buyers and freelancers managing Meta ads for clients.

Updated 29 October 2026

Work out whether the restriction is on the client’s asset or on your agency’s profile or portfolio. Contain it so other clients aren’t affected, then appeal the right asset through Account Quality.

Contain it first

Check your own profile in Account Quality, pause launches on other clients and review partner access before anything else.

Who should appeal

The client’s portfolio admin usually appeals a client account. You appeal for your own profile or portfolio from the right asset.

Patterns that trigger reviews

Many new client accounts in one week, the same card or landing page across unrelated clients, or clients in restricted verticals sharing access.

Contain it first

  1. Check your own profile in Account Quality. If you’re restricted, every client account you touch is at risk.
  2. Pause launches on other clients until you know what happened.
  3. Review partner access. Remove old freelancers and tools you no longer use.
  4. Turn on two-factor for everyone in the agency portfolio.

Who should appeal

  • Client ad account restricted: the client’s portfolio admin usually appeals, with your help.
  • Agency profile restricted: you appeal for your own profile first.
  • Agency portfolio restricted: appeal from the portfolio. Don’t move clients to a new one in a rush.

Agency patterns that trigger reviews

  • Many new client accounts launched in the same week.
  • The same card or landing page template across unrelated clients.
  • A client in a restricted vertical sharing access with others.

Can an agency recover a client’s ad account?+

Yes, with the client’s portfolio admin. The appeal goes through Account Quality on the restricted asset.

Can one client’s restriction affect my other clients?+

It can, if your own profile or agency portfolio gets restricted. Contain it early.

Should we create a new agency Business Portfolio?+

Not as a workaround. Appeal the restricted one first.

Does Unbanz work with agencies?+

Should I open a new ad account while I wait?+
