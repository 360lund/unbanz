This is a guide to Meta ad account problems: disabled accounts, restrictions, payment flags, business portfolio notices and rejected appeals. Choose the page that matches your notice. Each one covers what to check first and how Unbanz prepares an appeal through Meta’s official review. Meta makes the final decision.

Open Account Quality and read the exact wording. Most pages here begin from that message.

Billing, admins and ads are usually the first things to clean up before you ask for a review.

One clear review request beats many quick ones. We can help you write it.

By notice or problem

Pick the message you got, or the closest thing to it.

  • Circumventing systems The notice says your ad account was disabled for circumventing systems, and it rarely says why.
  • Payment issue restriction Meta restricts ad accounts when it cannot collect payment or does not trust a payment method.
  • Disabled after a new card Changing a payment method is one of the moments Meta watches closely.
  • Disabled by mistake Automated systems make errors, and plenty of honest advertisers get flagged.
  • Business portfolio restricted When a business portfolio (the old Business Manager) is restricted, every ad account, Page and pixel inside it can stop working.
  • Cannot add ad accounts You open your business settings and the option to create or add an ad account is greyed out or fails.
  • Unacceptable business practices This notice is about how your business or offer looks to Meta, not a single ad.
  • Identity verification loop Meta asks you to confirm your identity, you upload the ID, and the notice comes back.
  • Repeated ad rejections Every rejected ad is recorded against the account.
  • Personal attributes policy Ads that speak to a person as if you know something private about them are rejected.
  • Special ad categories Meta applies stricter rules to ads about housing, credit, jobs and social issues.
  • Misleading claims Meta restricts accounts when ads promise things the product cannot show.
  • Landing page problem Meta follows your ad to the page it leads to.
  • Spending limit problem A spend limit stops your ads from delivering even though the account looks active.
  • Disabled after a boost A boost is a quick way to run an ad, and it goes through the same checks as a full campaign.
  • Advantage+ campaigns Advantage+ campaigns let Meta pick placements, audiences and sometimes creative.
  • What to write in an appeal Most appeals fail because they are vague, angry or too long.
  • Business Suite restricted Business Suite sits on top of your Page, Instagram account and ad account.
  • Page cannot advertise Your ad account may be fine while the Page is the problem.
  • Pixel restricted Your ads depend on pixel or dataset events.
  • Disabled again It is frustrating to get access back and lose it again.
  • Unpaid balance A disabled account can still carry charges.
  • Lost access after a departure The ad account is yours, but the only admin was the employee, freelancer or agency who set it up.
  • Client ad account disabled When you manage ads for clients, one disabled account can threaten several others.
  • Profile restriction affects ads Ad accounts run through a personal Facebook profile.
  • Disabled after a domain or name change A rebrand or a new website is a normal business event, but it can look unusual to Meta.
  • Disabled after a new location login Logging in from a hotel, a new city or a different computer can look like someone else is using your account.
  • Disabled after adding an admin Giving someone access to your ad account is routine, but the person you added can carry their own history.

By type of business

The same notice means different things to a shop, a coach or a clinic.

  • E-commerce stores Stores depend on ads for daily sales, so a restricted account hurts quickly.
  • Dropshipping stores Dropshipping stores face extra scrutiny for shipping times, product claims and supplier images.
  • Coaches and course creators Coaching and course ads sit close to several sensitive policies.
  • Local service businesses Local businesses often boost a post or run a simple campaign and then find the account locked.
  • SaaS and B2B companies Software and B2B ads are not always flagged for obvious reasons.
  • Freelance media buyers Freelancers carry risk from every client they touch.
  • Real estate agents Housing ads follow tighter rules on wording and targeting.
  • Health and wellness brands Health ads face some of Meta’s strictest checks.

By country

Local registers, tax numbers and rules that matter for an appeal.

  • Ad account help in Ireland Irish advertisers contract with Meta’s European business, and several checks depend on your business details.
  • Ad account help in New Zealand New Zealand advertisers often run lean setups with one owner and one card.
  • Ad account help in Germany German sites must show a legal notice, and Meta’s checks often start with the site.
  • Ad account help in the Netherlands Dutch advertisers usually have a clear business register entry, which helps an appeal when the details match.
  • Ad account help in Singapore Singapore advertisers often run regional campaigns from a local entity.
  • Ad account help in the United Arab Emirates UAE advertisers often operate through free zone or mainland licences, and the licence details matter for appeals.
  • Ad account help in Sweden Swedish businesses usually have a clear organisation number, which helps an appeal when the details match.
  • Ad account help in Denmark Danish businesses are easy to verify through the CVR register, which helps when the details match Meta.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Where do I find the notice about my ad account?+

Open Account Quality in Meta Business Suite or Business Support Home and select the ad account or the business portfolio.

Which page should I start with?+

Start with the one that matches the wording of your notice. If nothing matches, read the page on restricted versus disabled accounts.

Does Unbanz work with Meta?+

No. Unbanz is an independent service. We use the same official review routes that are open to you.
