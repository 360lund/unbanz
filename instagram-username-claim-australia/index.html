Routes that can work from Australia

  • Registered trade mark: an Australian registered trade mark that matches the handle is the strongest route, through Meta’s intellectual property report.
  • Used to be yours: if you lost the handle in a hack or by mistake, recovery usually comes first.
  • Inactive or abandoned account: there is no “inactive” button, so the claim goes through the platform’s own processes.

Checking your Australian trade mark

  • IP Australia keeps the Register of Trade Marks, and you can search it online. Have the trade mark number, the owner and the classes ready.
  • A pending application is weaker than a registered mark, and the mark should be the same as, or very close to, the handle.
  • If your mark is registered in another country, ask us what Meta’s form will need before you rely on it.

What does not give you a right to a handle

  • An ABN or a business name registration. ASIC says registering a business name does not give you exclusive rights to it, and it does not create a trade mark.
  • A domain name on its own.
  • A handle that is an active account run by someone unrelated, such as a person who shares your name.

Many Australian sole traders and small businesses start with a business name and add a trade mark later. If the handle is part of the brand, applying early gives you something to show.

What we never do

  • Buy, sell or take over accounts. The handle goes onto your own profile.
  • File false or mass reports to free up a name.
  • Ask for your password or login codes.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Can I claim an Instagram username from Australia?+

Yes, through Instagram’s official routes: a registered trade mark, impersonation, recovery of a handle that used to be yours, or an abandoned account. Send us the handle and you hear upfront which route fits and whether it looks doable.

Does my ABN or business name give me the handle?+

No. An ABN or a business name registration does not give you a right to an Instagram handle. A registered trade mark that matches the handle is the strongest route.

Where do I find my trade mark number?+

On your IP Australia certificate or in a search of the Register of Trade Marks. Send us the number and we check that it matches the handle.

Can I buy the handle from the current owner?+

No. Buying or selling usernames breaks Instagram’s Terms of Use. The handle goes onto your own profile through official routes. Unbanz does not buy or sell accounts.

What if the username claim fails?+

You get a full 100% refund. A claim fails when the username is not successfully claimed onto your profile.

Who decides whether the claim succeeds?+

Instagram does. We pick the right official route, file it properly, and tell you upfront whether the handle looks doable.
