Can you ban anyone?+

No. Nobody can ban an account just because they want it gone. We help you report accounts that actually break the rules: scammers, impersonators, fake accounts, harassment, stolen content and other clear policy violations. The platform makes the call.

Is using Unbanz against Instagram’s terms?+

No. Reporting accounts that break the rules is exactly what Instagram’s reporting tools are for, and appealing a decision you think is wrong is what the appeal process is for. We use those same official channels. We don’t hack accounts, mass-report or buy and sell accounts.

Can you remove brand impersonators?+

Yes. Send the fake profile, your genuine profile and examples of the copied content. We tell you exactly what to send.

Do you handle Facebook too?+

Yes. We handle Instagram and Facebook profiles, Pages and content.

Is the removal guaranteed?+

We only take on accounts that clearly break platform policy, and we stay on it until the platform acts. If an account isn't breaking the rules, we'll tell you straight and won't take it on.
