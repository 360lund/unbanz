The checklist

  1. Message Unbanz on WhatsApp +1 934 201 2490 or by email to team@unbanz.com and say what your agency does.
  2. Agree the terms in writing before any request is sent.
  3. Decide who in your team may send requests, and who approves.
  4. Ask any NDA question now, not mid-incident. See client confidentiality.
  5. Prepare the intake checklist.
  6. Adopt the status templates.
  7. Decide how you price. See pricing models.
  8. Add a line to your client terms. See client terms.
  9. Run a dry test with a past lockout.

The dry run

Pick a past lockout and fill out the intake checklist as if it were new. Note anything you could not answer. Those are your onboarding gaps, and it is cheaper to find them now.

Preparing your clients

Add an account security step to your client onboarding: admin list, backup email, authenticator app. See the prevention checklist.
