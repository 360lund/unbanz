Meta appeal links for the UAE.

What is specific to the UAE: regulators, reporting lines and rights.

In the UAE, hacked or extorted accounts are reported to Dubai Police through the eCrime Hub (UAE Pass login), to Abu Dhabi Police through Aman on 800 2626, or nationwide through the Ministry of Interior’s eCrimes service; Meta’s own appeal is still the only way to reverse a disable. If the official route fails, Unbanz handles Instagram ($800) and Facebook ($900) cases, paid upfront with a 100% refund if it fails.

The Meta routes

The Meta routes themselves do not change by country: appeal from the notice you see at login (or in Accounts Center), use www.instagram.com/hacked/ or www.facebook.com/hacked for a takeover, and ask for ad account reviews in business.facebook.com/accountquality. The full list is in the Meta appeal links directory. What follows is only what differs here.

The legal background

Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on combating rumours and cybercrimes covers hacking, extortion and impersonation, and Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 is the UAE’s personal data protection law. A police report helps when a hacker used your account for blackmail or fraud.

Common UAE situations

  • Business accounts for Dubai shops and agencies losing the ad account and Page together.
  • Accounts flagged after logins from several countries while travelling; appeal from your usual device.
  • Extortion over private photos: report to the police and do not pay.

Time zone

The UAE is on Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) all year.
