What counts as inactive

No posts, no profile photo, no activity in a long time, or an account that’s clearly abandoned. If it looks dead, send it over and we’ll tell you if it’s doable.

How we claim it

Instagram has no “inactive” button, so every claim goes through the platform’s own processes, matched to the handle: Meta’s intellectual property report if it matches your registered trademark, the impersonation report if the account pretends to be you or your brand, account recovery if the handle used to be yours, and the platform’s own processes for abandoned or squatted handles. We check the handle, tell you which route fits and what it costs, then run the claim and keep you posted by email until it’s on your profile. Nothing is charged for asking.
