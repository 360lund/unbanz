Where app growth meets account risk

  • Meta ad accounts that fund user acquisition.
  • App brand profiles on Instagram and Facebook used for organic and creator work.
  • Business portfolios shared across several apps.
  • Pages tied to app store listings and ad identity.

Ad account problems are covered on white label Facebook ad account recovery and the media buyer guide.

Habits that reduce surprises

  • Check Account Quality on a schedule, not only after a problem.
  • Keep a list of which apps share which business portfolio, so one restriction does not catch you unprepared.
  • Keep creative approvals and claims within the platform’s ad policies.
  • Keep payment and verification details on every ad account current.

These habits do not prevent every restriction, but they shorten the time between a notice and a complete appeal.

Talking to the app client

App founders ask about install volume, not appeals. Lead with the effect: spend paused, plan in motion, next update at a set point. Avoid dates for a decision. The status templates have wording for this.
