Why e-commerce notices are common

Shops sell products, and products are subject to commerce and advertising rules. A notice may point to a product category, a claim in an ad or a mismatch between a store and its ads. The wording of the notice matters, so keep the screenshot. Do not edit ads or products in a rush without reading it, because changes can complicate the appeal. See ad account recovery for how those appeals are structured.

A note for dropshipping agencies

Dropshipping shops often run on thin setups: a new Page, a fresh ad account and quick scaling. That is precisely where restrictions land. Unbanz handles real appeals for real businesses through the official process. It does not buy, sell or rent accounts, does not provide replacement accounts, and does not help to dodge a restriction.

Giving the shop owner a plan B

  • Keep email and SMS lists up to date so a lost social account does not mean a lost audience.
  • Do not move all ad spend to one new account in a rush.
  • Make sure a second admin exists on every business asset.
  • Keep the notice screenshots and the date of each event in one folder.

The handoff

Send the notice, the date, the business name and the website to Unbanz, and keep the shop owner informed with short factual updates. A checklist is in the agency intake checklist.

Can an e-commerce agency resell account recovery?+

Yes. Under white label the agency stays the contact for the shop owner and Unbanz handles the platform work behind the scenes.

Does this work for dropshipping stores?+

Unbanz handles appeals for real businesses through official routes. It does not help dodge restrictions, supply accounts or buy and sell accounts.

Should I edit ads while an appeal is open?+

Read the notice first. Hasty changes can complicate an appeal, so decide with the facts in hand.

What if the ad account is not restored?+

The platform’s decision is final for that request. Discuss plan B with the shop owner, such as other channels and a second, properly set up account.

Does the shop owner see Unbanz?+

No. Unbanz works for your agency and never contacts the shop owner.
