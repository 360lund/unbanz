Can a reputation management firm white-label Instagram username claims?

By the Unbanz team · updated November 20, 2026

When an inactive, squatted or infringing account holds a client’s brand handle: what Instagram allows, the trademark and impersonation routes, and how firms resell username claims under their own brand.

Short answer

Yes. A reputation management firm can offer Instagram username claims to clients under its own brand, with Unbanz doing the claim behind the scenes. Claims work through Instagram’s own policies: trademark reports when an account infringes a registered mark, impersonation reports when it pretends to be the client, and inactive-account routes. The white-label rate is $1,200 per handle, with a 100% refund if the claim fails. Instagram makes the final decision.

The routes that exist

  • Trademark: the client owns a registered mark and the handle is used in a way that confuses people. Uses Instagram’s trademark report form.
  • Impersonation: the account pretends to be the client or their business. Uses the impersonation form.
  • Inactive accounts: handles held by long-inactive accounts. There is no public form; outcomes depend on the account and the claim.
  • Not a route: buying a handle from its owner (against Instagram’s terms) or mass-reporting it.

What to send

  • The handle wanted and the client’s current handle.
  • Trademark registration number and country, if any.
  • Proof the client uses the name: website, other social accounts, press.
  • Screenshots of the account holding the handle.

Why reputation firms use it

A squatted or fake handle can rank for the client’s name, collect DMs meant for the brand and run scams. Claiming it removes a risk and gives the client the clean, matching handle. In white label, the client only ever deals with your firm.

Fees and terms

A white-label rate of $1,200 per handle, including handles held by inactive or banned accounts, with a 100% refund if the claim fails, paid the same business day. Partner pricing for firms with regular claims is agreed in writing after a first test case. Paid upfront by bank transfer, no cards.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Can you claim any handle we want?+

No. Only where Instagram’s policies give a route: trademark, impersonation or inactivity. We tell you honestly which handles are realistic.

Does the client need a registered trademark?+

It helps a lot for the trademark route, but impersonation and inactive-handle claims don’t always need one.

Is buying the handle from the owner allowed?+

No. Instagram’s terms don’t allow buying or selling usernames, and bought handles can be removed.

Will the client’s name appear in the claim?+

The client is the rights holder, so claims are made in their name or their brand’s name. We never contact them directly; your firm stays the contact.

How long do username claims take?+

Many finish within one to two weeks, but it depends on the route and on Instagram.

What if the claim fails?+

100% refund, paid the same business day.
