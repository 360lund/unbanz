Typical local business problems

  • A Page for a salon or restaurant unpublished with no clear reason.
  • An Instagram account hacked and used to message customers.
  • An ad account disabled after a first boosted post.
  • A business listed under a name the platform considers misleading.

The routes are the same as anywhere. Pages are covered on white label Facebook Page recovery.

Explaining it to a small business owner

Plain explanation

Facebook or Instagram switched off your account. There is an official way to ask them to look again. I will prepare and file it with a specialist, and you will get updates from me. I cannot promise the decision, because they make it. Please do not try to log in over and over and do not share any codes.

More wording in the client template page.

What to ask the owner for

  • The business name as it shows on the Page or profile.
  • A screenshot of the notice.
  • The email and phone number used for the account.
  • Proof the business is real, such as a website or registration details, if the platform asks.

Country-specific business details are covered on the white label hub by country.

Pricing for small clients

Small clients are sensitive to cost and to surprises. Be clear up front about what you do and do not promise. A simple incident fee or a small monthly protection add-on works well. See how to price recovery.

Prevention that suits small shops

Add a second admin, use an authenticator app and keep the recovery email in the owner’s name. Offer to do this setup in a short call. The checklist has the steps.

Good to know.

Is white label recovery suited to local agencies?+

Yes. Small business owners call their agency when a Page or profile is disabled, and white label lets the agency respond without building the skill.

What if the owner cannot find the notice?+

Ask for any email from the platform and a screenshot of what they see on login. The date and the wording matter.

Can you recover a Page without admin access?+

It depends on the situation. Tell Unbanz exactly what the owner sees and ask. The platform decides.

Should I tell the owner a specialist is involved?+

That is your decision. If the owner asks directly, answer honestly.

Does Unbanz contact the owner?+

No. Unbanz only works with your agency.
