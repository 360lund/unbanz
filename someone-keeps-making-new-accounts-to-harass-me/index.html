Use the built-in tools

  • When you block an account, choose to also block new accounts they may create.
  • Turn on Hidden Words to filter abusive comments and DMs.
  • Make your account private if you can, and limit who can message or tag you.
  • Use Restrict if you want to avoid a confrontation.

Document everything

  1. Screenshots of every message, comment and profile, with dates.
  2. Each username and link, since they change.
  3. A short timeline: first account, second account, what links them (same bio, photos, phrases).

Report it properly

Report each account as harassment or bullying, and mention in the report that it’s a repeat account. For threats or stalking, also contact the police and keep copies of what you send. See community guidelines strikes for how enforcement works.
