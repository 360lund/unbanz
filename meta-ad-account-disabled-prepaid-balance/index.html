Your Meta ad account is disabled. What happens to the prepaid balance?

Prepaid funds sit on the account while it is disabled. The clock that matters is how long it stays ineligible.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A prepaid balance stays on a disabled ad account while the restriction is open. Meta says that if an account disabled for a policy violation stays ineligible for reinstatement for six months, unused prepaid funds may be forfeited where the law allows. So act early: read the notice, fix the cause and request a review.

Note the date

Write down the day the notice appeared. The six-month window counts from the restriction.

Screenshot the balance

Save the billing page with the remaining prepaid amount and the account ID.

Do not top up

Adding more funds to a disabled account does not help the review.

What Meta says about prepaid funds

In Meta’s Business Help Center page on disabled or restricted ad accounts, Meta explains that an ad account disabled for a policy violation that remains ineligible for reinstatement for six months may lose unused prepaid funds, where the law allows. After that point the account can no longer be reinstated. Meta does not publish more detail than this, so treat the six months as the outer limit, not a target.

What to do in the first week

  1. Open Business Support Home and select the disabled ad account to read the reason given.
  2. Check whether your own profile or the business portfolio is also restricted. If it is, that may be the real blocker.
  3. Fix anything that can be fixed: payment details, a landing page, business verification.
  4. Request a review as an admin, once, with clear and accurate details.
  5. Save every notice, decision and email with its date.

If the review is rejected

A rejection does not always end things. Read the decision for another review option or a support route, and only go again with new information. See what to do after a rejected appeal and what happens to an unpaid balance.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID, if you have one.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date visible.
  • What you were advertising and what changed just before the notice.
  • Who the admins on the account are.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Can I move the prepaid balance to another ad account?+

Meta does not offer a self-serve transfer from a disabled account. Billing questions go through Meta’s own billing support.

Does spending the balance on a new account help?+

No. Running ads from a new account to get around a restriction can be treated as circumventing and make things worse.

Who can request the review?+

Meta says you must be an admin on the ad account to request a review.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
