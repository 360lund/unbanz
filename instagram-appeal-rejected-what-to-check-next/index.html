Checklist after a rejection

  1. Screenshot the rejection screen and the original notice.
  2. Try logging in on your usual phone and on a browser. Note whether any other button appears (it often does not).
  3. Open Accounts Center from any working Meta login and check linked accounts and security.
  4. If Facebook is also disabled, see Facebook and Instagram disabled together.
  5. If the notice ever offered no button, see no appeal button.
  6. In the EU, after Meta’s appeal is finished, look at Appeals Centre Europe.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. There is no “private” or “direct” appeal link. Use the official links on this page, or send us your case.

Avoid these next

  • New accounts on the same phone or email while you wait.
  • Buying a “restore” from someone in your DMs.
  • Long legal letters emailed to Meta. Meta does not process those.

For Instagram, the appeal starts from the notice at login or from Account Status in the app. Meta may ask for a video selfie before it looks at the appeal. A countdown on the notice shows how long the account can still be appealed. If both Instagram and Facebook went down together, see linked accounts disabled together. I emailed my appeal letter to Meta. Did it arrive?

Meta does not process account appeals by email. Paste a short version of your letter into the appeal field shown in the app, Account Status or Business Support Home.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Can I appeal the rejection?+

Instagram usually does not offer a second appeal for the same decision. Check other assets and regional routes instead.

Does Meta Verified reopen a rejected appeal?+

Meta Verified can open a support chat for subscribers. It does not replace the disabled-account decision.

Should I delete the app and reinstall?+

That does not change the decision. Keep screenshots first.
