The in-app appeal

Free and the first thing to try. Log in, follow the on-screen steps and submit once. Great when the notice gives you a clear button and a window to use it.

Meta Verified

A paid Meta subscription for eligible profiles that includes access to account support. Handy if you already have it and can still log in.

Unbanz

Done for you. Send the username and the notice, get your price, say go. Covers unbans, account removals and username claims on Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat, plus Meta ad accounts. Usually 3–14 days from the day we start. How it works.

Which one should I pick?

Try the in-app appeal first if you have one. If it’s gone, didn’t work or you’d rather not spend the time, message us and we’ll tell you if it’s doable and what it costs.
