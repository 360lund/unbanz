Your options

  • Request review in Account Quality. Free. Always try this first.
  • Meta advertiser support or Meta Verified. Free to paid. Good for getting a human reply, but support can’t overrule a decision.
  • Your agency or ad rep, if you have one. Mostly for bigger spenders with an existing relationship.
  • An independent service like Unbanz. Price quoted upfront. Useful for denied reviews, portfolio restrictions and repeat restrictions.

What a good service looks like

  • Never asks for your password or login codes.
  • Works through Meta’s official review, not workarounds.
  • Gives the price before starting.
  • Has a real website, email and contact people can check.
  • Is honest that Meta makes the final decision.

Red flags

  • Selling “aged” or “agency” ad accounts as a fix. That’s buying accounts, and it breaks Meta’s terms.
  • Asking you to log in through their link or share codes.
  • Guaranteeing a result.
  • Telling you to open new accounts to get around a restriction.

What is the best Facebook ad account recovery service?+

There isn’t one official answer. Look for a service that keeps your login private, uses Meta’s official review, quotes the price upfront and is easy to verify. Unbanz is one independent option.

Should I buy a new ad account instead?+

No. Buying accounts breaks Meta’s terms and those accounts often get disabled too.

Can any service promise Meta will restore my ad account?+

Meta makes the final call. Ask any service exactly what they do and what happens if it doesn’t work.

Is Unbanz affiliated with Meta?+

No. Unbanz is an independent service at unbanz.com.

How do I contact Unbanz?+

What if Meta turns the review down?+

Read the reason in Account Quality and do not resend the same request unchanged. Some notices still have a next step. See what to do after a rejected appeal. Meta makes the final decision.

What does Account Quality show?+

It lists the assets Meta has limited, the reason given and whether a review can still be requested. Take a screenshot before you change anything on the account.
