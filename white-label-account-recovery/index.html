What the term means

White label account recovery

A service one company produces and another company sells as its own. Here: Unbanz prepares and files the appeal or username claim, and the agency presents it to its client under the agency’s name.

The word “recovery” covers a few different problems: a disabled or suspended Instagram or Facebook account, a hacked account, a restricted ad account or business portfolio, and a brand handle the client wants claimed. Each has its own official route, and the guides linked below explain them one by one.

When it makes sense for an agency

  • Your clients regularly run social accounts or ads for you, so a lockout hits your own work.
  • You get asked for help with disabled accounts and do not want to learn appeal routes for each platform.
  • You want one clear answer for clients instead of sending them to a stranger on a forum.
  • You would rather keep the client relationship than refer the client away.

If you only see one lockout a year, a referral may be enough. The page on white label, referral and in-house compares the three side by side.

What you can hand over

  • Disabled, suspended or restricted Instagram accounts: white label Instagram recovery.
  • Disabled or locked Facebook profiles and Pages: white label Facebook recovery.
  • Hacked accounts where the email, number or password was changed: white label hacked account help.
  • Restricted or disabled ad accounts: white label ad account recovery.
  • Brand handles a client wants: white label username claims.

Our refund policy covers username claims: 100% refund if a username claim fails. Other terms are agreed with you before work starts.

What it cannot do

Meta, or the platform concerned, makes the final decision on every appeal and claim. Nobody can promise an outcome, and this page does not.

  • It cannot restore an account that the platform has removed for good.
  • It does not use shortcuts or tricks. Unbanz is independent and not affiliated with Meta.
  • It does not take accounts that were really used to scam or abuse people.
  • It never needs a client’s password or login codes.

How to start

Email us with the client’s username, an unedited screenshot of the notice and its date. You do not have to name the client. We tell you honestly if we can help, then agree terms with you before any work begins. The full routine is on how white label account recovery works, and a checklist for the details is on the agency intake checklist.

What changed in 2026

  • Meta says ad account reviews are limited, and once a review is completed the decision is final (Meta Business Help Center). Fix the cause named in Account Quality before you click Request review.
  • Restrictions, reviews and business verification all sit in Business Support Home on a computer. Only an admin of the ad account or Business Portfolio can request a review.
  • Meta has no general public phone line for Business Support. Eligible advertisers get chat support, and Meta Verified for business adds support access for accounts you can still log in to.

Good to know.

What is white label account recovery?+

It is account recovery that an agency sells to its clients under its own brand. Unbanz does the platform work behind the scenes, the client deals only with the agency, and the platform makes the final decision.

Does the client know Unbanz is involved?+

No. Unbanz works for the agency, behind the scenes, under the agency’s brand. The client deals only with the agency. Platform emails and checks such as codes still reach the account owner from the platform itself.

Can Unbanz promise the account will come back?+

No. Meta, or the platform concerned, makes the final decision, and nobody can promise an outcome. Unbanz tells the agency honestly whether it can help before any work starts.

Is this the same as a referral?+

No. In a referral the client is passed to another company and deals with it. In white label the client stays with the agency and never deals with Unbanz.

Email team@unbanz.com with the username, a screenshot of the notice and its date. Terms are agreed with the agency before work starts.
