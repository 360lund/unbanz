Does Unbanz have official authority with Meta?+

No, and we don’t claim to. Unbanz is an independent service, not affiliated with Meta. We prepare your appeal or report properly and handle it through Meta’s official channels, then follow up until there’s an answer.

Why do people warn about unban services?+

Because many offers come from strangers in comments or DMs who ask for a password or crypto and have no website. Unbanz works in the open: one website, one email, written terms and a refund page, card accepted, and your password stays with you.

Can someone who gets my account back also get it banned, or ask for more money later?+

Not with Unbanz. We never have access to your account: we work from the username and a screenshot of the notice, and your password and login codes stay with you. Appeals go through Meta’s own process, so there is nothing we hold over your account afterwards. Unbanz does not add “tool”, “release” or “deposit” fees. If anyone claiming to be Unbanz asks for more, check with us at team@unbanz.com.

Will Unbanz send me an escrow website to pay through?+

No. Unbanz never sends escrow links. Look-alike escrow websites are a known trick in this space, so be careful with any link like that. You pay by bank transfer, and you can reach us at team@unbanz.com.

Does Unbanz accept gift cards?+

No. Unbanz takes payment by bank transfer. Nobody at Unbanz will ask for gift cards, deposits, “release fees” or investments. If someone asking for those says they’re Unbanz, email team@unbanz.com to check.

How do I pay Unbanz?+

By bank transfer. Nothing is charged for asking. Unbanz never asks for your password, login codes, gift cards or deposits. If someone says they’re Unbanz and asks for any other kind of payment, email team@unbanz.com to check.

Can my account get banned again after it’s back?+

It can, if the same thing that caused the first action happens again. When your account is back, we share simple steps to keep it in good standing: turn on two-factor authentication, remove apps you no longer use that are linked to the account, and steer clear of whatever the notice named. Meta makes every decision, so nobody can promise an account is never actioned again, and anyone who does is overselling.

Will sending lots of appeals get my account back faster?+

No. Sending the same appeal over and over can be treated as spam and push your request further back. One complete, accurate appeal through the right official route works better, followed by clear replies whenever Meta asks for more, like an ID or a video selfie. That’s how we handle it, and Meta makes the final decision.
