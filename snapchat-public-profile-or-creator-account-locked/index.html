Snapchat public profile or creator account locked?

Creator accounts follow stricter content rules than private ones. A lock often traces to one post.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A public profile, Spotlight or creator account can be locked or restricted after a post that breaks content rules, repeated reposting, or reports from viewers. Find the post named in the message, remove it if it breaks policy, stop posting for now, and use the support form for your lock type with a short, factual explanation.

Find the post

The message often names the content or policy.

Pause posting

Posting more while locked can make things worse.

Keep a backup

Save your own content outside the app.

What usually sits behind it

  • A post flagged by viewers.
  • Repeated reposting of the same content.
  • Music or media used without rights.
  • Earlier warnings on the account.

What to do

  1. Read the lock message and note the policy.
  2. Find and remove the post that breaks it.
  3. Use the support form with a short explanation.
  4. Send us the message if the lock stays.

What to send

  • Your Snapchat username.
  • A screenshot of the lock or ban message, with the date visible.
  • Which phone number or email is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What phone you use and what happened before it started.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your Snapchat username and a screenshot of the message.
  2. We tell you honestly whether this is something we can help with.
  3. We prepare the appeal and the details to go through Snapchat’s own support channels.
  4. We stay in touch while you wait. Snapchat makes the final decision, and nobody can promise the outcome.

Who this is for

  • Creators with a public profile.
  • Spotlight posters.
  • Brands running Snapchat profiles.

Staying safe

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or claims to have a special way in, walk away. Unbanz never asks for logins and has no special access. We use the same official routes that are open to you.
