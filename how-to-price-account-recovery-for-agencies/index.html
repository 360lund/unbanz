How to price account recovery without promising outcomes.

Updated 4 November 2026

You are selling effort and handling, not a result you do not control. Here is a method that keeps the price honest.

Price account recovery by working out your costs and the effort you put in: intake, coordination, partner terms and client communication. Do not price the outcome, because the platform decides. State clearly what the client pays if the account is not restored, and put it in writing.

A pricing method

  1. List what you do: intake, coordination, updates, handoff, follow-up.
  2. Estimate your own time for each in a normal matter.
  3. Add the partner’s terms.
  4. Add a margin that fits your business.
  5. Decide what happens if the account is not restored and put it in the quote.
  6. Check that nothing in the wording promises a result.

What a quote should say

  • What you will do: start the official process with a specialist and keep the client updated.
  • What you will not do: promise a result or a date.
  • What the client pays in each situation, including if the result is a refusal.
  • That the platform makes the final decision.

Wording for a quote

This quote covers starting the official recovery process, preparing the request through a specialist and keeping you updated. The platform decides the result and the timing, and we cannot promise either. The fee is for the work and does not depend on the decision.

Why not a success fee

A success fee tempts people to overpromise, and it can push towards shortcuts. It also makes the client read the fee as a promise. An effort-based price is easier to explain and keeps everyone honest.

Questions people also ask (2026)

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.
