Facebook codes going to an old number in the Gulf?

People who move to or from the Gulf often change SIMs. When Facebook still sends codes to the old one, here is what is left.

Updated 31 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If Facebook sends login codes to a phone number or email you no longer have, start from the login screen on a device you have used before and choose the option that says you no longer have access. Facebook may ask for ID or let a trusted device approve you. An old home-country SIM can sometimes be reactivated.

Steps

  1. Try logging in from a phone or laptop you have used with the account before, on your usual connection.
  2. On the login screen, choose the option for when you no longer have access to the email or number.
  3. If the old number was from your home country, ask that carrier whether it can be reactivated or moved to an eSIM.
  4. If Facebook asks for ID, use the document whose name matches the profile, and use the same one each time.
  5. Once you are back in, add your current Gulf number and a second email, and save your recovery codes.

Expat details that cause trouble

  • Logins from two countries in the same week can trigger a security check. Try from one place.
  • A profile name in Arabic and an ID in Latin letters. Mention both spellings if asked.
  • An old work email that was closed when you left a job.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Can Facebook send the code to my new number?+

Not until the new number is on the account. You need another way in first, such as a trusted device or an ID check.

Should I make a new account?+

Facebook allows one personal profile per person. A new one can be removed and does not bring back your Pages.

Is there a Facebook office in the Gulf I can visit?+

Not for account recovery. It happens online, through the login screen and the Help Center.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
