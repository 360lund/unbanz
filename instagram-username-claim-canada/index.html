Instagram username claim in Canada.

Your business is incorporated and your brand is in use, but the matching Instagram handle is held by someone else. Here is how a claim works from Canada.

Instagram usernames can’t be bought. From Canada, the routes that can work are Meta’s trademark report for a registered mark, the impersonation report, recovery of a handle that was yours, or the platform’s processes for abandoned accounts. Unbanz files the claim; Instagram decides. If it fails, you get a 100% refund.

Registered trade mark

A registered mark that matches the handle is the strongest route. It goes through Meta’s intellectual property report.

Impersonation

If an account is posing as you or your business, Instagram’s impersonation report applies.

A handle that was yours

If you lost the handle in a hack or by mistake, recovery usually comes first. There is no public form for inactive handles.

Routes that can work from Canada

  • Registered trademark: a Canadian registered trademark that matches the handle is the strongest route, through Meta’s intellectual property report.
  • Impersonation: an account posing as you or your business. Instagram’s impersonation report applies.
  • Used to be yours: if you lost the handle in a hack or by mistake, recovery usually comes first.
  • Inactive or abandoned account: there is no “inactive” button, so the claim goes through the platform’s own processes.

Checking your Canadian trademark

  • The Canadian Intellectual Property Office (CIPO) keeps the Canadian Trademarks Database, which you can search online. Have the application or registration number, the owner and the goods or services ready.
  • Canadian brands are often in English and French. Tell us which wording is registered, because the handle has to match the mark closely.
  • A pending application is weaker than a registration.

What does not give you a right to a handle

  • Incorporation or a business name registration. A corporate name is not a trademark.
  • A domain name on its own.
  • A handle that is an active account run by someone unrelated, such as a person who shares your name.

What we never do

  • Buy, sell or take over accounts. The handle goes onto your own profile.
  • File false or mass reports to free up a name.
  • Ask for your password or login codes.

Refund policy

If a username claim fails, you get a full 100% refund. A claim fails when the username is not successfully claimed onto your profile. The full wording is on our refund policy page, and the price is agreed with you before work starts.
