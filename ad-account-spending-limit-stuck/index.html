Ad account spending limit stuck or lowered?

A spend limit stops your ads from delivering even though the account looks active. This page is for advertisers who cannot raise the limit or saw it drop.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Meta sets ad account spending limits based on payment history and account trust. A limit that drops or will not rise often points to billing or a trust signal. Clear any balance, check the payment method, then request a review. Unbanz can look at whether the limit is part of a restriction.

Two kinds of limit

A limit you set yourself, and a limit Meta applies. Only the second needs a review.

Billing first

Unpaid balances and declined charges keep limits low.

Wait for history

New accounts start with low limits that rise with clean payment history.

Where to look

  • Billing and payments: the account spending limit and the daily limit.
  • Account Quality: whether the limit is linked to a restriction notice.
  • Payment activity: failed or pending charges.
  • Ads Manager notifications for limit messages.

Why limits stay low

New ad accounts begin with small daily limits. Failed payments, many card changes or disputes keep them low. A sudden drop after a policy problem is different and is usually shown in Account Quality. Raising the limit by opening new accounts is not a fix.

Steps

  1. Check whether you set the limit yourself and remove it if so.
  2. Pay any outstanding balance and keep one reliable payment method.
  3. Look at Account Quality for a notice tied to the limit.
  4. Keep running compliant ads at modest spend so the history improves.
  5. If there is a restriction notice, request the review there.

How Unbanz helps

We tell you whether the limit is a normal feature of a young account or part of a restriction, and if it is the second, we prepare the appeal. Related: payment issue restrictions and the guide to declined payments.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Why is my ad account spending limit so low?+

New accounts and accounts with payment issues have lower limits. Meta raises them as clean payment history builds.

Can Unbanz raise my spending limit?+

No. We can only help with restrictions and appeals. Limits depend on Meta’s own checks.

Is a spending limit the same as a restriction?+

No. A restriction is a policy action. A limit can be a normal part of a new account.

Does adding a second card raise the limit?+

Not by itself. A reliable payment history matters more.

Do I need to share my Facebook or Meta login?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from the ad account ID, the portfolio ID and a screenshot of the notice. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
