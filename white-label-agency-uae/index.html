How it works

  1. Your client sends you the notice screenshot, the account link and the date.
  2. You email the case to team@unbanz.com with your own reference; no client name is needed.
  3. We confirm whether we can take it, and you pay upfront for that case.
  4. We work it through official routes and send you unbranded updates to forward.

Details that matter in the UAE

PointWhat to do
Notice languageNotices may appear in Arabic or English. Send the screenshot as it is; no translation is needed.
Phone numbersA UAE mobile written 05x xxx xxxx is entered as +971 5x xxx xxxx, without the leading zero.
Business detailsHave the trade licence (mainland or free zone) and the business name in English exactly as printed on it. See Meta verification with a UAE trade licence.

Partner terms

  • Each case is paid upfront, and each case that fails gets a 100% refund.
  • Partners who send cases regularly or in batches get volume pricing, agreed in writing before the work starts. We do not publish those numbers.
  • A dedicated contact for your agency at team@unbanz.com, with your own case references.
  • Private-label updates: plain reports with no Unbanz name or logo, ready to forward or paste into your own template.
  • Unbanz never contacts your client and never mentions Unbanz to them.

What is never part of it

  • No passwords or login codes, from you or your client.
  • No buying, selling or renting accounts.
  • No fake or mass reporting.
  • No promised outcome: the platform makes the final decision.
