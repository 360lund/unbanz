Instagram suspended your account in Saudi Arabia?

The countdown on the notice is your window to disagree. Use it early, once, with accurate details.

Updated 2 November 2026

Tap the option to disagree on the suspension notice and send one short appeal; you usually have 180 days. If Instagram asks for ID, use a national ID, Iqama or passport with the name you use on the account. If the account was hacked first, report it through the Kollona Amn app.

Disagree early

On the notice, in the first days.

Matching ID

A national ID, Iqama or passport.

Hacked first?

Report it and mention the date.

Steps

  1. Screenshot the notice with the date showing.
  2. Tap disagree and write two or three plain sentences.
  3. Complete any selfie or ID step on your usual phone, with any VPN off.
  4. Log in every day or two to check. Results often show in the app without an email.
  5. Do not open a new account on the same phone while you wait.

Notes for Saudi Arabia

If your profile name is in English and your national ID or Iqama is in Arabic, a passport showing the Latin spelling often matches better. Turn off any VPN before you appeal.

What to send

  • Your profile link or username.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
