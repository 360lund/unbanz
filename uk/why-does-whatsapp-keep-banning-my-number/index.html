Why does WhatsApp keep banning my number again and again?

By the Unbanz team · updated 19 November 2026

Unbanned and then banned again? The cause usually hasn’t gone away. What to change before your next review request, and when a ban becomes final.

Get help with my case →

Short answer

Because whatever triggered the first ban is still happening. The usual causes are an unofficial or modified WhatsApp app, bulk messages to people who haven’t saved your number, being reported by recipients, or being added to spam groups. Remove the cause first, then request a review. Repeated bans make WhatsApp less likely to reverse the next one.

Step by step

  1. Delete modified apps and any auto-reply, broadcast or “WhatsApp marketing” tools linked to the number.
  2. Leave groups you don’t recognise and turn on Who can add me to groups → My contacts.
  3. For a business, use the WhatsApp Business app and only message customers who’ve contacted you or agreed to messages.
  4. Request a review on the ban screen and explain what you’ve changed.
  5. Wait for the in-app decision. One number per request.
  6. If the ban is final, don’t keep re-registering the number on new phones.

Good to know in the UK

  • Small businesses in the UK: under UK GDPR and PECR, marketing messages generally need consent. Messaging cold lists is also what WhatsApp bans for.
  • Second-hand phones: a factory reset removes leftover modified apps.
  • Scams using your number: report to Report Fraud, or Police Scotland on 101.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

How many times can WhatsApp unban a number?+

There’s no published limit, but each ban makes the next review harder.

Is it my phone or my number?+

Bans attach to the phone number. A new phone with the same number is still banned.

Can being added to groups get me banned?+

It can, if those groups are used for spam and people report them. Limit who can add you.

Should I use a new number?+

If the ban is final, that’s the only way to use WhatsApp again. Don’t repeat what caused the ban.

Can Unbanz help?+

We check what’s still triggering the bans and help with the review request. Only WhatsApp can lift a ban.
