YOUR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What is a username claim?+

It's us going after a handle you want on Instagram, Facebook or Snapchat, whether it's sitting on an inactive account or held by someone else.

Can you get any username?+

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Some handles can be claimed and some can't. Send the one you want and we'll tell you straight.

Which platforms do you cover?+

Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat, where the platform allows it. Tell us the platform and the handle.

What should I send?+

The handle you want, your current profile and any brand details. Never send your password or login codes.

Do you buy or sell accounts?+

No. We don't trade accounts and we don't hand over someone else's login. We go after the handle itself.

Is this the same as account recovery?+

No. A username claim is its own service. For a banned or disabled account, pick an unban instead.

How much does a username claim cost?+

It depends on the handle. You'll know the price before we start.

Can Unbanz get me a taken username?+

Often, yes. Inactive or taken handles on Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. We tell you straight if it’s doable before you pay.
