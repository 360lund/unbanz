Facebook disabled your account and you are in Canada?

In Canada the appeal in the notice is the main route. Privacy and fraud bodies sit alongside it, but they do not reverse bans.

Updated 31 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If Facebook disabled your account in Canada, appeal once from the notice and upload ID if asked, with the name on the profile. You can ask Meta for a copy of your personal data under PIPEDA. If the account was hacked or used to scam people, report it to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and keep the reference number.

Steps

  1. Screenshot the notice with the date and any appeal deadline shown.
  2. Appeal from the notice, logged in on the device you normally use.
  3. If asked for ID, upload a clear photo of a government ID where the name matches the profile.
  4. If you manage Pages or ad accounts, ask another admin to check them in Meta Business Suite.
  5. Once you are back in, turn on two-factor authentication and remove devices you do not recognise.

If you run Pages or ads

A disabled profile can cut you off from your Pages and ad accounts. If there is no second admin, say in the appeal that the profile manages a business Page. See profile disabled while managing business Pages.

Privacy and fraud bodies

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada takes complaints if a data request is mishandled. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre and your local police take reports about hacks and scams. None of them can restore an account, but a reference number is useful to mention in an appeal about a hack.

What to send

  • Your Facebook profile link or the name on the profile.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Can I call Facebook?+

Facebook does not offer phone support for disabled accounts. Numbers online that claim to be Facebook support are often scams.

Can I write the appeal in French?+

Yes. Write in English or French, whichever you are more comfortable with. Clear facts matter more than the language.

Will a new profile help?+

Facebook allows one personal profile per person. A new one can be removed and can count against the appeal.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
