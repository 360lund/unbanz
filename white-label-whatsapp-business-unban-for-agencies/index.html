Why business numbers get banned

  • Bulk messages to people who didn’t opt in, or many users blocking and reporting the number.
  • Unofficial apps or “WhatsApp mods” and some automation tools.
  • Sending content or selling items that break WhatsApp’s Business and Commerce policies.
  • A brand-new number sending a lot of messages straight away.

What the client should do first

  1. Open WhatsApp and use the in-app review request on the ban screen, once.
  2. Stop any automation or unofficial app on the number.
  3. Don’t reinstall repeatedly or move the SIM to another phone to get around it.

What to send us

  • The full number with country code and whether it is WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business (app or API).
  • A screenshot of the ban message and the date.
  • How the number is used: support, sales, broadcast lists, tools connected.

Usually 7–14 days from the day we start, with a 100% refund if the account isn’t reinstated within 14 days, paid the same business day. A signed agreement with a named authorised signatory is available before you pay.
