The main differences

  • Respect Network is broader. If you also need content removed on several platforms, it covers more ground.
  • Unbanz is narrower: three Meta services only. If your problem is a disabled Instagram or Facebook account, a Meta ad account or a username, that is all we do.
  • Respect Network describes an official Meta partnership. Unbanz makes no such claim. Meta makes the final decision either way.

When Respect Network may suit you better

If your problem is outside Meta, or you want a service that covers several platforms at once, Respect Network may fit better. Ask them directly.

When Unbanz may suit you better

If your Instagram or Facebook account is suspended or disabled, your Meta ad account is disabled or restricted, or you want a username held by an inactive account, that is the whole of what we do. You talk to us in writing by email.

Try the free official route first

Whoever you choose, start with Meta’s own tools: the notice in the app, Accounts Center and the official appeal links, or Business Support Home for ad accounts. In the EU, including the Netherlands and France, Digital Services Act routes such as Appeals Centre Europe exist after the internal appeal.

Checks before you pay any recovery service

  • It never asks for your password or login codes.
  • It does not claim a private line into Meta.
  • It tells you the terms in writing before work starts.
  • It does not ask for crypto or gift cards.
