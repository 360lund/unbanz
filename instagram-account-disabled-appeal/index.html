Instagram account disabled? Get help with the appeal.

This page is for people whose Instagram account was disabled and who want to appeal it properly through Instagram’s official process, whether it’s your first try or the first appeal already failed.

Log in, tap the disabled notice and choose to appeal. You usually have 180 days. Confirm your details, complete any selfie or ID check, and submit once. If there’s no appeal option or it was denied, it isn’t always over.

The official appeal

Log in, tap Disagree with decision or Request a review, confirm your details, do any selfie or ID check and submit once.

Common reasons

Impersonation reports, account integrity flags such as bots or automation tools, content flagged by mistake, or logins that looked like a hack.

If the appeal fails

A denied appeal is not always permanent. The guides on appeal denied and no appeal button cover what comes next.

The official appeal, step by step

  1. Open Instagram and log in to the disabled account.
  2. Tap “Disagree with decision” or “Request a review”.
  3. Confirm your email or phone, and do the video selfie if asked.
  4. Submit. Then wait. Re-submitting doesn’t speed it up.

Common reasons

  • Being reported for impersonation, even when it’s your own account.
  • Account integrity flags: bots, bought followers, automation tools.
  • Content flagged by mistake.
  • Logins that looked like a hack.

If the appeal fails

A denied appeal doesn’t always mean permanent. See Instagram appeal denied for next steps. No appeal button? Our guide on no appeal button covers it.
