Reputation management firms deal with lost profiles, impostors and unwanted accounts. White label recovery lets such a firm hand the appeal, impersonation report or username claim to Unbanz while the firm stays the only contact. Nothing is promised, because the platform decides every request.

The platform problems on a reputation desk

  • A client profile disabled or restricted right when it matters.
  • A fake account using the client’s name and photos.
  • A hacked account posting things the client never wrote.
  • A brand handle held by someone else.

Each has an official route. Impersonation has its own report form, hacked accounts have the hacked-account tools, and disabled accounts have an appeal. See white label impersonation and fake account removal.

Discretion by design

Reputation work is sensitive. With white label you decide what the client is told, and Unbanz works with you alone. You can send the username and the notice without naming the client or the firm’s other engagements. Read client confidentiality for recovery work for how to handle the details.

Where reputation firms must stay careful

  • Do not file reports against accounts you only dislike. Reports must describe a real breach of the platform’s rules.
  • Do not organise mass reporting. It breaks the rules and can backfire on your client.
  • Do not promise removal of a negative review or critical post. Criticism that follows the rules does not come down.

Unbanz helps with real impersonation and rule-breaking accounts, not with silencing critics.

A workflow that fits a reputation firm

  1. Document the offending or lost account: URL, screenshot, date.
  2. State clearly what rule is broken or what access is lost.
  3. Send the details to Unbanz.
  4. Keep a log of what was filed and when, so the client report is accurate.
  5. Report the platform’s answer in plain words.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What can a reputation management firm hand over?+

Disabled or hacked profiles, impersonation accounts and brand handles. All go through the platform’s official routes.

Can you remove negative posts about my client?+

No. Posts that follow the platform’s rules stay up. Only accounts or content that break the rules can be reported, and the platform decides.

Will the client know a partner is involved?+

No. Unbanz works for your firm behind the scenes and does not contact the client.

Do you use mass reporting?+

No. Mass or fake reports break the rules and are never used. Each request is a single, accurate report or appeal.

Do I need to name the client?+

No. Send the username, a screenshot and the date. A name is not needed.
