Can you buy an Instagram username?

Not safely. Instagram’s terms don’t allow buying, selling or transferring accounts or usernames, and bought accounts are often recovered by the original owner or disabled.

Short answer

You’ll find marketplaces and DMs selling handles. The problem isn’t only the rules. When you buy an account, the seller (or the original owner) can often recover it with the old email or phone, and Instagram can disable accounts that change hands.

What goes wrong when people buy

  • The seller takes payment and disappears, especially with crypto.
  • The original owner recovers the account weeks later.
  • The account gets flagged for a sudden change of location, device and owner.
  • You inherit the old account’s history and strikes.

What to do instead

Get the handle onto your own profile through Instagram’s own processes. Whether that’s possible depends on who holds it: a dead profile, a squatter, or an account using your name or brand. Nobody needs your password for that, and no account changes hands.

How to spot a username seller vs a claim service

  • Seller: gives you login details for an account. Claim: the handle goes onto your profile.
  • Seller: DMs only, no website. Claim service: real site, email, written terms.
  • Seller: asks for your login or crypto only. Unbanz: never asks for logins, and takes card or crypto.
