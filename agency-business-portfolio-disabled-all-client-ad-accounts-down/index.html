Our agency Business Portfolio was disabled and all client ad accounts are down.

One restriction on the agency portfolio can stop every client at once.

Updated 18 November 2026

Email us ↗︎

Request a review of the Business Portfolio in Business Support Home, and check whether an admin’s personal profile is also restricted. Ad accounts owned by clients can often be reconnected to the client’s own portfolio, while accounts owned by your agency portfolio stay blocked until it is reviewed. Do not create new portfolios to get around the restriction. Meta makes the final decision.

What to send us

Email team@unbanz.com with:

  • The account link or username, and which platform it is on.
  • A screenshot of the exact notice, with the date.
  • Whether an appeal was already submitted, and what happened.
  • Who holds admin access: the client, your agency, or both.
  • The Business Portfolio ID or ad account ID, if ads are involved.
  • Never your password or login codes.
