Facebook ad account restricted? Help for US businesses.

A restricted ad account can stop a US shop, clinic or agency cold. Send Unbanz the ad account ID and the notice. You get a price before we start, and your login stays with you. Clients share video reviews of working with us.

Check these first

  1. Open Account Quality in Meta Business Suite and read the reason on the restricted ad account.
  2. Check the payment method. A failed or disputed card charge is a common trigger for US advertisers.
  3. Turn on two-factor for every admin in the Business Portfolio.
  4. Finish business verification if Meta asks for it. Use your legal business name and address exactly as they appear on your EIN letter or state registration.
  5. Hit Request review if the button is there.

Common reasons US ad accounts get restricted

  • Ads in special ad categories (credit, employment, housing, social issues, elections or politics) that weren’t declared.
  • Health, supplement, finance or crypto claims that break Meta’s advertising standards.
  • Payment problems, chargebacks or a sudden jump in spend on a new account.
  • An admin profile that was hacked or flagged.

When to get help

  • The review was denied, or there’s no review button.
  • The whole Business Portfolio is restricted, not just one ad account.
  • It keeps getting restricted again.
