Account security audit as an agency service.

Clients rarely think about account security until something breaks. An audit gives you a natural reason to talk to them first.

Email us ↗︎

An account security audit is a short check of who owns a client’s accounts, who has access, how login is protected and whether recovery details are current. Agencies can sell it as a one-off or a quarterly service. It reduces risk without promising any outcome, and recovery support sits alongside it.

Proactive

Raise the issue before the client has a problem.

Simple scope

Ownership, access, login protection, records.

Honest

It reduces risk. It does not remove it.

What an audit covers

AreaWhat you check
OwnershipWho owns the business portfolio, Pages and ad accounts
AccessAdmins, editors, old agencies and collaborators
Login protectionAuthenticator app, backup codes
Recovery detailsEmail on the client’s domain, current phone number
Account healthAccount Quality notices, payment and verification
RecordsWhere notices and dates are kept

What the client receives

  • A short report listing what is in place and what is missing.
  • A list of fixes, in order.
  • A note on what to do if an account is locked, including who to call.

The working list is the prevention checklist.

How to sell it

Position it as care, not fear. A one-off audit at onboarding and a short review each quarter fit most retainers. Do not use scare tactics and do not claim it will stop a platform decision. See pricing models.

How it leads to recovery support

An audit shows clients where they are exposed and makes the recovery service easier to understand. Clients who know the process before a lockout are calmer during one. See white label account recovery.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What is an account security audit?+

A short check of ownership, access, login protection and recovery details for a client’s accounts.

Does an audit prevent lockouts?+

It reduces the risk and the damage. It cannot stop a platform decision.

Does the audit need access to passwords?+

No. Use the platform’s own settings screens with the client.

How often should I run it?+

At onboarding and once a quarter is a sensible rhythm.

Does Unbanz run the audit?+

No. The audit is your service. Recovery support is where Unbanz comes in.
