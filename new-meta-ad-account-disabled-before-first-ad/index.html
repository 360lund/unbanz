New Meta ad account disabled before your first ad?

New accounts get extra checks. A restriction before any ad runs usually points to the profile, payment or setup.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

A new Meta ad account disabled before any ad ran is usually flagged by account signals, not ad content: a new or restricted personal profile, a card or billing country that does not fit, many accounts created at once, or a past restriction linked to you. Check your profile status first, then request a review.

Check your own profile

If your personal profile is restricted from advertising, every new account inherits it.

Match payment and country

Card, billing country and business address should fit together.

Stop creating accounts

Each new account made after a restriction adds to the problem.

Common triggers

  • A personal profile that is new, has little history or has its own restriction.
  • A card that failed, belongs to someone else or was used on a restricted account.
  • Several ad accounts or portfolios created in a short time.
  • A link to an earlier restricted account through the same page, domain or device.

What to do

  1. Open Business Support Home and check your profile as well as the ad account.
  2. Complete any identity or security step that is shown.
  3. Correct payment and business details so they match.
  4. Request a review once, as an admin, and wait for the decision before doing anything else.

What not to do

Do not open more accounts or ask a friend to run the ads for you. Meta treats that as getting around a restriction. See making a new ad account after a ban and circumventing systems help.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID, if you have one.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date visible.
  • What you were advertising and what changed just before the notice.
  • Who the admins on the account are.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Who this is for

Can a new account be disabled by mistake?+

Yes. Automated checks are strict on new accounts, and some decisions are reversed after a review.

Should I verify my business first?+

Business verification helps with trust. It does not lift a restriction on its own.

Does the card brand matter?+

What matters is that the card works, belongs to you or the business, and fits the billing country.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+
