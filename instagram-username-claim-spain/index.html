An Instagram username claim in Spain goes through Instagram’s own routes. The strongest is a trademark report, backed by a mark registered with the Spanish Patent and Trademark Office (OEPM) or the EUIPO. Without a trademark, a claim depends on the account breaking a rule, such as impersonating you. Unbanz checks the handle first.
