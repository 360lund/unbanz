Meta appeal links for the USA.

The official Instagram, Facebook and ad account routes, and the US reporting bodies that matter when an account is hacked or used for fraud.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

The Meta appeal routes are the same in the USA as everywhere else: appeal a disabled or suspended Instagram or Facebook account from the notice you see when you log in, use instagram.com/hacked or facebook.com/hacked for takeovers, and request ad account reviews in Business Support Home. Meta has no general support phone number. If a hacker spent money or stole your identity, you can also report to the FTC and the FBI’s IC3; those reports do not restore an account but create a record. Meta makes the final decision.

Which link do you need?

  • Disabled or suspended notice: appeal from that screen. See Facebook appeal or Instagram appeal.
  • Hacked: the hacked links below, then the reporting bodies if money or identity is involved.
  • Ad account restricted: ad account links.

State attorneys general

Some people also contact their state attorney general’s consumer office. It does not reopen a Meta decision, but it is a formal record. See FTC and state attorney general complaints.

Official Meta links

Disabled Instagram accounts
help.instagram.com/366993040048856		Meta’s help page for disabled accounts. If your account is disabled you see a message as soon as you log in, and the appeal starts from that message, not from a separate form.
Account Status
help.instagram.com/338481628002750		In the app: Settings, then Account Status. Shows removed posts, features you are blocked from and, where offered, a Request a review button. Check it before anything else if you can still log in.

www.instagram.com/hacked/		For a login that stopped working with no disabled or suspended message, or an email, phone or username that changed without you.

www.facebook.com/help/103873106370583		Meta’s page on suspended and disabled personal profiles. It explains the appeal window and that the appeal starts from the notice you see when you log in.
Hacked Facebook account
www.facebook.com/hacked		For a takeover: password, email, phone or name changed, or posts and messages you did not send.
Find your account
www.facebook.com/login/identify		Password reset by email, phone or username. Start here if you see no disabled or suspended message.
Phone support
www.facebook.com/help/1088016719924954		Meta says it has no general phone line for customer service. Anyone calling or messaging you as “Meta support” is very likely a scammer.
Business Support Home (Account Quality)
business.facebook.com/accountquality		The one place that shows ad account, Page, pixel and Business Portfolio restrictions, with Request review where it is offered.

Where to report in the USA

LinkWhat it is for
ReportFraud.ftc.gov
reportfraud.ftc.gov/		The Federal Trade Commission’s fraud reporting site. Useful when a hacked account was used to scam people or charge your card.
IdentityTheft.gov
www.identitytheft.gov/		For identity theft: a recovery plan and a report you can show your bank.
FBI IC3
www.ic3.gov/		The FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center, for hacking and online fraud with a loss.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone for a “private” or “direct” appeal link. There is none.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is there a Meta support number in the USA?+

No. Meta says it has no general public phone number for customer service. Numbers claiming to be Meta support are likely scams.

Where do I appeal a disabled Instagram or Facebook account?+

From the notice you see when you log in, or from Account Status and Accounts Center.

Will an FTC report get my account back?+

No. It records the fraud. The account itself is decided by Meta through its appeal routes.
