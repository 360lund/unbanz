Facebook Page account down? A guide for car dealerships.

Your bookings, leads and messages run through this account. Here is where to start.

Updated 5 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

For a car dealership, start in Business Support Home to see whether the Page, the ad account or the admin profile is restricted. Vehicle ads with financing terms fall under Meta’s financial products rules. Fix the flagged ad, then request one review. Meta makes the final decision.

Appeal

Once, in plain words.

What commonly triggers it in your industry

  • Financing or credit offers in ads, which fall under Special Ad Categories.
  • Marketplace listings that break Commerce Policies.
  • Several staff logging in to one personal profile.
  • Sudden spend increases on a new ad account.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, with the date.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone who says they have inside access.
