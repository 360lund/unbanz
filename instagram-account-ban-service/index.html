Questions people also ask (2026)

Where is the appeal in Accounts Center?

If you can still log in, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Account status, and pick the account. If you cannot log in, the login screen usually shows the notice with a button to disagree or request a review. Menu names change between app versions, so match the wording on your screen.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.

Can you ban any Instagram account?+

No. Only accounts that actually break Instagram's rules, like impersonation, fake accounts, scams, harassment and stolen content. We report them through Instagram's own tools and Instagram makes the call.

How long does an Instagram takedown take?+

It depends on the violation and evidence.

Will they know I reported them?+

Reports on Instagram are private, so who reported stays with you.

Will the account actually come down?+

We only take on accounts that clearly break platform policy, and we stay on it until the platform acts. If an account isn't breaking the rules, we'll tell you straight and won't take it on.

Can the account come back?+

Sometimes they make a new one. Tell us. We take the new one down too.

Can Unbanz get a violating Instagram account removed?+

Yes, if it’s breaking the rules: fake, impersonation, scam or harassing accounts. Send the profile link and what it’s doing.
