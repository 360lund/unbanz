YOUR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Can you ban any Instagram account?+

No. Only accounts that actually break Instagram's rules, like impersonation, fake accounts, scams, harassment and stolen content. We report them through Instagram's own tools and Instagram makes the call.

How long does an Instagram takedown take?+

It depends on the violation and evidence.

Will they know I reported them?+

Reports on Instagram are private, so who reported stays with you.

Is the removal guaranteed?+

We only take on accounts that clearly break platform policy, and we stay on it until the platform acts. If an account isn't breaking the rules, we'll tell you straight and won't take it on.

Can the account come back?+

Sometimes they make a new one. Tell us. We take the new one down too.

Can Unbanz get a violating Instagram account removed?+

Yes, if it’s breaking the rules: fake, impersonation, scam or harassing accounts. Send the profile link and what it’s doing.

Can I see proof of Instagram takedowns?+

Yes. Real takedown screenshots, names hidden, are on unbanz.com/work/.
