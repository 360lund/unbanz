The first steps for the client

  1. Check the email for messages from the platform about changes to the account and use the official link to undo them.
  2. Do not share login codes with anyone, including anyone claiming to help.
  3. Use a device and network used on the account before.
  4. Note the date, what changed and what the account posted.
  5. Send the details to the agency.

Common signs of a hacked account

  • The email address or phone number on the account has changed.
  • The password no longer works and the reset goes to an unfamiliar address.
  • Posts or messages the client did not write.
  • Unknown devices in the login history.
  • Two-factor authentication changed or removed.

A warning about scam helpers

After an account is hacked, the client may receive messages from people offering to get it back for a fee, often asking for logins or payments by gift card. That is a scam. Warn clients about it. Unbanz never asks for passwords and only uses the platform’s own routes. See red flags for agencies.

After recovery

Change the password, review connected apps, remove unknown devices, set up an authenticator app and update the recovery email. Use the lockout prevention checklist.
