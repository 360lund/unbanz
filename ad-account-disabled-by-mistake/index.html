Ad account disabled by mistake?

Automated systems make errors, and plenty of honest advertisers get flagged. This page is for people who ran clean ads and cannot see what they did wrong.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

If your ad account was disabled and you cannot find a reason, start by confirming what Meta actually flagged in Account Quality. Then request a review with proof that you are a real business running allowed ads. A calm, specific appeal works better than a long complaint.

Confirm the flag

Account Quality shows what Meta flagged. Sometimes it is a single ad, your profile or the portfolio, not the whole business.

Gather proof

Business registration, website, invoices and screenshots of your ads show you are what you say you are.

Keep it short

One review request with clear facts. Long emotional messages and repeated requests do not help.

Signs it may be a wrong flag

  • The ads that ran were ordinary products or services with accurate claims.
  • Nothing changed recently: same card, same admins, same landing page.
  • There was no earlier rejection or warning in Account Quality.
  • The notice is general and does not point to an ad or a policy.

Check before you argue

Even when it feels like a mistake, look for the small things that tip automated checks. A landing page that is slow or broken, a missing privacy page, an ad that mentions a personal attribute, a profile that logged in from a new place, or a sudden jump in daily spend are common. Fix what you find, then say so in the appeal.

What goes into a good appeal

  1. Who you are: the business name, what you sell and where you operate.
  2. What you were advertising, with the ad and the landing page.
  3. What you believe was flagged, and what you checked or changed.
  4. Proof: registration, website, product photos, invoices.
  5. A request for a human to look again.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date it appeared.
  • What was running: the product or offer, the landing page and roughly how much was being spent per day.
  • Anything that changed shortly before: a new card, a new admin, a new domain, a login from another country.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Good to know.

Can Meta disable an ad account by mistake?+

Yes. Automated systems make wrong calls. A review by a person can change the outcome, though Meta makes the final decision.

Why is there no reason in the notice?+

Meta often keeps the wording general. Account Quality and the account history give better clues.

Should I contact Meta support first?+

If the support route is open to you, use it and keep the reply. If it is not, a formal review request in Account Quality is the main route.

How many times should I appeal?+

Once, done properly. Repeating the same request does not add anything and can slow things down.

Do I need to share my Facebook or Meta login?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from the ad account ID, the portfolio ID and a screenshot of the notice. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.
