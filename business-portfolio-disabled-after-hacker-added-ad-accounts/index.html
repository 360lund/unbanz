What to clean up before the review

  • Unknown people and partners under Users.
  • Ad accounts you did not create, and campaigns you did not run.
  • Apps and system users you do not recognise.
  • Payment methods you did not add.
  • Turn on two-factor for every admin.

What to write

  1. One sentence: the portfolio was compromised on [date] and the activity after that was not ours.
  2. What the attacker did: new admin [name], ad accounts [IDs], ads about [topic].
  3. What you did: secured profiles, removed access, blocked the card, reported to [IC3 / Report Fraud / police] with reference [number].
  4. A request for a human review of the portfolio and its ad accounts.

Why the whole portfolio goes down

Meta can restrict a portfolio when its assets, including ad accounts a hacker created inside it, repeatedly break advertising policy. Scam crypto and fake-discount ads are the usual pattern. Because the restriction sits on the portfolio, your own Pages and ad accounts inside it can lose advertising access too, even though they did nothing wrong.
