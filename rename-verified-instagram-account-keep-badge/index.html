Need to rename a verified Instagram account and keep the badge?

A username change can trigger a fresh verification check. Plan it so the name, bio and brand all match before you switch.

Updated 4 November 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

You can change the username on a verified Instagram account, but the change can trigger another check and the badge may be removed if the new name no longer matches the person or brand that was verified. Meta Verified subscribers are told that changing profile name, username or photo can pause the badge until it is checked again. Change one thing at a time and keep proof of the rebrand ready.

One change

Change the username alone, not photo and name too.

Proof ready

Press, trademark or company records for the new name.

Expect a check

The badge can pause while it is reviewed.

Before you switch

  1. Make sure the new name is free, or that it can be claimed. See the eligibility checklist.
  2. Update your website and other profiles to the new name first, so the rebrand is visible.
  3. Keep the profile photo and display name stable on the day you change the username.
  4. Screenshot the profile before and after.

If the badge disappears

For legacy verification, apply again from Settings once the new name is established. For Meta Verified, follow the in-app prompt to confirm your identity. Do not pay anyone who claims they can restore a badge through a contact.

When we help

When the name you want is held by an inactive account, we can help with the claim to your existing verified profile. Terms are agreed with you before any work starts. Instagram makes the final decision.

What to send

  • Your profile link or username.
  • A screenshot of the message or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
