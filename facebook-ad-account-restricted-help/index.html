Facebook ad account restricted? Get help.

Ad account restricted and you can’t run ads? Send Unbanz the ad account ID and the notice. You get a price before we start, and your login stays with you. Clients share video reviews of working with us.

Try this first

  1. Open Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (Business Support Home).
  2. Pick the restricted ad account and read the reason.
  3. Hit Request review if it’s there. You need to be an admin.
  4. Fix anything it flags first: payment method, two-factor, identity checks.

Full walkthrough in our ad account restricted guide.

When to get help

  • The review was denied, or there’s no review button.
  • The whole Business Portfolio is restricted, not just one ad account.
  • It keeps getting restricted again.
  • You’re losing money every day the ads are down.
