What happens to an Instagram username during a 180-day disable?

Updated 6 November 2026

A long disable window makes people worry the handle will slip away.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

While an Instagram account is disabled or suspended, it usually still holds its username. Other people generally cannot take that handle during the window. If the account is permanently removed later, the username can become available on a first-come, first-served basis. Restoring your own account is still the cleanest way to keep the handle.

After removal

The name can free up later.

If it is your account

  • Appeal from the disabled notice. See Instagram suspended appeal.
  • Do not create a second account just to grab the same handle.
  • If the appeal ends and the account is gone, a separate username claim may fit when the handle matches your brand or someone else took it unfairly.

Also: get your username back after a ban and username held by a banned account.

If someone else’s account is in a long disable

You still need a legit route: trademark, impersonation, or waiting until Instagram removes the account. Inactivity or a long disable alone is not a public request form.

Which do you need?

  • Handle on a dead or empty account: see can I claim an inactive Instagram username.
  • Handle that matches your brand: see someone has my brand name on Instagram.
  • Account pretending to be you: see impersonator using my username.
  • Lost your own handle: see after a ban or after deleting.

Before you start

  • Write down the exact handle, with any dots or underscores.
  • Screenshot the account holding it, with the date.
  • Have your own profile link ready.
  • If you rely on a trademark, have the registration number and office.
  • Never share your password or login codes with anyone.

If you are in the EU

Under the EU Digital Services Act, Instagram has to explain moderation decisions and offer an internal complaint route. If Instagram removes or restricts an account in a username dispute, you can also use a certified out-of-court body such as Appeals Centre Europe. This does not create a right to someone else’s username.
