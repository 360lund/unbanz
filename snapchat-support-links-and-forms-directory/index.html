Snapchat support links and forms.

The official Snapchat routes for a locked, banned or hacked account, and the difference between an account lock and a device ban.

Updated 18 November 2026

Get my account back →Prefer email? ↗︎

A locked Snapchat account is checked at accounts.snapchat.com. If the lock is temporary, an Unlock option may appear there after the lock period. Permanent locks and content decisions use Snap’s appeal steps in the Snapchat Support site. Hacked accounts use the compromised-account help page. A device ban is separate from an account lock. Snapchat has no customer service phone number. Snap makes the final decision.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, including the date and any reference number.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, on your normal network, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated or copy-pasted appeals do not help.
  4. Uninstall any third-party Snapchat add-ons or modified apps before you unlock.
  5. There is no “private” or “direct” appeal link. Use the official links on this page, or send us your case.

Snapchat locks and bans are handled through Snapchat Support forms. A temporary lock usually ends on its own; permanent bans and device bans need the support form. All official links: Snapchat support links directory.

Can I unlock Snapchat by making a new account?

Not while the device is banned. New accounts on the same device can be locked too.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Where do I unlock my Snapchat account?+

Log in at accounts.snapchat.com on a browser. If the lock is temporary and the period has passed, Unlock may be offered there.

Can a permanently locked Snapchat account be appealed?+

Snap describes an appeal route in its Support site. Snap makes the final decision.

Is a device ban the same as an account lock?+

No. A device ban is tied to the phone; an account lock is tied to the account.

Does Snapchat have a phone number?+

No. Support is through the app and the Snapchat Support site.
