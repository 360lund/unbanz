YOUR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What if the notice says permanently disabled?+

Send the exact notice. The wording and case history matter. We review the situation before deciding whether to accept the case; the notice alone is not a recovery promise.

Can you help a small account?+

Yes, you can ask about personal accounts as well as creator and business profiles. Follower count alone does not decide whether we can help.

Will I have to share my password?+

Do not send your password, login code or recovery code in an enquiry. We start with the account link and restriction notice.
