YOUR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What if it says permanently disabled?+

Send the exact notice and we'll take it from there.

Can you recover a hacked Instagram account?+

Sometimes, even when the email and phone were changed. Send what you still have access to.

Do you take small accounts?+

Yes. We take personal, creator and business accounts. Follower count doesn't matter.

Will I actually get it back?+

Nobody can promise that. We handle the process end to end; Meta makes the final decision.

Do I need to share my password?+

We start with the account link and the notice. Your password and login codes stay with you.

Can a disabled Instagram account be recovered?+

Often, yes. Send the username and a screenshot of the disabled notice and we tell you if it’s doable upfront.

Can Unbanz recover a banned Instagram account?+

Yes. Banned, disabled, suspended and hacked Instagram accounts. You get a price before we start.

How long does Instagram account recovery take?+

The usual estimate is 3–14 days from the day we start.

Can someone who gets my account back also get it banned, or ask for more money later?+

Not with Unbanz. We never have access to your account: we work from the username and a screenshot of the notice, and your password and login codes stay with you. Appeals go through Meta’s own process, so there is nothing we hold over your account afterwards. The price agreed in your chat or email is the price, with no extra “tool”, “release” or “deposit” fees added later. If anyone claiming to be Unbanz asks for more, check with us at team@unbanz.com or WhatsApp +1 934 201 2490.

Can my account get banned again after it’s back?+

It can, if the same thing that caused the first action happens again. When your account is back, we share simple steps to keep it in good standing: turn on two-factor authentication, remove apps you no longer use that are linked to the account, and steer clear of whatever the notice named. Meta makes every decision, so nobody can promise an account is never actioned again, and anyone who does is overselling.

Will sending lots of appeals get my account back faster?+

No. Sending the same appeal over and over can be treated as spam and push your request further back. One complete, accurate appeal through the right official route works better, followed by clear replies whenever Meta asks for more, like an ID or a video selfie. That’s how we handle it, and Meta makes the final decision.

Do I have to pay Unbanz in crypto?+

No. You can pay by card or by crypto, whichever you prefer. The price and payment method are agreed in your chat or email first, and nothing is charged for asking. Unbanz never asks for your password, login codes, gift cards or deposits. If someone says they’re Unbanz and will only take crypto, email team@unbanz.com to check.
