Meta business verification with a Saudi CR.

The usual failure is the name: Arabic on the CR, English in the form.

Updated 18 November 2026

Get my account back →Prefer email? ↗︎

Upload your Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce and enter the business name exactly as it appears on it; if the CR is in Arabic only, enter the Arabic name or add an official document that shows the English name. Meta compares what you type with the document, so a transliterated or brand name is the most common reason for rejection. Use the registered address and a phone or domain that belongs to the business. If a failed verification has led to a restricted Business Portfolio, Unbanz handles Business Portfolio recovery from $1,000, paid upfront with a 100% refund if it fails.

Common mismatches

ProblemFix
English brand name, Arabic-only CREnter the Arabic name, or add a document with the English name.
Transliteration differs (e.g. Al- vs El-)Copy the spelling on the document.
Branch CR used for main companyUse the CR that matches the legal entity you enter.
Personal phone numberUse a number tied to the business where possible.

What to have ready

  • Commercial Registration certificate from the Ministry of Commerce.
  • A second document if needed, such as a utility bill or a VAT certificate in the business name.
  • A website or business email on a matching domain.
