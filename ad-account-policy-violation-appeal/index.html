Policies that cause most restrictions

  • Unacceptable business practices: offers Meta thinks could mislead people. Our guide goes deeper.
  • Personal attributes: copy like “Are you in debt?” that implies something about the viewer.
  • Health and weight loss claims, including before/after images.
  • Financial and crypto products without the right authorisation.
  • Circumventing systems: cloaking, or links to accounts that were banned.

Before you appeal

  1. Open Account Quality and list every rejected ad.
  2. Delete or edit them. Leaving rejected ads live works against you.
  3. Check the landing page matches the ad. Mismatches are a big trigger.
  4. Then request the review with one or two clear sentences.

What a good appeal says

Who you are, what you sell, what was wrong, and what you changed. That’s it. No threats, no long stories, no screenshots of sales.

Ad account questions people also ask (2026)

What are the usual reasons for an ad account restriction?

The common ones are a declined or disputed payment, a billing country or currency change, a sudden spend increase, repeated rejected ads, a login from a new device or country, unverified business details, or an admin whose personal profile is itself restricted. Account Quality usually names the policy.

Where do I request the review?

Go to Account Quality in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/accountquality), select the ad account, and use Request review if it is shown. For a personal ad account, open Accounts Center, then Account status.

How long does an ad account review take?

Meta does not state one. While you wait, do not open new ad accounts or move spend to a fresh profile, which can be read as circumventing systems. Check Account Quality for status rather than resubmitting.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

How do I appeal an ad account restricted for policy violations?+

In Account Quality, select the ad account and tap Request review. Fix or remove the flagged ads first and explain what changed.

Can a single rejected ad restrict my account?+

Usually it’s a pattern of rejections, but one serious violation can be enough.

Should I delete rejected ads before appealing?+

Yes, or edit them so they comply. Live rejected ads weaken the appeal.

Can Unbanz help with policy violation appeals?+

Yes. Unbanz helps advertisers prepare and handle ad account appeals through Meta’s official review.

How do I reach Unbanz?+

Email team@unbanz.com.

What should I avoid while a review is open?+

Do not send repeated requests, add new cards or admins to the account, or run ads from another account tied to the same business. Pay any unpaid balance, answer any request for documents, and keep screenshots of every notice.

What does Account Quality show?+

It lists the assets Meta has limited, the reason given and whether a review can still be requested. Take a screenshot before you change anything on the account.
