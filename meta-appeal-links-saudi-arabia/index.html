Meta appeal links for Saudi Arabia

The same official links work in Saudi Arabia as everywhere else. This page lists them, with notes for people in the Kingdom.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Updated 5 November 2026

In Saudi Arabia, Instagram and Facebook appeals start from the notice you see when you log in, or from Account Status in the app. Ad accounts and Pages are reviewed in Business Support Home. Meta does not take appeals by email or phone. Use the device you normally use and appeal once. Meta makes the final decision.

Instagram

Notice at login, then Account Status.

Facebook

Notice at login, then Accounts Center.

Ads

Business Support Home on a computer.

Notes for Saudi Arabia

  • Meta’s apps work in Arabic and English. If your appeal screen is in Arabic, you can write the appeal in Arabic or English.
  • Use your normal mobile network. Many people use a VPN for other apps; turn it off before you appeal.
  • If your ID is not in Latin script, upload it as it is. Do not edit or translate the image.

Before you open any link

  1. Screenshot the full notice, with the date.
  2. Use the phone or computer you usually log in from, without a VPN.
  3. Appeal once, in plain words. Repeated appeals do not help.
  4. Do not make a new account while you wait.
  5. Never pay anyone who says they have inside access.
