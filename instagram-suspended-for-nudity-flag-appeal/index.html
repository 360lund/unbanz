Find the flagged post

The notice or Account Status usually names the post or reel. Start there.

Be honest about it

If the post is close to the line, say what it is and why it is there. Do not argue the rules.

Check older posts

Several flags build up. Remove or archive anything borderline before you appeal.

What is often flagged by mistake

  • Classical art, sculpture and museum photos.
  • Swimwear, beachwear and gym content.
  • Breastfeeding, medical and health education posts.
  • Illustrations and cartoons that look like real skin.

These can be restored on review. Content that is plainly sexual will not be.

How to appeal

  1. Open the notice and select the option to request a review.
  2. Explain in one or two sentences what the content is, such as a museum photo or a swimwear product shot.
  3. If other flagged posts exist, remove the ones you cannot defend.
  4. Wait for the answer instead of resubmitting.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

Questions people also ask (2026)

How long does an appeal take?

Meta does not commit to a timeline. Waits vary widely, and identity checks can add to them. The appeal window shown on a disable notice is usually about 180 days, so note the date on yours.

Is there anywhere to go after a final no?

In the EU, Appeals Centre Europe can review Facebook and Instagram account decisions. It is free and non-binding, and Meta’s internal appeal must be finished first. Outside the EU there is no equivalent body for account decisions.

Disabled and no option to send ID?

Meta often asks for a video selfie instead of a document, or shows no upload at all. Record the selfie once, in good light, with no filters or glasses. If no option appears, screenshot the screen with the date. Sending repeated requests does not help.
