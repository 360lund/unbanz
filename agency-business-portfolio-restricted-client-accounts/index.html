Your agency business portfolio is restricted. What about client accounts?

When the agency portfolio goes down, every client connected to it can feel it. Sort the assets before you appeal.

Updated 18 November 2026

Email us ↗︎

If your agency business portfolio is restricted, client ad accounts shared with it may stop delivering even when the client’s own assets are fine. List which assets sit in your portfolio and which belong to clients, appeal the portfolio from an admin profile, and let each client’s own admin handle any client-owned account.

Owned or shared

Client accounts that the client owns and only shares with you usually stay under the client’s control. Accounts created inside your portfolio are yours, and a portfolio restriction reaches them directly. Knowing which is which decides who files what.

Containment for agencies

  1. Check every admin profile in the portfolio for its own restriction.
  2. Pause new client onboarding and new ad accounts until the review is decided.
  3. Remove access for anyone who no longer needs it.
  4. Ask clients to check their own portfolio status.

Working with Unbanz on it

Agencies can hand the portfolio and the affected client accounts to Unbanz under their own brand. Your clients deal only with you. See white label recovery for media buyers and how to tell a client their account is disabled.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the business portfolio ID, if you have one.
  • A screenshot of the notice in Account Quality, with the date visible.
  • What you were advertising and what changed just before the notice.
  • Who the admins on the account are.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.

How it works with Unbanz

  1. Email us with the ad account ID and a screenshot of the notice.
  2. We tell you honestly whether this is something we can help with.
  3. We read the notice with you and prepare the appeal and the supporting details, to go through Meta’s own review.
  4. We stay in touch while you wait. Meta makes the final decision, and nobody can promise the outcome.

Who this is for

  • Marketing and media buying agencies.
  • Freelance media buyers with client accounts.
  • In-house teams that manage several brands.

Staying safe

Nobody needs your password or login codes to help you. If someone in your DMs offers to fix it, asks you to log in through their link, or wants to be paid in gift cards, walk away. Unbanz never asks for logins. We are an independent paid service, not affiliated with Meta: payment is upfront, with a 100% refund if the case fails. Meta makes the final decision.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Will my clients know Unbanz is involved?+

No. In white label work your agency stays the contact, and Unbanz works behind it.

Should clients appeal their own accounts?+

If they own the account and it carries its own restriction, yes. Their admin has to request it.

Can we move client accounts out of our portfolio?+

Client-owned accounts can be unshared. Accounts created in your portfolio cannot simply be moved while it is restricted.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?+

No. Your password and login codes stay with you. Unbanz works from your public username, the notice and the details you send. Anyone who asks for your login is not someone to work with.

How do I contact Unbanz about a Meta ad account problem?+

Email team@unbanz.com. Send your username and a screenshot of the notice, if there is one.
