My personal profile got disabled and I lost every client Page.

Pages and ad accounts are managed through people. When the person is disabled, access goes with it.

Updated 18 November 2026

Email us ↗︎

Appeal your personal profile from its notice, and ask each client to check their own admin access. If a client still has full control of their Page or portfolio, they can keep running and later add you back. If you were the only admin, the assets wait on your profile appeal. Do not open a second profile. Meta makes the final decision.

What to send us

Email team@unbanz.com with:

  • The account link or username, and which platform it is on.
  • A screenshot of the exact notice, with the date.
  • Whether an appeal was already submitted, and what happened.
  • Who holds admin access: the client, your agency, or both.
  • The Business Portfolio ID or ad account ID, if ads are involved.
  • Never your password or login codes.
