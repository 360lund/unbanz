What is not offered

  • Buying or selling usernames or accounts.
  • Paying the current holder or anyone else for a handle.
  • Fake reports or any other way around the rules.
  • Any promise that a specific handle will be obtained.

How agencies use it

Branding studios and social agencies add handle research to a launch package. Check availability early, tell the client truthfully if no route exists and offer a variant that works. See branding and creative studios.

The refund policy

Our refund policy: if a username claim fails, the client is entitled to a 100% refund. Agree with Unbanz how that is handled between you and your client before you quote a price. Read the payment and refund policy.

Intake for a claim

  • The handle wanted and the client’s current handle.
  • Trademark registration details, if any.
  • Whether the wanted handle once belonged to the client.
  • The business name and website.
