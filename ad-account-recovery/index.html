Meta ad account restricted or disabled? Start here.

Restricted or disabled ad account, or a Business Portfolio blocked from advertising? Unbanz prepares your appeal and handles it through Meta’s official review. Meta makes the final decision.

Updated 29 October 2026

Unbanz prepares and handles appeals for restricted and disabled Meta ad accounts and Business Portfolios. Open Account Quality first, see which asset is affected and why, then request a review. If the button is missing or the review was denied, send us the notice and we will tell you honestly whether we can help. Meta makes the final decision.

Find the affected asset

The ad account, the Business Portfolio, a Page or your personal profile. Account Quality shows which one Meta limited and the reason given.

Send the notice, not your login

The ad account ID, the Business Portfolio ID, a screenshot of the notice and a short timeline. Never card details, passwords or login codes.

Scoped to your issue

A rejected ad, a restricted account and a disabled portfolio are different problems. You get the price in the chat before any work starts.

What you can try first

  1. Open Account Quality in Meta Business Suite and screenshot every notice before changing anything.
  2. Work out which asset is affected. The fix differs for an ad account, a portfolio and a profile.
  3. Clear any unpaid balance and check the payment method is yours and current.
  4. Turn on two-factor for every admin and remove people who should not have access.
  5. Request the review once, with a short and accurate note. Repeated requests do not help.

What Unbanz does

We read the notice and the account history, work out the likely cause, prepare a clear appeal and the supporting documents, and handle it through Meta’s official review with you. You stay the owner of the account throughout. We do not ask for passwords, we do not create replacement accounts and we do not use any special access.

What to send

  • The ad account ID and the Business Portfolio ID.
  • A screenshot of the restriction or disabled notice.
  • What was running and what changed before it happened.
  • Whether any admin profile was hacked or restricted.

Who it is for

Advertisers, online shops, coaches, local businesses, SaaS teams, freelance media buyers and agencies handling client accounts. See agency ad account recovery and how pricing works.
