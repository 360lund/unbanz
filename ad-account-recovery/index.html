Tell us whether the restriction is on an ad account, a Business Portfolio or your personal advertising access. Facebook and Instagram ads can be affected by the same Meta advertising restriction.

Send the notice, not your login

Include the ad account ID, business name, a screenshot of the restriction and when ads stopped. Do not send card details, login credentials or access tokens.

Work scoped to your issue

We assess the restriction and help you prepare a clear, accurate appeal. Meta makes the final decision, so recovery is never guaranteed. One rejected ad and a disabled advertising account are different issues, so the quote follows a review of your specific account.
