What to put in your client terms about account recovery.

A few clear lines in your agreement prevent most arguments later. These are plain points to raise with your lawyer.

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Client terms about account recovery should state what you do, that the platform decides the outcome, what the client must do, how information is handled and that a specialist partner may be used. This page lists plain points to discuss with your own lawyer. It is not legal advice.

Scope

What you will and will not do.

No promised outcome

The platform decides.

Client duties

Be reachable, never share codes, complete checks.

Points to cover

  • Scope: you will start the official recovery process and keep the client informed.
  • Outcome: the platform decides, and no result or date is promised.
  • Client duties: provide accurate information, complete identity checks themselves, never share passwords or codes with the agency.
  • Third parties: you may use a specialist partner and share the information needed.
  • Fees: what is charged for the work, and what happens if the account is not restored.
  • Ownership: the account belongs to the client, and you act only with their go-ahead.

Sample wording to adapt

Not legal advice

If an account managed under this agreement is disabled, restricted or locked, the agency will begin the platform’s official recovery process on the client’s behalf and keep the client informed. The platform makes the final decision. The agency does not promise any result or timing. The agency may use specialist partners and share only the information needed. The client will complete any identity checks themselves.

Have a lawyer look at it

Laws on contracts differ by country, and so do rules on data. Use the sample as a conversation starter, and have your own lawyer adapt it. Nothing here is legal advice.

Data points

If you share client details with a partner, say so in your terms or privacy notice and share the minimum needed. See client confidentiality.
