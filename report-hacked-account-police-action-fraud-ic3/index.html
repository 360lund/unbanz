Where should you report a hacked social media account?

A police or fraud report rarely brings the account back, but it matters when money, extortion or identity theft is involved.

Updated 29 October 2026

Message us on WhatsApp ↗︎Prefer email? ↗︎

Report a hacked account to the platform first, through its recovery flow. If money was taken, you are being extorted, or your identity is being used, also report to the national fraud service: Action Fraud in the UK, IC3 and the FTC in the US, the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, ReportCyber in Australia, and the Gardaí in Ireland.

Platform first

Only the platform can restore access.

Report fraud

When money or threats are involved.

Banks and insurers may ask for it.

Where to report

  • United States: the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (ic3.gov), and the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. If your identity is used, IdentityTheft.gov.
  • United Kingdom: Action Fraud (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) or Police Scotland on 101.
  • Canada: the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, and your local police.
  • Australia: ReportCyber through cyber.gov.au, and Scamwatch for scams.
  • Ireland: your local Garda station.
  • EU countries: the national police cybercrime unit.

What a report does and does not do

Fraud reports help the authorities link crimes and may be needed for a bank dispute or an insurance claim. They do not usually make the platform restore your account faster. Keep the reference number and mention it if you write to the platform or your bank.

What to include

  • The account username and platform.
  • The date and time you lost access.
  • Screenshots of any threats or ransom demands.
  • Any payments made, with dates and amounts.

What to send

  • Your Instagram username (the public @handle).
  • A screenshot of the notice or error, with the date visible.
  • Which email address or phone number is on the account (just which one, never a code).
  • What happened in the days before it started, in a few lines.

Please do not send passwords, login codes, card numbers or access tokens.
