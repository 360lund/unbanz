Instagram says “we restrict certain activity”. Is that a ban?

No, it’s a temporary limit, not a ban. Stop the action it blocked (following, liking, commenting, DMing in bulk), give it time, and don’t use automation. Repeated limits can lead to a suspension.

What to do

Stop the blocked action for a day or two, use the Tell us option once, remove third-party apps and log out of other devices.

When it becomes a suspension

If you push through the limit or keep repeating, you can end up suspended or disabled, and the way back is an appeal.

What usually triggers it

  • Following or unfollowing lots of accounts quickly.
  • Mass liking, commenting or DMing, especially with repeat text.
  • Posting the same link or caption again and again.
  • Using automation tools, scheduling or growth apps that log in for you.
  • A new account suddenly doing a lot.

What to do

  1. Stop the blocked action completely for a day or two.
  2. If there’s a “Tell us” or “Let us know” option on the message, use it once.
  3. Remove any third-party apps with access to the account.
  4. Log out of other devices and change your password.
  5. Return slowly, as a normal user would.

When it becomes a suspension

If you push through the limit or keep repeating, you can end up with a suspension or disabled account. At that point the way back is an appeal. See Instagram account locked and disabled account appeal.
