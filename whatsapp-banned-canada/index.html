Official steps that come first

  1. Open WhatsApp on the banned number. The screen says the number can’t use WhatsApp and, if a review is possible, shows Request review.
  2. Tap Request review and give a short, honest explanation. WhatsApp reviews one phone number per request.
  3. Wait for the in-app notification. WhatsApp says most reviews are done within 24 hours.
  4. If there is no Request review option, WhatsApp treats the ban as final.
  5. Contacting WhatsApp through other channels or other people’s accounts does not speed it up.

Checks that matter in Canada

  • Recycled +1 numbers: Canadian carriers reassign numbers too. If the number is new to you, say so in the review request.
  • Business numbers: WhatsApp Business app bans are reviewed in the app; WhatsApp Business Platform (API) numbers in Meta Business Suite.
  • English or French: write the request in the language you use.
  • Scams through your number: local police and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca or 1-888-495-8501.

Banned, restricted or reviewed?

  • Banned: “This phone number can’t use WhatsApp”. Request a review if offered.
  • Temporarily restricted: you can still chat with existing contacts but can’t start new chats for a while, usually after messaging lots of people who don’t know you. It ends by itself.
  • Account under review: wait for the in-app decision before trying anything else.

How do I get my WhatsApp number unbanned?+
